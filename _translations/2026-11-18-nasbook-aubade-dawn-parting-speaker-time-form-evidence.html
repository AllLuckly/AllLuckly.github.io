Dawn in a poem still needs a relation to the ending night, parting or longing. Read an original aubade card in Book Isle, separating supplied context, textual time cues and interpretive effects. Genre is not automatic keyword classification, nor is the poetic I the real author’s biography.

Preserve four authorial lines and a separate context note

Externally keep four A lines plus a separate supplied context: two lovers must part at dawn after a night together. This background is not a fifth line or a fact derived automatically from one pronoun. Keep B separately, export fixed-line PDF or labelled EPUB and retain source. Verify L1–L4 and distinct context/body areas, then bookmark A. Wrapping does not change four authorial lines. A filename containing aubade does not replace evidence, and an invented later reunion is not source text.

A context: two lovers must part at dawn after a night together.
L1: The first tram rings below your windowpane.
L2: I fold the note and leave before the rain.
L3: You wake; the milk-white east divides the sky.
L4: We count the bells instead of saying why.

B: morning-only control
L1: At dawn the tram begins its rounds.
L2: I check the clock and count the stops.

Retain often rather than imposing an absolute template

The Academy describes an aubade as a dawn song greeting morning while lamenting night’s end, often involving lovers parting. A common situation does not require the same lovers, line count or degree of grief in every example. Check A’s dawn-parting evidence, not mere mention of the Sun or a requirement for tears. List dawn, ending night and parting/longing as questions with source/context evidence. Do not silently replace often, possible and required with each other.

Read temporal cues alongside the city sounds

The supplied context states dawn; first tram in L1 and milk-white east in L3 add urban sound and brightening imagery. Real first-tram schedules need not equal sunrise, so that phrase alone determines no solar altitude or precise time. Context and verse work together here. Before the rain places departure ahead of expected rain, not already falling rain. Separate explicit time, relative ordering and associations rather than invent season, latitude, city or measured weather from eastern light.

Trace I, you and we without identifying the real author

Your in L1 and You in L3 address or evoke another; I in L2 folds the note and leaves. In the supplied two-person setting, We in L4 includes both in counting bells. Their relationship comes from context and the whole reading, not every poetic you automatically being a lover. Label speaker/addressee rather than substitute Bison’s real travel. Names, genders and relationship duration are absent; windowpane or note does not supply them. Keep roles, grammatical subjects and real people distinct.

Use departure and withheld explanation for proposed effects

Fold the note, leave and instead of saying why place action around dawn parting. Proposed tones include reluctance, restraint or avoiding a stated reason, each supported by wording. Permanent departure, breakup, death and the note’s contents are unstated; a traditional sorrowful association cannot supply a tragic ending. Counting bells shifts attention to audible repetition without proving emotionlessness. Context supplies the situation while verse leaves interpretive room. Distinguish supported tone from unproved plot facts.

Record form separately from the situation

Windowpane/rain and sky/why give AABB in common pronunciation, but four lines and that scheme are this original card’s choices, not requirements for every aubade. Removing rhyme does not automatically remove dawn parting; retaining rhyme in a plain weather account does not retain A’s situation. B offers morning tram/clock/stops without A’s lovers, ending-night or parting connection. It does not establish the branch tested here, without ruling out all broader dawn-song uses. Give form and situation separate evidence.

Troubleshoot the source of context and unproved details

For first automatically meaning dawn, inspect supplied timing. For We identified as the author and a real partner, return to roles. Find evidence before treating rain already falling or a farewell note as facts; otherwise mark unproved. Losing instead of in conversion changes the relation and requires source checking before old interpretations. Correct notes while retaining background/verse identities.
