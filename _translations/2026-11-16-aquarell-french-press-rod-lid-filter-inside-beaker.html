A French press rod connects its top knob to a filter inside the beaker rather than stopping on the glass surface. Draw an invented empty vessel with its filter midway down, checking alignment and what is visible through glass. There is no hot water or coffee and no physical pressing task. Proportions are drawing choices, not brewing or assembly instructions.

Establish one complete beaker volume

Create an approximately 1500×1800 px portrait canvas, beaker slightly left and handle space right. Sketch the top ellipse, side boundaries and shallow bottom arc in one consistent view rather than looking upward at the rim and downward at the base. A pale blue-grey ground will reveal the internal rod through glass. Keep the beaker empty instead of adding a liquid line suggesting another state. Pixels, height and diameter do not establish capacity. Leave room for knob and complete handle.

Seat the lid on the rim

Add a low lid seated on the rim, with its centre near the beaker axis and two boundaries indicating modest thickness. A small pouring opening belongs to one rim side and the handle to the other, both on the same vessel. Avoid a deep bowl-like lid or concealment of the rod passage. Bring a floating overwide lid back over its support. This is an assembled view, without another detached lid; any component study belongs on a hidden checking layer.

Keep knob and rod on one axis

Place one knob above the lid and a narrow rod down through its centre into the vessel. Exposed sections share one direction, with a short section hidden by the opaque lid instead of pasted across it. Use an invented rounded knob without branding. Realign a displaced rod section rather than distort the beaker. Keep the rod inside the opening, not through a sidewall. Visible glass does not require transparency through the lid or base supports.

Connect the filter inside the middle of the beaker

Draw the filter ellipse midway inside, connecting its centre to the rod end. Keep the rim near the internal wall with enough definition to read it, not projecting outside like a saucer. A thin border and sparse crossed lines suggest mesh without reproducing real pores. Its ellipse may tilt on screen but belongs to parallel beaker cross-sections. Midway placement is the chosen static state, not evidence of an actual intermediate stop setting.

Give the external handle attachments and an opening

Curve a handle outside the right wall, connecting its upper and lower roots to the external support rather than the internal filter. Preserve ground between handle and glass instead of filling the opening with glare. If the invented support uses two narrow bands, wrap them outside the beaker rather than through the rod. This frame is one chosen simplification, not the only French-press design. Warm dark grey separates handle from cool glass without replacing attachment boundaries.

Keep glass highlights from breaking internal relations

Retain much of the ground through the glass, with narrow bright edges and a cooler grey base. Subdue the internal rod and filter slightly relative to exterior metal. Avoid an opaque white fill containing heavy black drawings. A near highlight may weaken a local internal line, but should not erase the central filter connection. Check glass and internal layers separately, then together. This manually suggests transparency without calculating refraction or certifying real optical behaviour from an apparently straight rod.

Trace external and internal paths for floating parts

Trace knob–rod–filter and, separately, upper handle root–external frame–lower root. Do not cross-connect them. For a filter mistaken for the base, retain vessel volume below it; for one mistaken for a liquid surface, clarify rim and mesh rather than add coffee colour. A second rod may be an unhidden guide axis. Repair ownership before glass brightness and preserve required internal parts. Keep hidden relations traceable in the sketch while the final view shows only visible portions.

Reopen and check empty volume around the filter

Save native work, hide axes and export PNG. Read vessel, lid and handle small, then trace rod and filter centre enlarged. Keep empty volume above and below the filter and opaque lid/base supports. Restore ground in a sticker-like handle hole or lighten overly strong internal lines separately. Reopen editable glass, highlights and internals. Record no physical pressing, brewing or sealing test. Completion concerns alignment, attachment and cavity relations, not heat resistance, capacity or assembly safety.
