Old recordings or converted media can show comb-like horizontal lines around moving edges. Seeing them does not establish scan structure or an available deinterlace switch in iCouldPlay. Record the phenomenon, then compare conditions with the same file without modifying originals or promising one-click repair. Use an existing permitted short sample. Material without the phenomenon remains a useful control; do not manufacture damaged files to complete the task.

Fix a permitted clip and location

Choose a permitted recording interval with slow movement, clear edges and no strong flashes. Record identity, duration, source and conversion history without inferring interlace from age or extension. Identify a nearby static control and the exact target edge. A small redistributed movie is not the capture original and cannot establish original format. Preserve originals and keep screenshots/comparisons authorized rather than downloading unknown codec test collections.

Describe where the horizontal lines appear

Watch at normal speed and note whether lines cover the whole frame, moving silhouettes only or an already striped object. Real background texture is not automatically a coding fault, and filmed-screen interference may have another origin. Describe direction, movement relationship and time rather than calling every line combing. Mark non-repeatable observations occasional instead of certain. Make the target locatable through words before comparing routes.

Record paused and continuous appearances separately

Pause near the interval, then play through from several seconds earlier. Record actual landing points without claiming an exact field or frame. Retain both results whether lines are clearer paused or moving. Do not represent the entire viewing experience through the worst still. Screenshots can themselves be scaled or compressed, so they describe shapes without proving which interlace-processing algorithm a player used.

Repeat through a known original-file route

Compare the same interval and similar size through another trusted working route for the original file. A re-encoded version must be labeled separately. Similar lines on both routes support investigating material or shared conditions but do not prove damage. One-route differences remain conditional. Record settings and scale; invisibility in a smaller window does not establish removal of the underlying pattern.

Use interlace concepts without diagnosing the file

Interlaced structure and deinterlacing concern media processing. The FFmpeg deinterlacing-filter reference provides context for moving-edge effects, not a command to run or a claim that iCouldPlay exposes that filter. Reliable media information or production records are needed to establish interlacing; a photograph of lines is insufficient. Mark unavailable information unverified rather than importing another tool’s menu capabilities into this player.

Hold other conditions when comparing engines

Where engines are offered, save the first result and compare the same file, speed and similar size. Hold captions, scale and source fixed. If motion appears smoother, also inspect text and static edges rather than declaring lossless improvement from motion alone. Without another route, retain the minimal observation instead of requiring hardware upgrades or purchases. Restore the original route and retain sample-specific limits rather than prescribing a universal setting for old recordings.

Prepare evidence useful for support

Keep clip identifier, interval, moving/static condition, viewing size, app version and engine changes. Identify the trusted source of any media information without exposing private paths or restricted links. Use a permitted reproducible excerpt where possible; otherwise describe the phenomenon without copying the work. Separate observed lines from unverified scan format and update when evidence arrives. Avoid a report title asserting algorithm failure before format and implementation checks exist.

Finish with limited conclusions and preserve media

State repeatable conditions, where lines weaken and what remains unexplained. A line-free ordinary control does not certify another file. Restore playback/display settings, preserve the recording and name conversions separately. If a professionally processed copy becomes available, recheck movement, text and duration before using it. A softer-looking picture does not establish recovery of all original detail. The useful result is reproducible observation with the original intact.
