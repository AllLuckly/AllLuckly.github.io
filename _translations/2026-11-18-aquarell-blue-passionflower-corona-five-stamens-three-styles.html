Draw a blue passionflower tilted slightly toward the viewer, with spread outer segments, a ring of corona filaments and central support carrying distinct organs. Establish layers and counts before purple-blue bands and fine lines. This original study uses a common groundplan, not colour-based species identification or a rule covering every variation in the genus.

Reserve three zones for outer segments, corona and centre

On an approximately 1700×1500 px canvas put the centre near (850,700), sketching an outer ellipse and a smaller corona guide. Shorten the far side in this slightly oblique view and expose a little central support above it. Separate sketch, sepals/petals, corona and central organs, then save native work. The guides are compositional, not laws requiring perfect circles. Leave space for near outer segments rather than omit some to fit an off-centre flower. Attach the stalk behind the flower base, not at a petal tip.

Alternate five sepals with five petals

Place five sepal guides S1–S5, then five petals P1–P5 between them, making ten outer segments rather than ten labelled petals. Related white/pink tones can be distinguished through subtle colour and root occlusion, not a different bright colour per segment. Both sets connect to the base instead of floating around the corona as separate stickers. Keep the count ledger despite unequal near/far projections; inspect concealed roots in the sketch without transparent final contours. Fade labels after checking rather than hide omissions with texture.

Build a radiating corona without calling it a stamen ring

Radiate corona filaments around the smaller ellipse, establishing four directional groups before filling between them rather than drawing equal sun rays. Near filaments can be longer and far ones shorter/denser, rooted around one centre. Use a dark-purple base, pale middle and bluish outer segment as an illustrative palette; bands follow each radial ring instead of three straight canvas stripes. Selected readable strokes supply no fixed biological total. Corona filaments are distinct from the five stamens; not every blue line counts as one.

Support five anthers on distinct stamens

Clarify the central support, extending five short filaments with a broader anther at each end. Use yellow-green masses rather than five long needles inserted into outer segments. Label each filament/anther pair in the checking sketch and trace its root enlarged, avoiding a decorative sixth highlight mistaken for an anther. Raised central organs conceal some corona filaments, so stop those lines behind the support. This common five-stamen layout is checked manually, not identified automatically by the App.

Connect three styles to one ovary and retain their stigmas

Indicate one ovary above the stamens, then connect three styles from its upper region, each ending in a rounded stigma. Their directions may differ, but they are neither three disconnected ovaries nor growths from anther tips. Trace all three in one checking colour before shading. The genus includes different style counts; this selected common three-style groundplan does not identify every species. Keep stigma masses subordinate to the centre instead of enlarging them into mushrooms concealing all five anthers.

Establish height through occlusion instead of black rings

Keep outer planes soft, corona lines broken and central-organ edges a little firmer to establish height. Light from upper left with short contact shadows beneath anthers and stigmas, not a black ring welding them into one gear. The darker stalk still reaches the base; dense background leaves are unnecessary. With labels hidden and view reduced, read spread outer segments and raised centre before individual filaments. If only purple fuzz remains, subdue far corona contrast and restore organ masses before adding white dots.

Troubleshoot ten segments, five pairs and three style paths

Hide tiny filament highlights and identify five sepals/five petals, five filament/anther pairs and three styles with stigmas. For nine visible outer segments revisit count guides and occlusion instead of pasting one into empty space. Exclude corona strokes from stamen counts before correcting them. Restore ovary attachment before styling a broken style. Preserve the earlier copy and change one category, checking enlarged and fit-to-window views. This ledger reviews the chosen illustration, not every genus-level variant or microscopic developmental stage.

Reopen to ensure filaments do not obscure organ identity

Save native work, hide S/P labels and guide ellipses, then reopen a PNG. Read outer spread and central height at fit-to-window size; enlarge three stigmas, five anthers and corona bands for mistaken identities. Reopen separate filament and central-organ layers so a missing-segment repair does not erase the centre. Document common blue-passionflower groundplan, illustrative stroke counts, projection and omissions, leaving unperformed export/reopening pending.
