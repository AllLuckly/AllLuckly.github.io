More labels may not help decide what to read today. Review around ten unread books and assign each a concrete next action. This is a personal method without automatic recommendations or value prediction, and it does not create tidiness by deleting originals.

Choose a limited scope and present goal

Select roughly ten unread or stalled books and one near-term purpose. Record edition, source, notes and actual progress, retaining originals. Distinguish unavailable remote files from unsuitable content. Use the goal as a temporary selection basis rather than a permanent judgement of every book.

Inspect a small amount of actual content

Read contents, introduction and a representative passage to assess scope, difficulty and fit. Distinguish missing prerequisites or personal taste from poor quality. Record one specific reason per book and describe the limited sample honestly. Keep an undecided state where evidence is insufficient.

Separate interest from active reading

Define interest, active reading and deferral as distinct personal states, using actual supported statuses or tags rather than requiring identically named controls. Avoid marking every favourite as active. Verify filtering after changes and keep classifications focused. State changes do not alter book content or perform future reading.

Give each retained book a concrete next action

Record a chapter or question for the next session and a reconsideration condition for deferred books. These notes are not automatic reminders. Keep long plans in a personal list and concise shelf labels, avoiding title misuse. Focus on actionable entries for one or two current books without forcing deadlines or deletion for the rest.

Preserve important notes before reorganising scope

Inspect notes/bookmarks in deferred books and export important material when needed. After changing classifications, verify retrieval from the full shelf and distinguish filtering from removal. Any later deletion requires explicit scope checks and independent preservation. The goal is fewer immediate choices, not artificially smaller counts.

Test whether the reduced list supports starting today

Open one retained book at its recorded next action and read briefly. If choice remains difficult, narrow the active list further for distinct purposes. The count is a method, not an app limit. Verify actual file availability and judge success by easier starting rather than a visually shorter shelf.

Allow decisions to change at the next review

Revisit deferral reasons after actual reading and allow changed goals or prerequisites to change decisions. Statistics do not determine value or require finishing every book. Clarify vague reasons behind repeated list changes. Adjust a few entries at a time and retain short prior notes, keeping the active list about current actions.

Check filters before assuming a book is lost

Clear search, tag and status filters before reimporting a missing-looking book. Verify edition and correct inaccurate progress honestly. Trace unavailable files to their source rather than confusing classification with migration. Confirm actionable selections, meaningful deferrals, readable notes and clear original locations; zero unread books is not the objective.
