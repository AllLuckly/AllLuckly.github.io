Prepare a recording long enough

Choose owned audio you are authorized to play, clearly longer than the timer interval you plan to observe, and check that the beginning and middle play first. If the timer offers only longer durations, choose a recording longer than the available setting, or record an unmet condition; do not use unauthorized audio to fill the time. Place the device safely and use comfortable volume. Do not test while driving or where environmental warnings must be heard.

Record original device state

Record 调调 version, current playback mode, audio identity, output destination and lock state so you can restore them. Apple describes Auto-Lock as a system display setting. Whether a dim display remains visible after locking depends on device and other conditions; screen dimming, a locked device and stopped playback are separate observations. This guide does not require changing Auto-Lock, disabling a passcode or altering security settings; retain the original system configuration.

Set up the visible-screen comparison

Open identifiable audio in 调调 and confirm actual sound matches the item. In the sleep-timer entry actually visible, choose a time offered by the device. Record the displayed duration, start time and sound state; if timer activation cannot be identified, mark the round incomplete. Keep the device in an observable state where permitted without changing security settings. If it naturally locks and the round no longer meets this condition, record that and mark the round invalid rather than forcing a lock-policy change. If your device permits safe touches on a non-control area to keep the display visible, record each interaction and result; do not touch playback, timer or queue controls. If no safe area is identifiable, do not perform that action. Record actual visibility, not the planned condition.

Observe the timer's end

Before and after the expected expiry, record the playback screen, whether sound is heard, playback position and observation times. If the audio finishes naturally first, a call occurs or the output disconnects, that round alone cannot establish timer behaviour. Note the interference and, if appropriate, repeat under the original conditions. A lit or dark screen alone cannot establish whether playback stopped; inspect sound and visible app state together. If the app does not show remaining time, record only what is actually visible.

Prepare the manual-lock round

On the same device select the same audio and timer duration again. Confirm starting position, playback mode and output device, and note the start time. Change only whether the screen is locked where possible; record any changes of file, headphones or volume. Stop the round if timer activation is uncertain. Do not change the passcode, disable locking or hand the device to someone else; use your own device.

Observe while locked

Lock the device normally and let the selected interval elapse. Record whether audio is heard during that time and the approximate stopping time. The lock screen may or may not display media controls depending on device settings; neither outcome is equivalent to whether sound exists. After expiry, unlock using your own normal method and return to 调调 to inspect playback state and position. Do not record a passcode or share screenshots containing private notifications.

Compare both rounds

Keep separate rows for the visible-screen and manual-lock rounds, each with audio identity, timer duration, interference, sound before and after expiry, and the state on returning to the app. Any cell may be unknown or not tested. Separately mark whether timer activation was confirmed, whether the recording outlasted it and whether observation reached the expected time. If any is uncertain, keep the conclusion open. Troubleshoot and keep security boundaries

If audio stops early, check whether the file ended, the user paused, output disconnected or the timer actually started. If sound remains after expiry, check timing records and the actual playback source; do not change security permissions to force an expected result. Auto-Lock and Always-On availability differ by device and settings; consult Apple's documentation and current interface. Record unclear outcomes as unconfirmed.
