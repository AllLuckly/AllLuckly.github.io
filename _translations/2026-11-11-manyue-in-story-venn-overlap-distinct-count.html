Two circles containing five and four people need not contain nine distinct people altogether. Read an explicit two-set diagram in ManYue using identifiers for overlap and outsiders. This is not automatic face deduplication or area-based counting. Establish universe, identity and circle meanings before arithmetic, leaving missing information unknown rather than filling blank regions.

Identify the universe and both classification conditions

Record work, edition, chapter/page, panel and legend. Identify the rectangle’s universe and both circle meanings. Our ten numbered cards represent fictional readers: A read magazine Alpha, B magazine Beta. Both conditions may hold, unlike exclusive alternatives. Without universe size, outside count is not determined, and page whitespace does not represent infinitely many nonparticipants. Count people, not reading events or books. Each identifier denotes one person; specify categories and units before counting.

Check membership identifier by identifier

A contains 1,2,3,4,5; B contains 4,5,6,7, with eight, nine, ten outside both. Record A-membership and B-membership for every identifier. Four/five satisfy both without becoming two people. A duplicated identifier in a picture requires checking repeated display versus unclear identity, not deciding from avatar colour alone. Keep the complete one-through-ten ledger beside the diagram so undrawn outsiders do not disappear. Overlapping membership is precisely the relation to retain, not a duplicate record to delete into exclusive groups.

Separate four disjoint regions first

Only A contains one, two, three: three people. Intersection four/five has two; only B six/seven has two; outside eight/nine/ten has three. These disjoint counts sum to ten, each person appearing once. A’s five already includes the shared two, as does B’s four; five, two, four, three are not disjoint-region totals to add. Name exclusive regions separately from whole circles instead of only left/right. Rotated layouts retain category identity through labels, not viewport sides. Count from membership, not circle size.

Subtract the double-counted intersection once for union

The union, at least one magazine, is five plus four minus two, or seven. Outside count is ten minus seven, three. Adding five and four gives nine membership occurrences, double-counting the two shared people, not nine distinct readers. Both magazines gives two; exactly one gives three plus two, five. Map “at least,” “both” and “only” to regions. Do not leave overlap undecided from the word “or” alone. An explicitly exclusive-or definition requires its own reading rather than an assumption from everyday wording.

Recalculate all regions when changing overlap

For a separate count control, keep A five, B four, universe ten but change intersection to three. Union becomes six, outside four, only A two, only B one. This is a feasible counting assumption, not the original identifiers’ established memberships; drawing people would require a new list. Overlap cannot exceed the smaller set’s four, so assigning five breaks conditions. Change one stated input, then check nonnegative regions and total. Enlarging a drawn circle does not establish changed membership; counts still require labels or member evidence.

Separate an empty region from absent evidence

An undrawn avatar does not establish zero: a diagram may use outlines and numerical labels only. An explicit zero is different from unknown. If a balloon obscures outsiders, mark limited visibility; arithmetic outside count does not mean you observed three avatars. Keep calculation separate from image observation. For inconsistent totals, inspect units, times and duplicate transcription before removing a member to fit a preferred result. Respect permitted scope for private material; fictional identifiers suffice without collecting real people’s reading histories.

Troubleshoot boundary placement and legends

For an avatar near a boundary, consult text, leaders or legend rather than automatically classifying by its centre. Unresolved membership stays unresolved even if a total invites convenient assignment. Keep both labels and universe boundary when zooming; an overlap-only crop loses scope. Record category names even with colour coding, relying on text when colours are unclear. A redraw is explanatory, not proof that the source is consistent because it contains ten dots. Every definite membership must trace to the source or explicit original stipulation.

Exit and return to check four regions and three questions

Exit the reader and return. Recount four regions from identifiers before answering at-least-one seven, both two, exactly-one five. Check outside three against universe ten, then the independent overlap-three control without importing old identities. Save universe, categories, membership list, region counts, question wording and unknowns. Completion means detectable double-counting, not app-based people recognition or proportional circle areas. A real survey with changed scope, time or units needs new evidence; these ten fictional readers support no real-world conclusion.
