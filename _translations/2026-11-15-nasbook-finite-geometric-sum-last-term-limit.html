Write shifted rows and cancel corresponding terms

Name the sum S. Doubling yields six plus twelve plus twenty-four plus forty-eight plus ninety-six. Subtract the original row: four equal terms cancel, leaving two S minus S equals ninety-six minus three, so S is ninety-three. Align by values and multiplication, not display wrapping. Cancellation removes matching terms, not arbitrary neighbours. Ninety-six is outside the original five terms but has a defined derivational role.
