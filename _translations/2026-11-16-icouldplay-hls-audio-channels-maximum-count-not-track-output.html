Two audio entries declaring 2 and 6 do not mean eight tracks play at once. Read an original audio-group sample, audit CHANNELS as a maximum simultaneous-channel count under RFC 8216 and separate media evidence from listening. This is no home-cinema certification or proof of field accuracy inferred from a few audible directions.

Identify two audio versions of the same content

Use an owned same-content source or the reading sample and record video identity, audio versions and acquisition time. Both paper renditions assume AC-3 with different channel counts. Without bytes, those are assumptions rather than measured reports. Different content, language or edits must be recorded before treating commentary and dialogue as a pure channel comparison. Preserve a playable reference and separate external evidence from iCouldPlay listening. No new equipment or in-app multichannel mixing is required.

Count one group and two entries first

The code has one sound group and two distinct NAMEs, Stereo/Surround, each with a media-playlist URI and quoted CHANNELS values 2/6. AUDIO references sound; renaming a group cannot create six-channel output. CODECS declares hypothetical H.264/AC-3 combinations without downloadable resources. Only Stereo has DEFAULT/AUTOSELECT YES; Surround has NO, avoiding an unconditional duplicate automatic candidate. Fields still need real-media agreement. Tidy declarations do not establish completed encoding.

#EXTM3U
#EXT-X-VERSION:4
#EXT-X-MEDIA:TYPE=AUDIO,GROUP-ID="sound",NAME="Stereo",LANGUAGE="en",DEFAULT=YES,AUTOSELECT=YES,CHANNELS="2",URI="audio-stereo.m3u8"
#EXT-X-MEDIA:TYPE=AUDIO,GROUP-ID="sound",NAME="Surround",LANGUAGE="en",DEFAULT=NO,AUTOSELECT=NO,CHANNELS="6",URI="audio-surround.m3u8"
#EXT-X-STREAM-INF:BANDWIDTH=1800000,CODECS="avc1.4d401e,ac-3",AUDIO="sound"

Interpret the first parameter as a maximum

Under RFC 8216 the first parameter is the maximum independent simultaneous audio-channel count present in any segment of that rendition. Do not sum counts across time or use the number of MEDIA rows. Hypothetical counts 2, 6, 2 have maximum 6, not sum 10; this is a statistical card without evidence for encoding or discontinuity handling of configuration changes. Two nonzero signals at one instant in a six-channel structure do not reduce it to two channels. Audibility is not structural presence.

Check the same-codec different-count requirement

Both renditions here use AC-3 but different counts, making CHANNELS required under this condition. The RFC generally recommends it for audio; do not turn every absence into an implicit stereo value or universalise mandatory status beyond its conditions. Remove Surround’s field in a fault copy while retaining the known media assumptions: the audit detects an omission, not conversion from six channels to two. Restore quoted values, distinct names and matching group references before observing client selection.

Separate count 6 from a 5.1 layout label

Six is an integer count; layout and roles need codec and media evidence, not a promised speaker assignment. An AC-3 5.1 example counts five main channels plus one LFE channel, totaling six, but 5.1 cannot replace the integer first parameter. Two speakers do not disprove six-channel media because processing may occur along the path. Keep group count 1, entry count 2, selected media maximum and output conditions separate. Do not add 2+6 as eight simultaneous channels or infer a volume percentage.

Identify selection before listening to one interval

After actual playback, record selected name, language and visible channel labels; mark unavailable fields honestly and attach an existing trustworthy external report separately. If selection is possible, replay the same dialogue or original prompt after switching, fixing output, speed and comfortable volume. Report heard differences without inventing six independently rendered outputs. Different mixes can alter dialogue clarity without proving a downmix fault. Without media or a control, leave switching unperformed rather than invent a multichannel panel.

Diagnose references and values before volume

If one rendition is silent, separate URI, retrieval and decoding checks rather than let Stereo certify Surround. Repair unquoted CHANNELS as an attribute-type issue; count two MEDIA rows before calling 6 six tracks. Preserve source and report identities for a metadata mismatch and ask the source administrator to check production conditions. Changing a field neither downmixes media nor adds speakers. Do not change codec, declaration and output simultaneously, or keep raising volume to infer unsupported directions.

Reopen with four quantities and limited conclusions

Reopen the reference and verify one group, two entries and the ownership of 2/6, then recompute max(2, 6, 2)=6. Reenter real media, inspect actual selection and restore desired everyday track/output, leaving untested paths unknown. Deliver reference, fault copy, media evidence or assumptions, same-interval listening and output conditions. CHANNELS is neither sound pressure, track count, fixed hardware layout nor a promise of unchanged iCouldPlay output. Completion verifies scope and calculations rather than every channel on all hardware.
