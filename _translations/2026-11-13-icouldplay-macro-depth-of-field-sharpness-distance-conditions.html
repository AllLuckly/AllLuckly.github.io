Unreadable near/far cards in close-up footage may reflect captured depth of field, movement, source resolution or display scaling. Depth of field concerns capture conditions, not an assumed in-app background-blur control or authenticated lens settings from one screenshot.

Plan three separate cards at different depths

Plan three original cards marked near, middle and far with distinct short words, offset without obscuring text. Fix viewpoint, card orientation and light, using the middle card as the focus target. Capture externally with available equipment and preserve originals if possible; otherwise keep an unperformed storyboard. Record chosen spacing rather than universal centimetres. Copy no protected test chart and assume no automatic focus-plane detection, aperture extraction or capture control in iCouldPlay: it supplies playback and manual observation.

Separate depth-of-field definition from readability threshold

Photography references describe a region of acceptably sharp appearance around the focused distance, not only an infinitely thin readable plane. Declare an operational criterion, such as distinguishing every character at one display size, without calling it a laboratory standard. Keep card type and size equal so larger far text cannot masquerade as greater depth of field. Record unreadable/uncertain text rather than filling from memory. Depth spacing is a design condition; do not prefill “middle sharp, others blurred” before viewing.

Create an aperture control only under stated conditions

If the capture device actually permits control, plan f/4 and f/8 versions with the same focal length, viewpoint, card positions and middle-card focus distance. The smaller f/8 opening generally increases acceptable sharp depth under otherwise matching conditions. Record shutter or other exposure compensation instead of falsely claiming a single changed parameter. Without controls, do not impose numbers or infer captured f/8 from appearance. No purchase or hidden feature is required. Sensor, processing and viewing criteria matter, so one aperture field supplies no exact near/far limits.

Read one moment under fixed display conditions

For actual files, confirm version, source and fit mode in iCouldPlay, then use a stable interval with all cards visible. Pause to read and resume to check stability, keeping screen, orientation and display size comparable across versions. Record readable/unreadable/unknown per card rather than judging attractive blur. Use content anchors and approximate intervals without claiming identical-frame positioning if unavailable. Mark storyboard playback unperformed; paper f-numbers are not source metadata, and absent produced controls means no verified aperture effect.

Log movement and changing focus separately

Look over time for one card smearing, shifting or all becoming soft, separately from near/far differences at a single moment. Movement is not automatically depth-of-field change, and one defocused card proves no whole-frame shake. Log changing sharp regions when focus deliberately moves instead of mixing that temporal event into a fixed-focus aperture comparison. Pausing cannot repair motion blur already captured. Retain processing unknowns for edited blur/sharpening and infer no specific algorithm from compression edges.

Keep display scaling separate from captured aperture

An optional fit-versus-larger display comparison uses one file and belongs to display conditions; it does not change captured depth of field. Enlarging may reveal existing character edges or merely expose existing blur. Record actual shapes, not just a magnification label. Without suitable controls, retain one display mode rather than inventing a macro-enhancement button. Rescaling a screenshot does not change source-video resolution, and compressed sharing images should not replace the source for optical comparison.

Troubleshoot missing premises without guessing lens settings

If f/8 shows no additional readable text, inspect focus, spacing, identity, display size and exposure-related motion instead of invalidating the definition. Without lens/processing records, report card readability and do not infer a unique f-number from blur. Universal sharpness likewise proves neither a small aperture nor automatic compositing. Repair one known record/selection at a time without replacing originals or clearing the library. Keep unknowns; changed text size, source or selected moment to force an expected result must be disclosed and breaks this comparison.
