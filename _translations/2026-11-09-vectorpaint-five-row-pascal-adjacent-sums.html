Triangular arrangement alone does not establish Pascal-triangle relations. Begin with top-row1 and draw five rows, checking boundary ones and the two parents of every interior entry. This manually verified rule diagram assumes no formula engine, automatic table filling or proof tool.

Count the top line among the five rows

Rows may be indexed0–4 or1–5, but declare the convention. Display first through fifth, including the top1. Rows are1;1,1;1,2,1;1,3,3,1;1,4,6,4,1. Preserve this external list and count1+2+3+4+5=15 positions. Keep row labels outside the sums. A source’s “fourth row” needs its indexing convention checked before declaring an error. Historical naming/discovery order is outside scope.

Use one positional rule, without perspective-scaling upper digits smaller and lower digits larger.

This exercise defines boundary ones directly without assuming software adds unseen outside zeroes. Inspect both edges; a mistaken2 at row3’s left is not excused by an interior2 elsewhere. Temporary pale boundary boxes may be removed after checking; final readability should not require colour. Filling boundaries before interiors reduces misplaced row copying.

Use row totals as a second check

Row sums are1,2,4,8,16, doubling each time. Keep totals in a separate check table rather than appending another triangle entry. A correct sum does not replace entry checks because opposite errors may cancel. Verify parents first, then use totals for omissions/duplicates. Distinguish five rows, fifteen positions and final-row sum16; the fifth row has five entries, not sixteen. Label those quantities explicitly.

Inspect digits and adjacency in output

Save and open a PNG, reading five rows/fifteen entries, particularly3,4,6 under font/overlap changes. With Arabic digits distinguish1 from lowercase l; any10 remains one text entry. Hide unwanted position marks/check boxes actively. For crowded long entries adjust spacing/font consistently rather than compressing one10 alone. A thumbnail should retain centring/offset relationships, while detailed sums can sit beside it rather than inside every position.

Check row numbering, boundaries, parents and totals

For an extra row check whether top1 was excluded; for a wrong interior value inspect its two parents; for a slanted layout inspect half-step offsets instead of hiding it under a thick triangular frame. Correct totals with wrong parents still require entry-level errors to remain visible. Retain rows, fifteen-position layout, central explanation,counterexample and output, identifying an optional sixth row separately.
