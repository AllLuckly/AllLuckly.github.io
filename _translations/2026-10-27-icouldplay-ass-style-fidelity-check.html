ASS can carry fonts, outlines, positioning and effects. Loading it does not establish identical rendering to its authoring tool. Use your own clip and an authorised sample to inspect text, timing, fonts and placement separately. iCouldPlay lists ASS/SSA, while actual appearance still depends on the sample and playback path.

Establish a reference appearance

In an appropriate original preview, identify ordinary dialogue, a long wrapped line and a specially positioned note. Record times and reference pictures. Keep the identical video edition; a short cut cannot be compared reliably with full-version subtitle timing.

Retain the ASS and its resource notes. A font name does not prove the device owns that font or that the subtitle embeds it. Identify resource relationships without requiring unknown fonts or system changes, separating text correctness from appearance requirements.

Verify ordinary dialogue first

Select the ASS for a known working video and play a simple line. Check words and timing before complex effects. Successful ordinary text remains a useful baseline when special placement fails, rather than a reason to declare all subtitle capability unavailable.

For no text, check selection, interval and original file coverage. Record the playback path and available controls. Renaming ASS to SRT cannot convert syntax or restore missing content, even when the underlying video plays normally.

Separate font substitution from missing characters

Readable words with different weight or shapes can reflect substitution; boxes or missing characters require coverage checks. Compare common text, special symbols and rarer characters separately. One differing glyph is not necessarily garbling, and some visible Chinese does not validate every character.

For exact typography, verify rights and supported resource handling. Without a confirmed font-import control, do not promise that a neighbouring font file works. Readable captions and faithful presentation have different requirements; retain originals and use appropriate external output tools where needed.

Inspect long lines and positioned notes

Check wrapping, outline interference and edge placement after controls hide. Compare the special note's intended reference, beyond colour similarity. A visible but displaced annotation may lose its relationship with an object even though its words remain readable.

Record exact time and expected placement and consider a simpler caption copy preserving meaning. Do not globally move all dialogue to repair one annotation; their roles differ and one blanket setting can damage otherwise correct passages.

Isolate style using simple captions

Create a small ordinary-text counterpart for your own sample with matching dialogue and times. Compare at the same scene. Simple success with ASS appearance differences suggests style or resources; shared timing errors require checking video editions first.

Simplification is a diagnostic comparison, without lossless preservation of every effect. Retain the original and inspect information formerly expressed by position, colour or speaker marks. Add explicit speaker text where stripped styling would otherwise make simultaneous speech ambiguous.

Restore settings and watch a complete segment

Record the chosen caption and restore experimental settings. Watch a segment containing all three examples, beyond a corrected still frame. Observe how global choices interact with file-carried styling rather than promising one universal rule.

For different dialogue content, check commentary or other-language audio independently. Font differences cannot explain unrelated words. After reloading, confirm selected file and revision before further changes, preserving an easily recoverable working baseline.

Keep specific rendering evidence

Record format, edition, font dependencies and three observed locations. Use self-made or authorised small examples when seeking help instead of exposing private films. A note intended at the upper left but displayed below is more actionable than a vague appearance complaint.

Completion separates completeness, timing, acceptable fonts and meaningful placement. Choose a simpler copy or original-tool viewing where necessary while retaining full ASS. Format support establishes scope; sample checks establish fitness for your actual task.
