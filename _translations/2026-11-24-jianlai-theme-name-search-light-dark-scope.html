When many built-in color schemes are available, their names can help locate a desired theme. The second current public 键来 App Store screenshot shows a Search Color Scheme Name field on the Keyboard Color page, light and dark options, and theme cards. Search for names actually present on your device in two rounds and record the scope and results; do not assume pinyin, fuzzy matching, or permanent availability of every pictured theme.

Establish the search scope

Open 键来 and navigate to the keyboard color page visible in your version. Record the Enable Keyboard Color state, the current light or dark option, and the active theme name. Do not edit or delete an existing theme. The screenshot supports a search field, but control placement and lists may change by version. If you cannot find the field, record the version and page and stop rather than inventing an entry.

From the visible list, choose a card and copy its complete name as a known target. Do not assume a screenshot name must still exist on your device; cards may change, be renamed, or vary by region or version. Make a nonprivate record of appearance state, target name, and its visible list position. You need not capture a screen containing other private information.

Search for the complete name first

Tap Search Color Scheme Name, or the search field actually shown, and enter the complete name you copied. Observe the results and card names and check whether the target appears; record the actual count and ordering. An input candidate, the search-field text, and the result list are separate states. Seeing a suggestion does not establish that a search completed or a theme was applied.

If there is no result, check spaces, punctuation, and Chinese or English characters one by one. Confirm that the appearance state matches your record, then check whether the target remains in the list. Do not immediately declare search broken or generalize a single failure to every theme. If the field has no keyboard or does not respond, record the page and action and try leaving and reopening it rather than repeatedly changing system permissions.

Compare a shorter search term

Delete the complete name from the field and enter one consecutive portion of that name. Record whether the target appears, whether other names appear, and whether the list is empty. The app might support partial matching or use another rule; this exercise does not guarantee a shorter term will return a result. Change only the search term and keep the appearance state fixed so the scope remains comparable.

Clear the search field and observe whether the original list returns. Use a visible clear button if present, or delete the text in a way the device supports. A restored list is not an applied theme; do not treat a reappearing card as a selection. If the list does not return, inspect the field for a remaining space or other character and record what is shown. Do not delete themes to “clear search.”

Separate search from selection on a result card

Make one row for the full name and one for the shorter term. Record the exact field text, displayed card names, and whether the target is visible. If the list offers preview, use it only if you wish and record it separately; a search hit does not require applying the theme. Do not filter or ignore extra results just to make the two counts match.

If the shorter term does not match, confirm it is a consecutive part of the full name and record the miss. The search algorithm is unknown, so there is no need to guess its internal indexing rules. Clear the field afterwards and confirm the original theme is still in use to leave a restorable starting point for the next round.

Observe light and dark scopes

If the device actually offers light and dark scheme options, record the starting state and switch to the other option. Clear the search field first, then inspect the names in that list. Choose an actually visible name and repeat the complete-name search. The screenshot shows both options, but does not establish identical lists or cross-scope search. Record differing results as observations; a missing target does not mean a theme was deleted.

If you do not want to change appearance, or the other option is unavailable, mark this round unperformed. Afterwards return to the recorded initial appearance and clear the term. There is no need to apply or purchase another theme to complete a search exercise. Keep the original theme, invoke 键来 in an ordinary note, and enter a short marker to confirm your usual state.

Verify and troubleshoot

The record should include appearance scope, original target name, full-term result, shorter-term result, list after clearing, and final restored state. If a theme name contains confusable characters, copy it into your nonprivate record or transcribe it carefully, then compare the search field. Do not assume search ignores case or punctuation. If the host automatically rewrites input, inspect the actual field contents rather than recording only what you intended to type.
