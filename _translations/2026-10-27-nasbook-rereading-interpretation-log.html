Overwriting an inaccurate old annotation can erase how understanding changed. Recheck the source, then date the new interpretation while distinguishing quotation and earlier judgement. This is a manual record, without automatic comparison or generated summaries.

Choose one useful old annotation

Pick one relevant annotation with available edition, chapter and date. Mark unknown provenance honestly. Preserve an old export or text copy before editing. The purpose is to understand the earlier reasoning, not assume the old view wrong and the new one automatically correct.

Verify quotation and context

Return to surrounding paragraphs and verify qualifications, negation and speaker. Record edition changes separately from changed understanding. Use chapter/pages where precise linking is unavailable and inspect context beyond search snippets. Establish accurate quotation before revising interpretation.

Preserve the earlier interpretation

Label the old interpretation, date and evidence without silently improving its wording. Use current note fields or an external document rather than assuming built-in version control. Preserve and mark it superseded as appropriate, identifying omitted conditions or overgeneralised examples instead of a bare right/wrong label.

Explain the new evidence

Identify the passage, later chapter or personal observation that changes the view. Source book evidence and label experience as your own. Keep other-book evidence separately attributed. Distinguish a wording change from a substantive revision and retain uncertainty; narrowing a claim is a valid rereading result.

Separate quotation, inference and action

Check what is quoted, what you infer and what action you propose. Label added assumptions and avoid attributing guaranteed outcomes to the author. Recheck conditions and keep the record concise. Any later quote card should separate interpretation from quotation and receive its own output proofread.

Test return references in the revised record

Reopen the annotation and test its source reference. Use textual anchors across reflow. For a new translation, attribute and locate the new layer independently rather than copying percentages. On another device verify both file availability and note position; record sync does not itself move the book.

Export an understandable history

Export through actual controls and retain date/edition information. Reopen to verify quotation and old/new interpretations remain distinct. Preserve important before/after versions without accumulating duplicates. Readable output is separate from full recovery data and the original book.

Acceptance and future revisions

Finish with identifiable quotation, old view, new evidence and current judgement, plus working references and preserved history. Add dated evidence for later revisions and distinguish edition changes. Confirming an old view can also be useful. The value lies in traceable reasons and clear remaining checks.
