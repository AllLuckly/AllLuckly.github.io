An earthworm is not a hose covered with identical cross-lines, nor is its clitellum an arbitrary central belt. Draw a simplified adult with a visible clitellum, using a continuous body, local grooves and anterior direction. Work from exterior references without collecting, cutting or stimulating animals. Colour and one band do not identify a species.

Reserve a continuous uncrossed S-curve

Use an approximately 1800×1300 px canvas with a broad uncrossed S-curve, generous bends, anterior upper-left and posterior lower-right. Add a thick continuous envelope, including solid inner bends rather than pointed contour crossings. Check the unsegmented silhouette small. Repair sausage-like discontinuities in width and boundaries. Separate crowded bends rather than draw transparent overlaps. This uncrossed pose keeps each later groove attributable, and canvas pixels provide no actual body length.

Mark ends using the reference and band relation

Temporarily label anterior/posterior. Give the anterior a modest rounded taper and the posterior a reference-based narrowing. Add no eyes, antennae or cartoon teeth, and do not replace attachment with an arrow. Use the selected adult reference and clitellum relation rather than universalise end thickness. An unclear reference remains uncertain; screen-left alone does not mean head. Once this invented direction is chosen, trace band and grooves along the same continuous body.

Reserve a broader smoother anterior band

Place a broader, slightly raised visible clitellum in the anterior half, closer to the front than to the tail, rather than at the midpoint or as a tied rope. Show the visible dorsal part without prescribing a fully encircling band or identical width for every species. Turn its edges with the body and use a slightly lighter tone without consuming most of the animal. Establish a pale area and transition into both ends. The band is a body region, not a severed-and-rejoined joint.

Turn grooves across the local body direction

Resolve short groove patches before the band, midway behind it and toward the tail, simplifying elsewhere without claiming a total segment count. Each groove curves around the cylinder across its local axis, rotating around the S rather than staying screen-vertical. Inner bends may compress spacing while outer bends open slightly on a continuous surface. Grooves are boundaries, not cracks exposing ground. Lighten bamboo-like hard rings and avoid a thick white border beside every line.

Run sheen along volume rather than every segment

Use muted warm brown or grey-pink, a slightly darker back and a broken narrow upper highlight, with a lighter related clitellum. Interrupt sheen locally at grooves rather than repeat one white dot per ring. A soft lower shade separates the ground without implying another body. Colour models this illustration rather than universal colour, moisture or health. Reduce overly plastic white contrast and retain modest variation instead of piling on larger highlights to imply slipperiness.

Keep ground and setae within stated scope

Use pale soil and two or three subdued grains as grounding cues without hiding ends and band. Fine setae may be omitted at this scale; optional local cues need clear references and should not become invented long legs. Omission is not absence, and faint bristles are not insect limbs. Keep a restrained shadow beneath the body without extending grooves into ground tracks. This invented ground records no collection site, soil composition or observed behaviour.

Trace anterior, clitellum and posterior continuously

Hide materials and trace front to band to tail, preserving shorter anterior and longer posterior stretches. Repair groove curvature where a bend resembles a cut face rather than conceal it under another contour. Rejoin a loose ring-like band smoothly and move labels back to guides. Enlarged drawing inspection is not biological counting; local groove orientation remains the goal. Locate faults by region and restore highlights only after structural repair.

Reopen and verify band and continuous pose

Save native layers, hide centreline/direction labels and reopen PNG. Check the continuous S and non-arbitrary anterior band fitted, then three groove patches enlarged. Lighten black striping rather than reduce the body to a few thick tubes. Reopen editable body, clitellum, grooves and ground. State adult simplification, unidentified species and local omission. Completion establishes no measured segment count, regeneration experiment or automatic identification of ends, species or physiology.
