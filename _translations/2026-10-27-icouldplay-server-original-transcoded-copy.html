Establish a retrievable original reference

Find the actual source file and record location, name, size, duration and confirmed video/audio parameters. Inspect three distinctive scenes. Leave unknown fields blank and do not substitute another same-name cut or trust specification labels in filenames.

Keep originals and purposeful companion copies separately. Inspect available service playback evidence

Use authorised server controls to inspect actual direct-play or output information where supplied. Record unknowns instead of inventing a common status page across Emby, Jellyfin and Plex. Login success does not establish this session's media handling.

Record time, device and target so another session is not mistaken for this one. Note changed components precisely. Container, video, audio and captions can be handled differently; one unchanged field cannot prove an untouched path, and one changed field cannot prove every picture was rebuilt.

Verify actual content in iCouldPlay

Check duration, episode identity and three scenes from the same source, then compare available and audible languages and captions independently. Record metadata discrepancies before rebuilding connections because display data and media retrieval can originate separately.

Play continuously and seek later. A processed copy may be useful while still needing track and colour checks. Startup alone is incomplete verification, and smooth playback does not prove original parameters. Evaluate the actual requirements of this viewing task.

Compare a complete local original

Where permitted, copy and verify the actual original locally, beyond another server-exported compatibility version. Compare on the same device and scenes with stable speed, sound and display. Avoid changing network, captions and brightness together and attributing every difference to processing.

Inspect detail, sound content, tracks, caption readability and seeking. Visual inspection cannot establish exact losslessness. Combine confirmed parameters and observations, retaining unknowns without reconfiguring every server policy merely to classify terminology.

Choose the path for the task

Casual phone viewing and inspecting personal production detail can have different requirements. Specify necessary tracks or framing and check output against them. Original quality labels are not universal best settings, and lower bitrate does not guarantee unchanged meaningful content.

For your service changes, preserve values, alter one understandable condition and recheck the same sample. Processing, bandwidth and decoding tradeoffs are separate. Use actual available controls or a known working path rather than hunting unverified buttons.

Keep a reusable identity and difference record

Record work, original location, source, session, known outputs and three scene results. Name copies clearly and pair captions explicitly. Recheck after service or device changes instead of extending an old session's success to new track or colour conditions.
