Press the same visible Previous control once at an early and a later position in one recording. Use four independent baselines to record track identity and position, without presuming the icon always selects the preceding track or substituting double presses for single actions.

Prepare two short recordings with opening anchors

Use two owned playable forty-second recordings, opening with 前段甲 and 当前乙, with 乙中段 near ten seconds in B. Retain real creation records; durations/anchors become available only after actual preparation. Avoid long opening silence that would conceal restart versus track change. Keep originals unmodified: this observes existing playback controls. Without materials, prepare the plan first. Match titles, sounds and durations. Identical artwork does not prevent spoken identification and requires no retagging or re-encoding.

Fix the A-to-B history and mode

Through confirmed controls, make A current, then use one identifiable Next action to enter B and verify title/sound. Record queue, mode and entry route. Direct selection in one round and natural continuation in another are not the same history. Establish a repeatable relationship rather than infer an internal history stack. Resolve an unexpected Next target before testing Previous. Rebuild the same A-to-B route between rounds and retain rebuilding separately so an earlier jump cannot silently alter a later baseline.

Identify the same control and press once per round

The current official player image shows previous/next controls but specifies no universal elapsed-time algorithm for Previous. Identify the installed control and retain its location/description while fixing foreground, orientation and output. Do not mix EarPods triple presses, lock-screen controls or holds into this task. Use one short press per round. Preserve an uncertain accidental repeat and mark it inconclusive before a replacement round, rather than call the final result of two presses a single response. The image establishes a control, not an executed return to A.

Run the first early-position trial

Rebuild A→B, then near two seconds in B record its actual identity and pre-press position before pressing Previous once. Immediately inspect title, opening sound and playhead. A return to A, B restart or another actual state are observations, not predetermined answers; two seconds is not a declared software threshold. If the readout is imprecise, retain the actual value and early-position condition instead of claiming millisecond targeting. Do not press again before preserving the result. A paused or changed pre-action source invalidates that baseline for comparison.

Run a separate later trial and repeat both conditions

Re-enter B through A, wait near eighteen seconds without approaching its forty-second end, record position and press the same control once. Retain the early trial’s identity criteria instead of changing a file to force a difference. Repeat each condition once, creating early1/later1/early2/later2, rebuilding the same route each time. Eighteen seconds is a sampling target, not a threshold claim. Preserve differing same-condition outcomes with their positions and histories. Deliver the actual number of completed rounds rather than invent missing observations.

Interpret identity and position on separate axes

Separate destination identity from landing position. B restarting and A selected with unknown position cannot both be reduced to “Previous passed.” If title changes while sound briefly differs, retain both without inventing buffering internals. Check history, action count and pause state

For differences, inspect actual A→B history, accidental repeats, pre-action position/pause, then queue and mode. Renaming tracks, clearing the queue and changing output together cannot identify a Previous algorithm. For unclear controls or no response, restore ordinary play/pause instead of repeated forceful presses. A B restart does not prove A corrupt; inspect A separately. Each correction creates a new condition while preserving earlier results. Without evidence keep the cause unknown rather than invent a developer-defined number of seconds.

Save four-round states and restore playback

Deliver entry route, pre-action identity/position, single action and post-action identity/position for all actual rounds, plus incomplete checks. Restore daily queue, mode and level without deleting recordings. A trial after reopening is new because session history may differ; do not append it as an unchanged original condition. Passing means distinguishing restart-current from select-previous and identifying unmeasured rules, not forcing opposite answers at two/eighteen seconds. Pass no unpressed control, unheard opening or unmeasured latency, and claim no universal hardware-remote compatibility.
