The later action concerns identifiable upcoming entries, not music deletion or storage cleanup.

Using confirmed installed playback and queue controls, establish 岸甲 as current and an actually visible upcoming arrangement of 岸乙 and 岸丙. Clear once and record three states immediately

When the target is clear and rebuilding is acceptable, clear once, then avoid Next, reopening or source changes. Record remaining list rows, current title/playhead and whether A continues, pauses or changes. These states can differ; do not impose another player’s behavior. Cancelling a dialog is not successful clearing. If the track naturally ends at the same time, retain that condition rather than attribute the ending automatically to Clear. Check library entries without reimporting

Separate rebuilding from the clearing result

Rebuild the desired upcoming arrangement through actual controls using the preserved paths/order, and identify whether A was deliberately selected again. Re-adding B/C is a new action; two final rows do not prove clearing failed. Record stale entries or mode changes rather than repeatedly manipulating controls until the screen matches expectations. Reimporting audio is not a substitute for rebuilding a queue: library entries and playback arrangements are different sets. If clearing was not executed, preserve the original arrangement instead of deleting something else merely to complete the article.

Diagnose scope and event-order mistakes

For remaining rows, inspect the actual target, completed confirmation and any subsequently added items before deleting files. For silence, check pause/natural ending, identity and output separately from row removal. For missing library entries, revisit location/filter conditions; one empty queue screenshot is not deletion evidence. Test one condition at a time and preserve the before-ledger and responses. If scope remains uncertain, stop repeated actions and deliver page wording and stopping point. Without suitable measurement, report no millisecond gaplessness or perfect continuity.
