Position can matter more than two occurrences. Check epanalepsis within a fixed authorial line: preserve boundaries, compare ends and interpret how intervening material changes the returning word’s context. Book Isle supports reading and marking; the reader establishes form and effect from the text.

Save boundaries for three original cards

Use the original English below. A/B are one authorial line each; C explicitly contains two sentences. Letter labels identify cards and are not first words of the quoted text. Export a line-preserving PDF or mark authorial lines clearly in EPUB, retaining the editable source. Check punctuation and final words after import and bookmark A. Narrow-screen wrapping does not change the stipulated line unit or turn an internal hope into new boundary evidence.

A: Hope is the work we do to keep alive our hope.
B: Hope is the work we do when mornings grow cold.
C: We keep our hope. Hope steadies us.

Declare the stricter positional usage

Silva Rhetoricae gives a broad return after intervening material and a stricter opening/ending repetition within the same line, phrase or clause. Use the latter with A as the complete authorial-line unit. Any later hope is not automatically sufficient, and Chinese characters are not silently counted as English words. Save definition, unit and locations together. Other materials may use a wider sense; identify that convention before declaring disagreement an error.

Count words and extract ends without deleting the middle

A has 11 words, Hope first and hope last, with 9 intervening words. Ignore initial capitalization and the final period only for comparison while preserving source form. Record positions 1/11 externally, highlight both ends if the format supports it, and link the two occurrences with the same identifier in a note or external table. Without highlighting retain the whole line and extracted words. Do not delete the middle to create symmetry or claim repeated our hope, which is absent initially. Fix the matching unit and length before comparing.

Use B to test the required ending position

B begins Hope but ends cold, so it fails the strict boundary condition. It may still discuss hope or share a theme; semantic similarity does not replace matching positions. Retain whole lines with their different endings. Align common openings and mark changed latter portions without recasting B’s opening fragment as a separate authorial line with an invented final hope. Evidence requires the word at both ends of the chosen unit.

Changing A’s last word to hopes keeps a related topic but breaks this exact-word-form rule; hopeful does not qualify merely by containing hope. Ignore only initial capitalization and final punctuation, not silently lemmatize, substitute synonyms or match substrings. Keep controls separate from source and comparison rules.

Keep C’s cross-sentence linkage separate

C ends its first sentence with hope and begins the next with Hope. This crosses a boundary rather than repeating both ends of either sentence. First-sentence endpoints are We/hope; second endpoints Hope/us. Draw its connector between sentences, separately from A’s same-line return. This preserves location evidence without requiring a work to use only one repetition device or forcing every echo under one label.

Interpret the intervening matter

A connects hope to work we do and action sustaining hope. The returning word therefore follows a practical account. Proposed effects include thematic return or hope needing maintenance, but repetition proves neither future success nor the author’s optimism. B has cold-morning context; C develops support in a second sentence. Cite intervening wording for each reading. Comfort and persistence may remain alternative tones with evidence rather than one universal psychological effect.

Diagnose search counts and wrapping artifacts

Two search hits for hope do not establish same-line endpoints. Return to authorial boundaries and check labels/punctuation; zoom out from a final fragment to confirm the beginning. A translation may vary wording, so keep original English as formal evidence rather than demand Chinese explanation recreate the pattern. Correct extracted notes or scope instead of altering the source to qualify. Leave unperformed search/highlighting pending; two hits are not automatic rhetoric detection.

Reopen and accept scope, match, effect separately

Reopen A and state complete authorial-line scope, Hope/hope at positions 1/11 and the role of nine intervening words. Revisit B’s differing ending and C’s cross-sentence boundary without denying their meaning. Deliver source, comparison rules, endpoint ledger and supported effect candidates; leave reopening blank if unperformed. New editions or lineation need renewed scope checks, not isolated hope tokens stripped of their positions.
