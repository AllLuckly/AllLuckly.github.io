Three to one may denote only the current round, and two round wins need not mean a total-score lead. Read fields and rules in a fictional scoring game without imposing real sport conventions. ManYue supplies viewing and location, not score recognition, adjudication or invented ranking rules.

Fix team fields and scoring scope

Choose readable scoreboard and context, recording work, edition, chapter, page and panel. Identify headings, round and cumulative status. Invent A/B scores two-one, zero-three, three-one across three rounds. Establish teams instead of equating screen sides across a swap. A colon may mark time or another identifier; inspect labels. Apply a real work’s stated rules rather than imposing the sample’s three rounds on a sport.

Record each round before totals

Create five fields: round, A score, B score, location and completion state. Enter two/one, zero/three, three/one. Zero is explicit here, not blank or unrecorded; an unexplained dash in a real image is not automatically zero. Mark ongoing versus final and retain later revisions separately. Preserve each locator and do not add a cumulative display as another round. Stable scope must precede calculation.

Add scores for the same team only

After three rounds A totals two+zero+three=five and B one+three+one=five. After round two totals are two-four. Round three’s three-one is not the cumulative score. Bind each total to completed rounds instead of comparing displays from different times. Use an existing external calculator if needed without claiming image recognition. Follow team identity across screen-side changes rather than blindly adding the left column.

Separate round wins from total points

Under the invented higher-score-wins-round rule, A wins rounds one/three and B round two: wins two-one while total points tie five-five. Do not change A’s five to six because of two wins or claim every round tied. Real works may rank by another metric; obtain rules rather than assuming round wins determine final standing. Separate round comparison, win count and cumulative points. A character’s lead needs its metric identified or ambiguity retained.

Require a source for tie-breaking rules

Five-five alone establishes equal cumulative points, not a playoff, preference for A or last-round victory. If the invented instructions additionally rank by round wins, A wins under that added premise while total points remain tied. The rule is not derived from five-five. Ask what rule is missing rather than supplying real-sport knowledge. Preserve a narrated result separately when its calculation basis remains unavailable instead of making the evidence appear complete.

Check dependent values after a one-point correction

In an external copy, change B’s round-two three to two only. B’s total becomes four while A stays five; A still loses that round zero-two, so wins remain two-one. A score correction need not change round winner. Check dependencies individually. Label the invented control, retaining both versions rather than altering a publication. For author errata, record source and edition and recalculate actual changes; an imagined point is not an official correction.

Check headings and time state when values disagree

For totals, check missing rounds, double-counted cumulative scores and ongoing versus final status before digits. Inspect three/eight or zero/letter confusion from the source rather than plot expectation. Mark occluded values unseen instead of reconstructing a unique history from the final score. An omitted round can leave known total but unknown round scores. Reports need round, team and location. Missing ranking rules are not a reader defect or an invitation for app-invented regulations.

Recompute three kinds of result after returning

Leave the reader and return to all three rounds, independently deriving total five-five, round-three three-one and wins two-one, explaining their different scopes. Recheck five-four and unchanged wins after the control correction. Preserve five fields, rule sources and unresolved standing separately from the original. Completion gives each number a team, round, state and evidence, not merely a victory cry or a current-score screenshot as proof of a completed rule-defined match.
