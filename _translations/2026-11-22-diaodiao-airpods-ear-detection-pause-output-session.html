Confirm devices, recording and safe conditions

Check system ear detection

Wear and connect the AirPods, open their settings on iPhone and check whether Automatic Ear Detection is available. Establish a two-ear baseline

Return to 调调 and find the local recording. Confirm the intended AirPods are the phone’s selected output, play the cue, and observe title and progress. Remove one earbud and observe three states

Record removal of both separately

Compare the disabled setting only if reversible

Only if the user wants the comparison and knows the original value, temporarily disable Automatic Ear Detection in system settings.
