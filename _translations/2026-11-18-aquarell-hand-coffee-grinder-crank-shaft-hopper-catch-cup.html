Draw a slender manual conical-burr coffee grinder with a dark upper hopper and a translucent lower catch cup. Similar cylindrical forms have different roles: crank meets shaft, lid surrounds the shaft, and the cup joins below the hopper. Establish these connections before transparency or particles.

Allocate upper and lower sections along one axis

On an approximately 1800×1400 px canvas put the body axis near x=900, lid centre near (900,420) and cup bottom near y=1100. Upper hopper and lower cup may differ slightly in width but share the axis. Separate sketch, solid colours, translucent cup and crank detail, using a pale warm-grey background. Reserve space on the right for crank and grip before centring the body too tightly. These coordinates guide a hand drawing; they are not product proportions or assembly tolerances.

Let the lid surround the shaft below the handle

Draw a compressed lid ellipse with a firmer near edge, then expose a short shaft at its centre. Connect the crank to the shaft’s top, not the lid rim or inside the lid. Use a narrow lid-to-hopper seam rather than a background-filled break. Locate the shaft inside the lid surface before removing concealed guides. Omit an unseen small cap with a simplification note if necessary; do not invent gears, blades or motor buttons merely to make the mechanism look complex.

Give crank and grip their own axes

Extend the crank from about (900,370) to (1340,310), giving the arm thickness with two nearby edges. Attach a short upright grip at its outer end so its own small rotation axis reads; it is not a black droplet flattened onto the arm. Identify main shaft, crank arm and grip axis without turning the grip into a second body. Inspect occlusion at both ends: metal edges stop behind the grip. Draw one stationary angle, not another unexplained handle overlaid at a second position.

Keep a complete junction between hopper and cup

Make the dark upper hopper slightly waisted and join a straighter catch cup below. Indicate their junction with a narrow collar and short contact shadow, with the near collar occluding rear contours. The cup must not float as a separate bowl outside the hopper. Temporarily separate sections with pale colours and check matching rim ellipses along the axis. The lower cup collects grounds, not a second complete hopper and crank. Because the casing is not cut away, do not show burrs or springs through an opaque wall even when the reference lists them.

Separate translucent plastic from opaque surfaces

Wash the lower cup with pale grey-brown so the background still influences it, then strengthen its curved edges. This represents translucent plastic, not specified glass. A small invented patch of grounds may sit at the bottom, with a surface consistent with the view; filling the whole cup with dots would not measure capacity. Keep the hopper more opaque and darker. Avoid equally heavy white outlines everywhere: lid, arm and cup need reflections of different widths, with subdued far-side detail.

Check cylindrical volume against the narrow metal arm

Light from front-left: give the hopper a lighter left plane, local-colour middle and soft right shadow, connected as one curved body rather than three stickers. The narrow crank needs only a thin light plane and grey lower edge, not the body’s broad shadow bands. Darken the grip contact slightly without severing its axis with a black ring. Hide detail and check that the cup’s contact shadow meets the table instead of suspending the grinder above a glow. Invented lighting does not assess grinding quality.

Inspect shaft, arm, lid and cup in order

Hide reflections and trace crank centre to shaft, lid, hopper and cup seam. A handle apparently inserted into the wall needs its centre relocated to the axis. A floating lid needs restored near-side contact; escaped grounds call for checking whether detail obscured a boundary. Preserve the faulty copy and fix one connection without moving every layer and misaligning both rims. An external four-item checklist is not automatic assembly inspection and does not imply disassembling or operating a physical grinder.

Reopen to check crank clearance and the translucent cup

Save native work, hide checking colours and axes, then reopen a PNG. Read the complete body-and-crank silhouette at fit-to-window size before enlarging both shaft joints, lid seam and grounds within the cup. If the crank merges with the lid when reduced, improve its readable clearance rather than delete the main shaft. Reopen editable cup and body colours separately. Document the invented pose, omitted internal burrs and nontechnical dimensions, leaving unperformed export/reopening pending. The drawing establishes neither measured grounds quantity nor actual mechanical operation.
