Distinguish script-unit spacing from percentage scaling

Aegisub defines fsp spacing in script-resolution pixels, allowing negative/fractional values, while fscx scales text shape by percentage, with 100 original. Both may widen a line but are different operations: more spacing is not stretched glyphs, and scaling is not a fixed extra 4 between every pair. Size 36 does not nullify horizontal scaling. Font/layout conditions affect glyphs and line bounds; no universal line-width formula or screenshot measurement is supplied.

Use four paper reference points without replacing rendering

Separately stipulate four paper x coordinates 0, 20, 40 and 60, with three gaps of 20. Adding 4 to each gap gives 0, 24, 48 and 72, changing span 60 to 72. Multiplying coordinates by 1.5 gives 0, 30, 60 and 90. These declared geometric points are not actual ABCD advances, kerning or mandatory renderer positions. Four points define three intervening gaps, not four additions. They distinguish gap addition from scaling without passing mobile widths of 72/90. Preserve axes/units; fallback cannot turn the model into a measurement.

Declared paper points, NOT font metrics:
base x:     0 20 40 60
gaps +4:    0 24 48 72
scale x1.5: 0 30 60 90
gaps -2:    0 18 36 54
