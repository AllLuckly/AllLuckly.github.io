Type an original three-line, six-word card, choosing the intended complete words before checking committed text. 行, 重 and 乐 have different Mandarin readings in these contexts. Correct glyphs do not prove semantic understanding; follow the actual Chinese scheme without prescribing pinyin, Zhuyin or candidate order for every scheme.

Fix six target words and an ordinary Chinese draft

In a new private note establish actual Jianlai Chinese input and record scheme, host and installed version, holding theme, font and orientation fixed. Targets are 行李, 银行, 重新, 重量, 音乐 and 快乐, two Han characters each. Complete words supply context; isolated characters do not replace them. Preserve the target externally before comparing output rather than edit it to match errors. Public Chinese input does not promise one encoding or candidate order across schemes. Re-establish identifiable Jianlai input before counting a system-keyboard result.

Identify three contrasts using meanings and dictionary readings

行李 means luggage, with 行 read xíng; 银行 means bank, with 行 read háng. 重 is chóng in 重新 and zhòng in 重量; 乐 is yuè in 音乐 and lè in 快乐. The cited Taiwan Ministry of Education dictionaries use traditional forms. These readings follow the cited entries rather than prescribe every regional pronunciation; this explicitly simplified target remains unchanged. An unnoticed script switch fails exact equality. Pinyin is external pronunciation guidance, not a requirement to type accented letters into composition, nor a claim that Jianlai reads aloud or corrects pronunciation.

Enter the first pair and verify commitment

Enter 行李 through the actual scheme, choose the intended candidate if offered and verify both committed characters before proceeding. Type an ASCII slash, then 银行. The slash groups the card; it is not part of pinyin. A pinyin scheme may use word readings, but accented tone marks are not assumed mandatory for every scheme; other schemes retain their real key routes. Seeing 银行 among candidates while composition remains uncommitted is insufficient. Inspect 银 and 行 in the document. Preserve a missing-target stopping point rather than paste the pair and claim word entry.

Complete the 重 and 乐 pairs in the same format

After an actual newline enter 重新/重量, then another newline and 音乐/快乐, without a final period or space. Commit each left word, slash and right word; repeated 重 or 乐 still need separate occurrences. Soft wrapping does not replace the two source breaks. Preserve host-modified separators before revision. This is not a speed test, so pause after each word. Pronunciation hints stay external and are absent from the final three-line text. Mark any portion completed with another keyboard separately.

Separate repeated characters from pronunciation contrasts

The card contains twelve Han characters, three ASCII slashes and two logical newlines. 行, 重 and 乐 each occur twice but differ in word, position and reading: 行 starts 行李 and ends 银行; 重 starts both words; 乐 ends both. Inspect text positions before consulting meanings/readings. Correct occurrence counts do not prove complete words. Counts apply to this listed card, not all character combinations or host newline byte encodings. Different pronunciation need not mean different code points: both 行 are the same character, not invented special variants.

Use a one-word control to separate text from meaning

In a separate external control replace 重量 with 重亮. It still has two characters and may preserve total length, yet fails the target. Keep the failure copy private. Length and the correct initial 重 do not establish the word or meaning. Compare the real committed draft word by word, making a separate one-point revision if needed. If host correction happens to supply the target, retain that route: it proves neither correct original taps nor universal automatic polyphonic-word correction. The control is external analysis, not an app-generated test result.

Diagnose scheme, candidates and committed text

Inspect actual scheme, complete target and commitment before host replacements. Font changes do not repair pronunciation because glyph appearance does not determine a word’s Mandarin reading. Keep the simplified target despite traditional source entries. Correct one word with before/after evidence rather than change scheme, reset dictionaries and change language together. An unavailable word needs a confirmed partial result/stopping point, not another keyboard silently added to six passes. Without evidence claim no built-in tone detector or pronunciation accuracy score.

Save six words, three lines and the actual route

Deliver target, original/revised drafts and actual routes for six words, with pronunciation sources and unperformed checks. Pass reopened text only after actually checking all three lines; preserved text proves no synchronized scheme/settings. Restore the original language/scheme and keep this private. Passing means correct complete words/grouping and understanding why one character has contextual readings, not a Mandarin exam or all-word/host candidate certification. Do not pass unperformed typing, speech or device actions.
