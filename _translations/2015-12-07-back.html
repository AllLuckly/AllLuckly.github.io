Recently our company needed to scan bank cards and obtain card numbers. I searched many related resources online; full bank-card scanning
solutions that retrieve card numbers were extremely expensive, and the price only covered licensing. So I looked for an alternative and found one that can
a third-party framework that scans credit cards. I am sharing it here, but it only scans credit cards… o.0.
The framework is called CardIO
Download URL
Here I mainly demonstrate the integration process, so watch these steps carefully.
I am using xcode 7.1. First, drag the whole framework into your project, then under
TARGETS—Build Phases—Link Binary With Libraries
Add the following frameworks there.
Accelerate.framework
MobileCoreServices.framework
CoreMedia.framework
AudioToolbox.framework
AVFoundation.framework
Also add the following under TARGETS → Build Settings → Other Linker Flags:-ObjC and -lc++

In the VC where it will be called, import these headers: #import "CardIO.h" #import "CardIOPaymentViewControllerDelegate.h" Then add the CardIOPaymentViewControllerDelegate delegate.

Then implement the methods.
