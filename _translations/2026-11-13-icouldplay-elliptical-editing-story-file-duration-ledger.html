A six-second file might retain three moments from a minute-long action, or might contain only six seconds of original action. Duration alone cannot decide. Start with a paper timeline under explicit premises, then read a produced owned or permitted copy in iCouldPlay. Separate story intervals, retained pieces and file position. Elliptical editing is narrative organisation, not an automatic skipping fault or a claim that this example has been produced or device-tested.

Declare the one-minute event as an original premise

On paper, posit continuous tabletop action from story second zero to sixty: a marked paper piece moves from the left box to the right. Sixty seconds is an explicit invented premise, not a filename inference. Optionally capture owned props externally and retain the continuous original; otherwise draw an unperformed storyboard. iCouldPlay supplies later ordinary playback, not assumed editing, clock generation or deleted-shot recovery. Give original and exercise copy separate identities so the paper premise never becomes claimed source metadata.

Write all three retained intervals with units

Plan retained story intervals 0–2 s, 30–32 s and 58–60 s, two seconds each, treating boundaries as half-open rather than extra inclusive seconds. Retained duration is 2+2+2=6 s. Omitted gaps are 2–30 s, or 28 s, and 32–58 s, or 26 s: 54 s total. Six plus fifty-four returns sixty. Do not sum endpoint labels or turn three segments into three independent events; the premise specifies one continuous action.

Map the edited file separately from zero to six

Join the three pieces in original order and separately map file intervals 0–2, 2–4 and 4–6 s to story intervals 0–2, 30–32 and 58–60 s. File second three lies in the second piece and ideally maps to story second thirty-one, not three. Handwriting is sufficient; no dual timeline display is assumed. Added titles/transitions require a fresh actual map instead of the six-second total. Separate plans from exported duration and claim no observed cuts or frame precision before a copy exists.

Do not infer omitted duration from object displacement

Read visible paper/box states, then label omitted-duration evidence as the continuous original or explicit creative premise. Left-to-right displacement without a clock/original cannot establish fifty-four seconds, nor does half-distance mean half-time. Ellipsis concerns event portions omitted from this presentation, not scenes secretly stored and hidden by the player. Replaying cannot recover absent images. Give any complete alternate version its own identity/map, preserving the current short version and leaving unseen gap contents unobserved.

Check only the produced copy in normal playback

For a produced copy, confirm title, source and playable range in iCouldPlay, then play start to finish and log content anchors for left box, intermediate position and right box in order. Pause to read markings, then resume. Retain approximate progress readings without pretending frame control. Keep normal speed rather than adding fast playback to force six seconds. If segment order differs, record the export and inspect external editing before blaming the player for missing action. With paper only, mark playback unperformed and limit conclusions to the plan.

Distinguish ellipsis from speed change with a continuous control

Define another paper version retaining the whole minute and presenting it faster to reach a target duration. Changing the time ratio differs from removing two gaps, even if both final plans last six seconds. Ask which source intervals remain. For a produced continuous control, inspect complete action and cuts under its own identity and record what the device reveals. Pitch, interpolation and transitions are outside this check, with no algorithm inferred from impressions. This is conceptual comparison, not promised in-app production or an exact generated speed result.

Troubleshoot mixed timelines and similar copies

If file second three becomes story second three, revisit the second mapping and add one intra-segment second to story start thirty. Do not alter the original to suit a wrong ledger. A recorded sixty versus displayed six calls for checking time categories before file damage. Disambiguate same-name copies with source and anchors, not posters alone. Repair one mapping/record at a time and preserve reasons, without deleting library, cache or sources. Real omitted duration without an original/explicit premise remains unknown, not this example’s fifty-four seconds.

Reopen and hand over the three interval records

Leave playback, reopen the same produced copy, check the segment order from the start, restore everyday speed and retain originals. Hand over the sixty-second story premise, retained intervals, file 0–6 mapping, fifty-four-second omission calculation and observed/unperformed status. Another reader should recompute 28+26 and distinguish creative premises from screen evidence. Paper-only completion is a ledger, not successful playback testing. Success separates presentation length from event span, not recovering hidden actions, proving unknown-file completeness or precise boundary display on every device. Remap new versions.
