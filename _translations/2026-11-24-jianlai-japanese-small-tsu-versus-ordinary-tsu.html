Identify the three kana

The Unicode Hiragana chart lists き, っ and て separately, and also lists ordinary つ. Small っ is not merely a font-scaled copy of つ: the characters have different identities. Their shapes can look similar at small sizes in some fonts, so a thumbnail alone is insufficient proof. This exercise does not require changing text with a code tool.
