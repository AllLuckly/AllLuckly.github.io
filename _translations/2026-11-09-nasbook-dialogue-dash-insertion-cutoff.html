The same dash may enclose an insertion or end unfinished speech. Read a short dialogue, preserve its punctuation and inspect syntactic continuation. This differs from quotation-deletion/ellipsis attribution, with no assumed automatic parsing, dialogue completion or punctuation normalisation.

Preserve punctuation and speaker order

Choose permitted fiction/drama and record book,edition,chapter,speakers and locators. Inspect dash position inside/outside quotation marks, pairing and the next speaker; two visible long strokes do not determine function by character count. Use manual image locators without claiming exact punctuation copying from scans. Preserve the file and necessary excerpts, without replacing dashes with commas,deleting quotes or adding ellipses. Display-width issues belong to the character lesson; this examines contextual function of existing punctuation.

Keep the negative main clause in the insertion example

In the original miniature “我——说实话——并没读完。” (“I—to tell the truth—haven’t finished reading”), dashes enclose the insertion. A separate analytical main clause is “I haven’t finished reading”, retaining subject,negation and action without changing the source. Do not remove the negative and conclude completion or invent another speaker for “to tell the truth”. Establish the same speaker continuing before identifying insertion. Preserve Chinese dash typography rather than requiring English typesetting as comprehension.

Do not invent completion of an interrupted example

Another invented scene has A say “I was going to—” and B immediately reply “Don’t explain yet.” Context establishes interruption, not completion after a parenthetical. Preserve the unfinished words without fabricating “I was going to apologise” as a quotation. A possible intended explanation can be a separately labelled guess, not a fact. Without an established interrupter/action in an actual work, record unfinished speech and uncertainty rather than inventing a speaker from a terminal dash alone.

Check syntax rather than visual pairing alone

Mark a possible insertion analytically and test whether surrounding syntax reconnects, then check quotation boundaries and speaker changes. Widely separated dashes are not automatically a pair. A hesitant “Go—go over there” is not a complete parenthetical merely because two word groups appear. Locate evidence for each reading, retaining alternatives when uncertain. Paired insertion and cutoff do not exhaust literary dash functions; this exercise selects those two contextual cases only.

Consult style guidance without overwriting edition conventions

Use Hull University’s interruption guide for English distinctions between cut-off dialogue and inserted narrative actions, without replacing Chinese-edition spacing,quotation or dash conventions. For translations preserve each language’s punctuation/syntax rather than declaring different forms erroneous. Examine speaker,cutoff and resumption in this passage, not wholesale reformatting. Unstated intention remains unknown; a writing guide is not a mandatory interpreter of every novel.

Preserve cutoff and uncertainty in paraphrases

Separate original,functional interpretation and paraphrase. The insertion still means unfinished reading. The cutoff establishes an attempted utterance left unfinished, not its intended action. Quote cards should preserve the necessary short wording/context rather than removing the dash and implying a complete promise. Preserve negatives without reversing meaning for fluency. Add later evidence/revisions instead of erasing initial uncertainty to imply certainty throughout. This is personal reading, not automatic grammar scoring.

Retrieve the continuation as well as the sentence

Leave and retrieve each target by chapter/wording, then revisit surrounding speakers to exclude another scene with the same name. Display wrapping after font changes does not change punctuation identity; screen-line numbers are aids only. Export through available means and inspect original dashes,negatives,unfinished status,speakers and interpretation separately. Keep the book separately, since notes are not its backup. Share necessary lawful excerpts, label invented examples and never pass invented completions as authorial text.

Separate uncertain function from display faults

For broken-looking strokes inspect source page/character display; for disjoint syntax inspect main clause/speaker rather than supplying a fluent ending. Preserve uncertainty when the next sentence does not settle function instead of treating every dash as cutoff or insertion. Repair summaries losing negatives/unfinished status rather than editing the book. Verify punctuation,context,evidence and paraphrase limits at their locations. The result is supported local reading, not automatic certainty about all punctuation or character psychology.
