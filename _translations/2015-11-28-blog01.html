A technical blog can be more than a website. What else could it become? Let's see. Having recently left my job and been looking for work at home, I could put work aside, visit an internet café, play games, and clear my head. As an iOS developer, I often needed to show app s I had built during interviews. So why not turn my technical blog into an app too? The idea stayed with me, and I tend to act on my ideas. I did not know backend development or how to write the essential APIs ordinary users never see. All I had was my own technical blog... Then a simple implementation occurred to me.
