Distinguish documented pass count from Gaussian strength

Aegisub defines extended integer be strength as the number of ordinary-edge passes: 2 means two passes, not a two-pixel radius. Gaussian blur permits fractional strength; 1.5 is not one-and-a-half be passes. No fixed conversion justifies declaring be 2 stronger because its number is larger. The documentation discourages be in new subtitles because of frame-scaling limitations; it remains here as a legacy-format comparison, not a production recommendation. Zero disables each effect. Large strengths may cost rendering work, but no measured CPU/frame-rate claim is supplied.
