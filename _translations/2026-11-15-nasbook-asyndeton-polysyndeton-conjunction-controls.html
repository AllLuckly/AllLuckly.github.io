Fix three clauses as the comparison units

Each version has three subject/verb clauses. Mark boundaries, then count connecting and: zero, one and two. Count sentence text only, not explanations. Commas are punctuation, not synonymous conjunctions; repeated she is another feature and not added to and totals. All versions retain three actions. Extra conjunctions do not create another event or an additional wait.

Read A as omission rather than missing events

BYU describes asyndeton as omission between clauses, often with hurried or forceful effect. A can suggest compact succession while retaining all three actions. Keep structural observations separate from inferred intent.
