First establish an uninterrupted timer baseline, then insert a deliberate twenty-second pause under the same conditions and observe what happens after resuming. Deliver two observed rounds and two paper clock hypotheses, separating content duration, manual pause and timer stopping without presuming 调调’s internal clock.

1. Choose long enough audio and a real preset

Choose owned playable local audio long enough for the entire round, with ordinary order, fixed output and volume, not a short song that ends naturally. In the installed sleep-timer interface choose an actual preset exceeding eighty seconds, accommodating sixty seconds of playback followed by a twenty-second pause. If unavailable, leave the task pending rather than invent free entry. Five minutes below is only an example; substitute the actual preset and choose audio longer than that value plus twenty seconds with additional margin. Do not change system time or confuse another app’s timer with 调调. Record the original playback arrangement for restoration.

2. Run an uninterrupted control first

Enable the selected value through the real flow and record the externally visible confirmation time, current item and progress, then avoid manual pause or a new source. At stopping, verify that the track has not naturally ended and the queue is not empty, recording visible state. If enablement or origin is unclear, retain uncertainty rather than compute a precise difference against the next round. An external clock supports approximate second-level ordering, not millisecond claims. This first observation is a device-specific baseline, not a universal implementation or a paper calculation substituted for waiting through the actual boundary.

3. Write both five-minute hypotheses explicitly

On paper set confirmation as zero and the preset as three hundred seconds. Play sixty seconds, pause twenty, then resume. If the timer continues on elapsed wall time, stopping remains at three hundred with content advancing only two hundred eighty seconds. If it accumulates active playback only, three hundred seconds of content requires three hundred twenty wall seconds. The hypotheses differ by twenty, but neither is an established app behavior. Pause may also cancel, reset or otherwise alter the timer; record those outcomes separately rather than force a binary choice. Substitute the real preset where needed and write hypotheses before observing, not after selecting whichever number resembles the result.

4. Add only one twenty-second pause in round two

After saving the baseline, re-establish the same preset through visible controls at the same identifiable starting point and record a new confirmation time. At about sixty seconds, pause deliberately, wait twenty seconds with the same external clock, then resume through the actual control and confirm the same sound returns. Silence during the deliberate pause is not timer expiration. If resume fails or timer state changes visibly, record that branch rather than complete the standard path on paper. Hold output, theme, network and other settings fixed so the intentional pause is the clear difference. Unperformed resume or final stopping stays blank; a pause screenshot does not establish the later round.

5. Observe stopping after resume, not merely a countdown

After resuming, wait for the later stop and record approximate wall time, progress and timer feedback, separately noting any natural track or queue ending. No displayed countdown means no invented remaining seconds; a changing number alone does not prove sound stopped. If the confirmation origin is uncertain, describe the operation-time range instead of certifying an exact implementation from proximity to three hundred or three hundred twenty. Keep the manual pause interval and later stopping as distinct events, including whether the same item remains displayed. External clock, playhead and audible state have separate columns and cross-check one another rather than provide interchangeable answers.

6. Compare hypotheses within observation limits

Compare actual stopping times with the baseline: did the pause accompany about twenty extra wall seconds, or a similar wall deadline with less content advancement? Support a hypothesis only when origin, pause and stop were observed clearly. If uncertainty spans both, record indistinguishable. Cancellation or restarting means model conditions changed, not that one clock calculation failed. Limit conclusions to this preset, device and state, not every background, lock-screen or server case. Playhead display differences are not sample-accurate measurements; preserve rounding and manual observation uncertainty.

7. Diagnose natural ending and repeated settings

For early silence, check whether the deliberate pause is still active, audio was too short or the wrong preset was selected. Continued playback calls for enablement, cancellation-after-pause or an accidental reset check. Do not add a system sleep timer concurrently and lose trigger attribution. A call or competing sound changes conditions and prevents unconditional subtraction from the baseline. Restore one factor at a time, keeping responses rather than deleting songs or reinstalling to force matching numbers. No undocumented timer internals, pause-retention rule or accuracy guarantee becomes a repair step. What is established is the actual action, time range and sound.

8. Clear residual timer state and save both rounds

Confirm the timer has ended or cancel an unwanted setting through actual controls, then restore daily volume, output and queue. Deliver preset, both confirmation origins, pause endpoints, actual stopping and natural-ending checks, plus the three-hundred versus three-hundred-twenty paper hypotheses and unknown branches.
