If your version offers reading-break reminders, inspect their scope, timing conditions and continuation controls. This guide verifies application behaviour rather than prescribing a health interval. An in-reader reminder and a system alarm serve different purposes, so interruption behaviour needs direct observation.

Check whether the reminder exists

Inspect the current reader settings and initial reminder state. If absent, retain your existing external reminder approach. Treat an all-comics description as global scope, not a book override. Record version and original state. Example intervals demonstrate controls rather than prescribe a personal schedule.

Choose an offered interval

Select an actual offered interval and verify that it persists after reopening settings. Do not assume arbitrary input or multiple reminders. Handle a visible save failure and confirm again. Check off/cancel behaviour separately so timing observations are not confused with an unsaved preference.

Observe with stable foreground content

Use a complete local chapter and note when readable content opens. Observe ordinary foreground reading through the selected interval if you choose to test it fully. Do not count loading/errors as established active reading or call a switch-state check a completed timing test. Mark unperformed waits honestly.

Record menu and app interruptions separately

Test one interruption type at a time: menu, another app or shelf. Wall-clock elapsed time may differ from qualifying reading time. Record the observed behaviour instead of generalising one overlay’s result to every pause condition. Avoid repeatedly changing intervals during timing comparisons.

Choose the next action explicitly

When prompted, note the scene and choose the available continue or turn-off action. Observe whether another interval begins rather than assuming overlapping repeated prompts. You may leave reading instead. Verify off state in settings. A reminder does not prove chapter completion or understanding; it offers a choice.

Check the all-comics scope

Open another comic to inspect the described global preference. Separate the interval setting from a running session or remaining time, including across devices. Follow existing sync and record observed values rather than assuming identical timers. Investigate differences locally without rebuilding accounts or libraries.

Investigate a missing reminder by conditions

Check saved enablement, readable foreground content, interruptions and interval changes. Distinguish an on-screen prompt from sound or a background notification. Repeat under comparable conditions and mark incomplete waits. A reminder issue is not evidence of damaged comics and does not call for clearing history.

Acceptance and limits

Finish with a persistent interval, understood scope, usable prompt actions and honest test records. No reading-quality or health outcome is established. Use a separately chosen system timer if fixed wall-clock timing is required. Keep rollback values and end reading whenever you choose rather than extending it for a statistic.
