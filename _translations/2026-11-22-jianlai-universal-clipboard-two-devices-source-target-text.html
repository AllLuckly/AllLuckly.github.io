Check devices and reversible conditions

Use two devices you are authorized to use that meet Apple’s Universal Clipboard requirements, such as an iPhone and Mac. Apple says they must be near one another, signed in to the same Apple Account, with Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and Handoff enabled; devices and system versions must meet compatibility requirements. Select the entire range and copy

Troubleshoot connection and host restrictions

First confirm source text and selection, whether another copy occurred, and that the destination focus is editable. Then check proximity, the same Apple Account, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and Handoff on both devices. Apple also notes that a VPN configuration may affect local networking. Do not sign out, alter enterprise management or disable a VPN without understanding the consequences. If a condition cannot be safely adjusted, leave it pending and stop.

To isolate a host issue, recopy the same source and paste into another ordinary local draft on the destination, recording this as a separate round.
