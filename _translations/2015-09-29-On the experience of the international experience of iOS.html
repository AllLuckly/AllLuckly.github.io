This was my first attempt at multilingual iOS localization, and I encountered quite a few problems. Here I will share some of them. I will skip the basic localization setup, which you can look up yourself if interested. 1. First, consider the translation format: "你好" = "hello"; We can prepare the text to be translated in a Word document using "你好" = "";, with one document per language. This makes localization much faster because you do not have to hunt for entries. We used a table this time, and inserting all the translations was painfully tedious. 2. Try to keep translations within the same VC roughly similar in length. Imagine a row of equally spaced buttons where one translated title is very long and another very short. How should the layout adapt? Our simplest approach was: btn.titleLabel.adjustsFontSizeToFitWidth = YES; You can imagine how frustrating that was. If you are reading this, at least next time you can raise the requirement with your translators early. 3. Some languages have multiple variants. During testing, a language may appear to be missing when the real issue is that you did not select the relevant variant, just as Chinese has Simplified and Traditional forms. The ones I encountered most often were: Spanish, which required selecting three variants before it worked; Danish; Russian; Portuguese; Italian.
