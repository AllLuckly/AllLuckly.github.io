Comfortable novel reading often depends on characters per line and how easily your eyes follow the next line, not simply larger type. Use three passages from one chapter to establish Book Isle EPUB settings suitable for your screen.

Confirm that the book is reflowable first

Use an imported EPUB that opens correctly, preferably ordinary paragraphs with a few illustrations. Find a long paragraph, a short paragraph, and punctuated dialogue on the same screen. Fixed-layout ebooks or image-based pages may not reflow like a novel. Enlarging their images does not prove the font-size setting failed.

Before starting, note the chapter and opening words of the paragraph and stay in portrait. Orientation changes page width too, making font-size and direction effects harder to distinguish. Change one item, read two or three screens, and decide whether to keep it. This finds a comfortable combination more clearly than changing font, background, size, and page turning together.

Open reading settings and change only font size first

In the text, tap the center to show reading controls and open Settings. Font size commonly has Decrease and Increase buttons and a current value; adjust step by step according to the interface. Increase one step from default and see whether long paragraphs are easier to follow, then compare another. If only a few words fit and you turn pages every few sentences, step back.

Set a concrete test: read for five minutes at a normal holding distance without leaning closer or frequently losing line starts. This does not prescribe one number for everyone; screen size, vision, and glyph proportions affect comfort. If exact size selection is available, try around twenty as a starting point and adjust rather than pursuing a universal best value.

Judge font and background with actual body text

If font and background options exist, preview the same passage one option at a time. Check whether Chinese characters are cramped, English and numbers are clear, and quotes and dashes are easy to recognize. A typeface attractive on a cover may not suit dozens of consecutive pages. For missing glyphs or abnormal symbols, return to a known reliable font and inspect again.

Choose the background for your environment: clear contrast in daylight, reduced glare at night, without making text and background too similar. If system dark mode and reader background have separate settings, identify which controls the page so one does not override the other during adjustments. An app theme does not change the actual color content of book images.

Understanding line spacing and page counts

Typography options vary between versions. If line or paragraph spacing is available, make small changes and read. Otherwise, improve density through size, font, and orientation rather than searching for a nonexistent advanced panel. Excessive paragraph gaps increase page turns, while cramped gaps blur paragraph boundaries.

After each typography change, let the pages reflow before checking the chapter position. Text moving from page twelve to page fourteen does not mean content was added or lost. Reflowable page numbers depend on layout, so references should also include book, chapter, and original quotation. For textbooks needing stable printed page numbers, use their PDF edition where appropriate.

Then compare in landscape

After settling portrait settings, rotate the iPad or iPhone to landscape and read the same three passages. Longer lines may increase eye travel even with the same font size. If you repeatedly lose your line, return to portrait or reduce the reading area using supported window sizes. More text per screen is not necessarily more efficient; sustainable reading matters more.

After comparing, return to the bookshelf and reopen the book to check retained preferences and reading position. Briefly compare another EPUB. If identical settings behave very differently, consider the book's paragraph structure and content before changing all your habits for one book.

Common questions and reversible adjustments

Why does the progress percentage change slightly after adjusting font size? Layout changes affect pagination, so check whether you remain near the same paragraphs. If clearly displaced, return through the contents list and find the recorded opening sentence. Save important references as bookmarks or notes; a page number alone is unstable.

Why is there no precise line-height input like on a webpage? The reader exposes the typography controls available in the current version; arbitrary CSS or ebook-style editing cannot be assumed. Reading settings affect the display. To alter source styles, back up the file and use an appropriate editing tool.

Will illustrations become clearer when text gets larger? Font size mainly affects text and adds no detail to low-resolution illustrations. Check the source image quality and use a clearer edition if needed. The reader also does not automatically turn scanned images into selectable text.

Keep a reproducible set of preferences

A note such as “portrait, current font, size level, light background” gives a reference when changing devices without recording many useless parameters. When finished, continue with PDF pages and text positioning to understand the other layout; to retain a passage, learn about Highlights, notes, and excerpt cards.
