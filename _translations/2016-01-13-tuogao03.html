II. Some HWPopTool properties and methods

First, look at HWPopTool.h.

The earlier version was written in a hurry, so I used some free time today to reorganize the code. For example, the callback after closing originally used a delegate; I rewrote it with a block for a simpler implementation. I also commented nearly every method, property, and enumeration, as is my habit. A few points need explanation:

1. ShadeBackgroundType controls the overlay background type, with solid and gradient options (ShadeBackgroundTypeGradient). If interested in the gradient, explore CAGradientLayer; it is quite interesting and will appear in a later article.


2. tapOutsideToDismiss determines whether tapping the overlay dismisses popView. Its default is YES.


3. - (void)closeWithBlcok:(void(^)())complete is the callback after closing, useful for cases such as returning to the previous page after sending a red envelope and waiting for popView to disappear.


The comments explain most things clearly, so I will not elaborate here.
