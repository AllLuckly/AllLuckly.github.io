Two circles on a punch are not arbitrary decoration. Draw an invented two-hole desktop punch with a raised handle, partly visible cutters and corresponding base positions to explain the paper path. A sheet indicates location rather than a cutting sequence. Dimensions are not manufacturing plans or evidence of real punching capacity.

Separate base and raised handle planes

Use an approximately 1700×1400 px canvas with an elevated base, front edge lower-left and handle raised from the rear. Sketch a thick base and tilted handle linked by a rear support, not a floating lid. Leave space around all corners and for a short forward sheet. The raised state supplies no opening angle or return speed. If the handle hides the inspection region completely, change viewpoint or its drawn elevation rather than make opaque housing transparent.

Lay out two cutter positions across the base

Mark two temporary cutter axes across one base line, A and B. They belong to a fixed two-hole layout, not arbitrarily sliding drills. Use pale vertical guides before short visible cylindrical portions joining their upper guide/support region. Keep both directions coherent rather than point one forward and one sideways or give unexplained unequal heights. Screen spacing is compositional, not standard millimetres. Cutter positions are also distinct from holes already present in paper.

Match base positions to cutter axes

Project both axes down to corresponding base locations, showing only visible rims or dark regions without opening opaque housing just to expose full holes. Trace cutter, paper plane and lower receiving position continuously in the sketch. Move unrelated-looking front screw holes back to the correct axes. Hole ellipses follow the base plane, not the tilted handle. Use checking colours to separate receiver positions from fasteners before materials. This manual audit is not automatic alignment verification.

Keep a thin open paper passage

Leave a narrow insertion passage above the base and below the cutters. Insert one pale original sheet partly, its front extending outward. Align its edges to the base view rather than pass it through solid material. In this unpunched state, leave the sheet without holes so projected cutter positions do not become a claimed result. A subtle edge suffices instead of a plank-like thickness. Restore a shadow-blocked passage locally; do not move the sheet above the handle.

Distinguish handle supports from surface openings

Connect the handle rear to side supports while keeping its raised front readable. A smooth solid handle needs no large hole; an invented recess is a recess rather than a transparent opening. One seam can suggest the lower paper-waste cover, distinct from another paper passage. Use tone to separate handle, guide housing and base without breaking one part into floating fragments. Omit unsupported internal drive details; the still life does not require a complete exploded diagram.

Use materials to distinguish paper and metal

Use muted dark blue-grey for the base, brighter cool grey for exposed cutters, warm pale paper and a mid-tone handle. A narrow highlight on each cutter should not be duplicated inside receiving holes and confuse projection with recession. Keep the sheet shadow thin and close to the base rather than implying a third box underneath. Add materials after structural checks. Reflections establish neither steel grade, load nor sharpness. Keep the paper original and free of private text or branded documents.

Diagnose two axes and one sheet

Hide highlights and trace A cutter to A receiver, then B to B rather than pair whichever circles are nearest on screen. If one cutter is concealed, record occlusion instead of relocating it to a wrong side to force a count. Repair a sheet wrongly split by the handle at the passage layer. Give a leaf-like base thickness and stable support. Name the affected axis, edge or gap instead of darkening everything. An unpunched sheet in this static drawing is no evidence of a faulty real tool.

Reopen and preserve handle and passage evidence

Save native work, hide labels and axes, export PNG and check the two corresponding positions and single sheet. Inspect raised-handle space small and visible cutters/paper edge enlarged. Clarify merged lines by reducing competing contours instead of creating an implausibly large cavity. Reopen editable sheet, body and materials and note fixed two-hole layout and unpunched state. Deliver no operational, capacity or spacing certification. Completion means another reader can retrace component ownership and the paper passage.
