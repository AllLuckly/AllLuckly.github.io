An ISBN check digit checks mathematical consistency; edition, format and publisher source still need independent verification.

Separate the check digit from book identity

The last digit of an ISBN-13 is a check digit that tests mathematical consistency. The International ISBN Agency says different editions or formats generally require distinct ISBNs. A valid check digit does not establish assignment, title, legality or equivalence of an ebook and print edition. Calculate a public example, then check the actual edition information for a book in Book Isle.

Prepare information to inspect

Choose an ebook you may lawfully read and record title, author, publisher, year, edition and file format. Find an ISBN in the book or publisher’s public information; mark unknown if absent. Do not guess it from a shelf filename or modify the source. Print and EPUB can have different identifiers, so record their sources separately.

Use a public calculation example

The International ISBN Agency manual gives the first twelve digits 978011000222. Multiply left to right by alternating 1 and 3, beginning with 1 and ending with 3. The weighted sum is 56; its remainder on division by 10 is 6, and 10 minus 6 gives check digit 4. The complete example is 9780110002224. This is a calculation exercise, not an identifier to assign to your own ebook.

Handle a zero remainder

The check digit is 10 minus the weighted sum’s remainder modulo 10, with the result itself taken modulo 10. A remainder of zero yields check digit 0, not the two-digit number 10. Check that the input has exactly twelve digits and weights alternate from the first. Hyphens and spaces are display separators and do not participate. An ISBN-10 requires a different rule; do not apply the 13-digit formula to it.

Inspect information visible in Book Isle

Open the actual ebook in Book Isle and inspect the details, copyright page or front matter available in the installed version. Record the exact location of an ISBN and its format label. If details do not show it, record that and consult legitimate publisher information rather than assuming the app must retrieve it automatically. When several numbers are listed for print and ebook, record each label separately.

Check mathematical consistency

For a 13-digit number found, remove spaces or hyphens while retaining the original written form. Verify all characters are digits, weight the first twelve alternately 1 and 3, and compare the calculated check digit with the last. A match is “mathematically consistent.” For a mismatch recheck transcription, position and source before marking pending. Do not edit the sole EPUB or declare the publication invalid based on one mismatch.

Check edition and format

After mathematical consistency, verify title, author, publisher, edition and electronic format in publisher or reliable bibliographic records. Print and ebook identifiers can both pass while describing different product forms. Different languages or revisions can also have distinct identifiers. If an internal number lacks a format label, record its position and uncertainty; the checksum cannot supply an edition.

Handle unresolved differences

If Book Isle details and copyright page differ, check that the same file is open, whether book metadata was manually edited, and whether the page lists multiple formats. Do not automatically prefer one source. Record both and observation time and ask the publisher when practical. An electronic file may have no ISBN; absence does not make all such reading illegitimate or prove file corruption.

Keep a reproducible ledger

Separate original ISBN, normalized digits, weighted sum, calculated digit, final digit, format label and source. Clearly label the public example as practice rather than mixing it with a real catalog. Share only necessary bibliographic information, not private accounts, full files or long copyrighted passages. Preserve existing Book Isle progress and notes.

Expand the example calculation

Split the twelve public example digits into two groups: positions 1, 3, 5, 7, 9 and 11 are 9, 8, 1, 0, 0 and 2, totaling 20 at weight 1. Positions 2, 4, 6, 8, 10 and 12 are 7, 0, 1, 0, 2 and 2, totaling 12 before multiplying by 3 to obtain 36. Together they give 56. This grouping helps hand calculation; the alternating order still starts at the original leftmost digit.

After appending check digit 4, the full thirteen-digit weighted sum is 56 + 4 = 60, divisible by 10. If a calculator differs, look for omitted zeroes, separators treated as digits or weights started from the right. Do not include the check digit in the initial twelve and then add it a second time.

A checksum cannot detect every transcription error or establish that a format label is correct. Separate mathematical validation from publisher-record identity verification. If the latter is unfinished, do not claim the ISBN is confirmed for the ebook.

For a real identifier, retain the original separators for later source checks; the normalized digits are only a calculation copy. If the publisher record is unavailable, leave identity verification pending rather than filling author or edition from a search-result title.

Restore and bound the conclusion

Confirm the original book and metadata were not overwritten. Report mathematical consistency, verified publisher identity and edition/format match separately; retain unknowns. Based on public ISBN Agency information, this guide claims no Book Isle device test of a particular details interface and does not claim the check digit establishes assignment or ownership.
