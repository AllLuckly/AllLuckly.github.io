When a call arrives, a playback screen, its progress and the audible sound can change differently. Do not assume 调调 will automatically resume after a call. With a willing participant, make one short call and record the states before, after and, if needed, following manual resumption. Apple documents phone-call audio destinations; the interruption outcome on a particular device and session must be observed.

Arrange a safe, optional exercise

In a quiet place prepare your phone and a short recording in 调调 that you own or may play, with a recognizable sound cue in the middle. Ask a willing person to place one ordinary call at a mutually convenient time. Agree it will be short and needs no private conversation; each person should consider any calling charges. If the conditions are unsuitable or you are expecting an important call, stop and mark the exercise pending. Never call emergency services or strangers for a test.

Do not operate while driving, crossing a road or needing to hear warnings. Use a comfortable volume and avoid exposing private audio in public. Stop other unnecessary audio without changing another person’s device. Record the app and system versions, whether headphones are used, and the recording’s name and duration; do not record the caller’s number or conversation. Keep the original queue and output arrangement for restoration.

Establish the playback baseline

Select the local recording in 调调, verify the selected output and play to the recognizable cue. Record the title, playback control state, position and whether sound is audible. A “connected” label does not prove sound is reaching the headphones, so check actual sound without increasing volume.

Prepare four columns: time point, visible 调调 state, recorded progress and audible sound. Observe without changing the track or queue. If the baseline is already silent, check volume, output and file before attributing later silence to the call. The noted position is approximate: human observation has latency, so a difference of about a second cannot establish an exact switching time.

Observe the call without interfering

Ask the participant to call. When it arrives, observe the system screen; if practical and safe, note whether the previous audio remains audible. Once answered, prioritize the call. Do not operate 调调 simultaneously, record the call or require additional actions. Apple describes selectable call outputs; keep the same output in this round so a route change is not mixed with the interruption observation.

If the call is silenced, missed or the participant becomes unavailable, record that it did not connect rather than making repeated calls. The banner, call state and app display vary with system and device. If playback state is not visible, write “not observed”, not “paused”. Do not generalize this result to all calling apps or headphones.

Check twice after the call

After the call ends, do not press play immediately. Return to 调调 and observe title, control, position and actual sound. Allow the interface to settle while the system changes audio sessions. Record visible play, advancing progress and audible sound separately; none alone proves the other two.

If playback did not resume, use the current visible play control to resume manually, confirm sound and progress, and record the state before and after. If already playing, do not press it and accidentally pause. Compare approximate positions and acknowledge human observation error. If the track differs, inspect the queue and any recent touches rather than assuming the system skipped it. Mark unsuccessful resumption unfinished.

Compare and troubleshoot

If the interface says playing but there is no sound, inspect output, comfortable volume and headphone connection, then check the same recording. If progress is stopped, check the app state and that the call really ended, then resume manually if needed. Change one condition at a time; do not simultaneously re-pair headphones, change tracks or alter settings. If recovery cannot be done safely, pause the experiment and use the phone normally.

One outcome describes this device and session only. A rejected call, an answered call, a different Bluetooth route and another app taking audio are separate conditions; leave untested ones blank. Do not clear the library, uninstall 调调 or reset the phone to force an outcome. Preserve the original playable recording and your preferred queue.

Finish the record

Check that the four-column table covers before the call, any observable incoming-call state, after the call before action and manual resumption if required. Note whether the call connected, output and unfinished steps without storing call audio or a phone number. Restore the original volume, headphones and queue and confirm the recording remains available. This exercise is based on public documentation and does not claim a physical-device or call test.

For a later day or another device, copy a blank table and record fresh results rather than prefilling the earlier outcome. Compare device and output conditions alongside prior notes; list different conditions separately instead of attributing a change solely to an app update.
