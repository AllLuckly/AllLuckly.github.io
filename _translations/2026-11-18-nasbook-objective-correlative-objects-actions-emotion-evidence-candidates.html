A work may organize emotion through objects and actions without saying I am lonely, but every cup is not automatically an emotional symbol. Read three original sentences in Book Isle using objective correlative as a critical approach, retaining evidence for the combination alongside uncertain story facts and alternative interpretations.

Preserve the complete three-sentence context and controls

Use A as one complete passage, with S1–S3 only reference labels. B is a shop-list control and C a direct emotion statement. Keep editable work and export searchable PDF or clear EPUB paragraphs. Verify cooled, hung, set and bookmark A; highlight objects/actions separately only if controls permit, otherwise use an external ledger. Do not extract cup/coat/plate nouns while dropping qualifications, or turn your note nobody returned into a fourth source sentence.

A
S1: Two cups cooled on the table.
S2: Your coat hung by the door.
S3: At six I set a second plate.

B: A shop sold cups, coats and plates.
C: I felt lonely.

A3 control: At six I put the second plate away.

Separate a critical proposal from a universal psychological law

In his Hamlet essay Eliot describes an arrangement of objects, situation and events giving emotion external expression, commonly called objective correlative. Use that approach to inspect organized sensory detail, not as an uncontested law prescribing the only possible artistic expression. Objective does not certify factual truth in the story. Record the question: which emotional candidates do these combined details support, and what context remains missing? A critic’s strong claim does not guarantee identical feeling in every reader.

Record stated details without supplying an ending

A states two cups cooling on a table, your coat hanging by the door and I setting a second plate at six. The two cups are the mentioned count, not the house inventory; second is an ordinal, not proof of an eternal total of two plates. Six supplies no AM/PM or date. You’s identity and presence are unstated. Separate those gaps from givens rather than invent death, separation or long disappearance and cite your invention as evidence.

Use the action combination to propose a reading

Cooled may draw attention to fading warmth or elapsed waiting; the coat retains an association with you, and set a second plate prepares another place. Together they can support expectation, waiting or maintaining a two-person ritual, not one universal sadness symbol per cup. A domestic-scene reading remains discussable through the selection of details rather than an accusation that a reader reacted incorrectly. Death is unstated and loneliness not the only permissible label. Attach wording to each proposal and distinguish support, discussion and missing evidence.

Do not substitute a list or direct statement for the same evidence

B shares cups, coats, plates but shop sold makes a merchandise list without A’s cooling, doorway association and six-o’clock setting. Shared nouns alone cannot prove identical emotional organization. C directly states loneliness without A’s object/action chain. Both methods can work in longer texts or coexist with objective correlatives; lacking the current structure is not literary inferiority. Compare forms of evidence rather than rank all poetry from three short cards.

Change setting to clearing and track the changed emphasis

Replace A’s third sentence with At six I put the second plate away, retaining the first two. Preparation becomes removal, supporting possible ended waiting, tidying or abandoned expectation without proving death or permanent absence. Contrast set and put away rather than assume equal nouns require equal interpretation. This is an alternative third sentence, not a fourth event after the original setting. Do not invent a setting-then-clearing chronology and attribute it to A. One change isolates the action’s contribution without writing an ending for the author.

Audit source, inference and counterevidence in notes

For two cups therefore someone died, identify the actual death evidence; without it, remove or downgrade the claim. If objective is read as emotionless, return to external expression of emotion. Search counts for cups do not establish the combination, and one highlight colour does not prove a complete chronology. Correct note copies while preserving original/control identities. A changed edition or OCR missing second needs source-page checking before stronger conclusions are drawn.

Reopen with two candidates and one unproved fact

Reopen A without notes, copy three stated details, then support waiting and domestic ritual as two candidates. Retain limits such as unproved death and unspecified time-of-day, comparing removal versus setting in the revision. Deliver source, relationships, interpretations and limits, not an emotion word or term alone. Mark unperformed highlight/bookmark/reopen actions pending. Claim no automatic Book Isle emotion reading, author-intent detection, resolution of critical debate or proof of real people’s experiences.
