Prepare source, accessories and backup

Prepare your iPhone, compatible connector or adapter, an existing compatible external drive and one short recording you own or may copy. On the source device verify it plays; note name, size, duration and a recognizable cue. Keep the original and another backup. Do not use private audio from another person’s device or buy equipment just for the exercise.

Apple notes that external devices may require compatible formatting, a single data partition and additional power. Check accessory instructions and your phone model. If uncertain or the drive contains irreplaceable data, stop and consult someone familiar with it. Never erase, format or repartition a drive merely to make it appear; these actions can remove data. Stop for a damaged cable, hot connector or abnormal power and never force a plug.

Identify the drive in Files

Connect the drive according to its instructions, open Files and select Browse to locate its name under Locations. Apple notes you may need to tap Browse again. Record the device name and a non-sensitive path description; do not publish private listings. If absent, check connector, power and compatibility against Apple’s guide before considering other action. Formatting is not a first troubleshooting step.

Open the confirmed practice folder, locate the audio and check name, size and modification time. If system preview is supported, listen for the cue and record it. A filename alone does not prove completeness or 调调 compatibility. For duplicate names inspect the directory and source first; do not open unfamiliar files or modify someone else’s data.

Copy to a recognizable local location

In Files select the audio and use the system’s actual copy or move menu to copy it into a local iPhone practice folder you can identify. Choose copy rather than moving the only original off the drive. If a local destination is missing or unwritable, stop and mark the step incomplete. Names and controls vary by system version.

Use a clear name for the copy. If asked to overwrite, cancel and inspect the existing target; do not overwrite unconfirmed content. Wait for completion and record source and destination separately. Inspect both names, sizes, durations and previews. Matching name and size aid identification but do not prove byte equality; a copy that cannot be opened is not a verified backup.

For an app size limit, unsupported format or protection, retain the prompt and mark incomplete; changing an extension is not conversion.

Check after disconnecting the drive

Ensure copying and playback operations have finished, then disconnect according to Apple and accessory instructions, not during a write. In Files confirm the local practice copy remains visible and preview it if supported. Reopen 调调 and check the same track’s visibility and playback. Report actual results. A local file does not automatically prove import, and playback does not establish byte equality between an internal copy and the drive source.

If audio is missing after disconnection, check whether the local copy truly completed and whether the selected path was the drive rather than local storage.
