A horseshoe-shaped front, rear region and telson should not become a round crab with an arbitrary added tail. Using Limulus polyphemus as a structural reference, draw a simplified dorsal view and connect three regions before shell shading. Most appendages remain concealed, with no complete ventral anatomy or field handling experiment.

Divide one dorsal axis into three regions

Use an approximately 1400×1900 px portrait canvas, front upward and telson downward with room for its tip. Along one slightly tilted axis reserve a broad prosoma, smaller opisthosoma and a telson direction, rather than one leaf-like silhouette. These are biological regions, not arbitrary armour-plate counts. The whole axis may tilt, without an unexplained opposing bend between front and rear. Proportions are invented rather than measured body length. First make the contour traceable from front to tail tip.

Give the horseshoe front a domed surface

Give the prosoma a broad curved front and sides gathering toward its rear connection, like a domed shield rather than an open horseshoe. Keep a broad central light, darker turning sides and a narrow edge thickness. A few shallow ridges are surface features rather than through-cracks. If it becomes circular, correct rear gathering and side directions before detail. The descriptive shape does not require a large background hole; without texture, the prosoma remains one continuous solid region.

Attach the opisthosoma at the rear

Join the smaller opisthosoma below the rear prosoma, tapering its sides toward the telson root. A narrow seam or soft dark band distinguishes regions without a second round shell emerging centrally or floating apart. Check the shared axis before edge projections, which are simplified without a universal count. Restore a continuous centre if the rear resembles two detached wings. A little dorsal overlap may hide the junction, but a broad black gap should not split the animal into two objects.

Taper the telson from the rear tip

Attach the telson centrally at the opisthosoma’s end, with a finite base narrowing toward its tip rather than entering from a side corner or prosoma. Use a light face and dark side, not a six-jointed leg. A slight directional shift should not become an abrupt hook. This body region needs no poison droplet or attack pose and supplies no field-contact advice. If the tip hits the canvas, reduce the composition or provide more space instead of calling a clipped end natural anatomy.

Keep surface marks distinct from a sensory map

Use the dorsal reference for restrained lateral bumps or eye-area cues, turning with the shell rather than cartoon eyes on antenna stalks. This is no complete sensory map; omitted small structures are not claims of absence. Perspective compression need not be repaired into screen symmetry at the expense of attachment. Ventral legs and book gills do not grow on top of the carapace. State the dorsal scope instead of adding unsupported complete organ numbering.

Model olive-brown planes without invented cracks

Use muted olive-brown across prosoma and opisthosoma, cooler shadows on turns and a smaller contrast area on the telson. Two or three narrow lights suggest shell sheen without polished-metal treatment. Sweep texture lightly rather than break every region into cracks. Put shadows under edges and overlaps instead of blackening all seams. Colour is an exterior reference, not a uniformity or identification claim. A restrained ground establishes no actual habitat, tide time or specimen provenance.

Trace the silhouette to the telson root

Hide texture and identify broad front, smaller rear and one telson, then trace both sides to its root. Repair a taper failing to meet centrally rather than add another telson. Restore a narrow solid face if two outlines enclose only ground. Confirm a solid prosoma, attached rear and no ventral features misplaced dorsally. Name the affected region, repair it and restore colour small. Decorative dots cannot substitute for connections among the three regions.

Reopen and document dorsal scope

Save native work, hide axis and labels and reopen an exported PNG. Check three regions and the full telson small, then seams, edges and root enlarged without inferring tiny appendages from a thumbnail. Restore clipped boundaries or unify excessively disconnected regional colours before keeping their seam. Reopen the three editable regions and label species reference and simplified dorsal scope. Completion is traceable drawing structure, not complete anatomy, automatic identification or a performed field observation.
