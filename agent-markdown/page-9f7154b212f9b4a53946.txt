## Distinguish script-unit spacing from percentage scaling

Aegisub defines fsp spacing in script-resolution pixels, allowing negative/fractional values, while fscx scales text shape by percentage, with 100 original. Both may widen a line but are different operations: more spacing is not stretched glyphs, and scaling is not a fixed extra 4 between every pair. Size 36 does not nullify horizontal scaling.
