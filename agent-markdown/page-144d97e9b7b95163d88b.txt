English translation of the [Chinese original](<https://allluckly.cn/guides/manyue-kinetic-energy-momentum-speed-direction-reference-panels/>), translated on 2026-11-18. Original publication dates, attribution, code and version notes are retained.

Two panels of one cart may point in opposite directions while both kinetic energies stay positive. Prepare velocity cards and a two-column ledger, reading momentum direction against squared-speed energy in ManYue. This is a nonrelativistic particle-model reading task, not speed measurement from motion lines or an in-app collision simulation.

## Put the frame and mass on the opening card

Externally draw three versions of a 2kg cart, all relative to the same ground frame with right positive. A has +3m/s, B −3m/s and C +6m/s. Label each value rather than infer metres per second from arrow lengths. Make P1 diagrams, P2 calculations and P3 two-cart controls/counterexamples, retaining editable work. After import verify page markers and velocity signs, then bookmark P2. Use classical speeds far below light speed, excluding extra wheel rotation and internal motion.

## Multiply signed velocity for momentum first

Read p=mv panel by panel: A is 2×3=+6kg·m/s, B −6kg·m/s and C +12kg·m/s. Signs reflect the chosen one-dimensional direction, not negative cart mass. Draw separate momentum arrows aligned with velocity and retain kg·m/s rather than joules. From A to B the speed is unchanged but momentum change is −6−(+6)=−12kg·m/s. Subtract initial from final before considering magnitudes; equal magnitudes do not make the vector change zero.

```text
m=2 kg; right is positive
A: v=+3 m/s; p=+6 kg*m/s; K=9 J
B: v=-3 m/s; p=-6 kg*m/s; K=9 J
C: v=+6 m/s; p=+12 kg*m/s; K=36 J
A to B: delta_p=-12 kg*m/s; delta_K=0 J
```

## Square velocity before asking whether direction remains

Use K=mv²/2 in a separate column: A and B each give 2×9/2=9J, while C gives 2×36/2=36J. Squaring negative three gives positive nine, so reversal does not make kinetic energy negative. With positive mass this translational energy is zero only at rest. Do not transfer B’s velocity minus sign directly to energy, or remove momentum signs to make everything positive. Both columns can correctly describe the same state with different kinds of quantities.

For a single positive-mass cart, zero momentum means zero velocity and therefore zero translational kinetic energy. This single-object conclusion does not transfer to the next page’s two-cart system. Identify whether zero belongs to one cart or the total system before interpreting it. Direction signs, magnitudes and sums are different operations requiring an object label.

## Change speed and mass one at a time

Compare A and C: fixed mass and doubled speed double momentum magnitude but quadruple energy. Add a separate D with 4kg and +3m/s: momentum +12kg·m/s, energy 18J. D and C share momentum but not energy, showing that a momentum value without known mass does not fix kinetic energy. Keep D in a control inset without changing A’s mass. State the held variable in each comparison before interpreting a ratio.

Independently use K=p²/(2m): A gives 36/4=9J, C 144/4=36J and D 144/8=18J. Squaring removes momentum’s directional sign but not the mass denominator. N·s is an equivalent momentum unit to kg·m/s; N alone measures force. Changing the unit expression does not change the physical definition.

## Zero total momentum does not erase two carts’ energy

Place A and B side by side as two independent carts at one instant, not successive states of one cart. Total momentum is +6+(−6)=0, but total kinetic energy is 9+9=18J. Zero total momentum does not make every cart stationary. Only add states here: no collision, deformation or external-force conditions have been supplied, so do not claim the carts must stop or energy must be conserved in a collision. Keep object/arrow assignments clear instead of treating one velocity arrow as a second mass.

## Change both inputs consistently when changing frames

For a frame moving right at 2m/s relative to the ground, classical subtraction gives A/B/C velocities +1,−5,+4m/s. With unchanged masses, momenta are +2,−10,+8kg·m/s and energies 1,25,16J. The original ground ledger remains correct for its own frame. Do not change velocity’s reference while retaining 9,9,36J, nor infer conservation of a single cart’s momentum in every frame. Keep both labelled tables instead of overwriting their answers in one column.

## Troubleshoot squares, final-minus-initial and system boundaries

For −9J in B, check that the whole negative-three value is squared. For 18J in C, check accidental linear speed scaling. Zero two-cart energy may incorrectly cancel scalar energy with momentum signs. Zero A-to-B momentum change calls for signed final-minus-initial subtraction. Repair one row in a fault copy and return to the bookmark. Zoom small signs rather than use decorative motion-line length as evidence. Missing mass or frame remains unknown; familiar-looking carts do not supply it.

## Reopen with blind reversal and two-cart checks

Reopen without consulting the answer page. Assign A/B equal 9J and opposite momenta, then assign their two-cart system zero momentum and 18J. Return to P2 for C’s quadrupled energy, D’s equal momentum but different energy, and all three updated frame rows.
