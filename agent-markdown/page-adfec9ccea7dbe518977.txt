## Extend the midrib into a tendril, not a flower stalk

Run the midrib through the leaf and extend it beyond the tip into a thinner tendril curving to the pitcher attachment. Do not perforate the leaf centre or hang the pitcher from its lid. A modest bend suffices; if the reference coils, keep coherent occlusion rather than separate rings. Temporarily trace leaf–tip–tendril–pitcher with light arrows, checking continuity. ## Surround the opening with a rim and attach the lid behind

Add neither metal hinge nor tightly plugged stopper. Lid and rim are not two petals; subtle values distinguish lid surfaces. If the lid hides the entire opening, adjust view or pose rather than move the mouth onto the front wall. No invented internal liquid level is needed, and a dark opening does not mean a pitcher full of black fluid.
