If you track progress on Bangumi or MyAnimeList, ManYue's tracker synchronization can connect the corresponding records. The most important step is not enabling automation but binding the local comic to the correct remote entry. This article verifies one work before expanding the scope.

## Prepare an account and one clearly identified work

Confirm that you can sign in to the target platform and identify the comic's official entry there. Start with a work whose title, author, and edition relationship are clear rather than a spin-off, remake, or collection with complicated volume rules. ManYue publicly lists tracking features such as Bangumi and MyAnimeList under Ultra. You need both the platform account and the app entitlement separately.

Record progress on both sides, such as chapter ten in ManYue and chapter eight on the platform. This mainly concerns chapters and collection status. It does not mean uploading comic images, nor does it guarantee that the platform stores an exact page position. Define what you intend to synchronize before judging the result.

## Open tracker synchronization from Tools

Go to Tools → Tracker Sync and choose the login entry point on the Bangumi or MyAnimeList card. Follow the prompt to the platform's official authorization page, confirm your own correct account is signed in, authorize, and return to ManYue. Completing a browser step does not necessarily mean the app received the callback. Wait for the app to show a successful login state.

If authorization is canceled or the callback is incomplete, return to the tracker page and check its state before retrying. Do not paste passwords or access tokens into substitute pages from unknown tutorials. Platform login and source login are independent: access to a comic source does not mean Bangumi or MyAnimeList is authorized.

## Create a binding for one work

Add an item through Manage Bindings, choosing the local reading record and target platform. You may also enter search through a tracking action actually available in the work's details. Compare the remote results' title, author, work type, and chapter information before choosing. Do not confirm the first result merely because its cover looks similar.

Pay particular attention to manga versus anime, main stories versus spin-offs, and individual volumes versus collections. Platform organization may differ from the comic source, and total chapter counts may be incomplete. If no exact entry is found, stop binding and continue local reading. Do not choose an approximate work just to show a linked state.

## Decide carefully whether to import remote progress

The binding confirmation may offer Import Remote Progress, explaining that a farther-ahead remote position updates the local record. Compare the two numbers you noted before deciding whether to enable it. If you want to preserve an earlier position for a reread, do not let old remote completion progress overwrite your plan without reading the option.

After confirming the binding, return to the management list and check the relationship between the local work and remote entry. Open the comic's details again and inspect any expected progress change. A binding does more than save a title: it sets the target for future synchronization. This verification matters more than repeatedly correcting mistaken uploads afterward.

## Synchronize manually before enabling automation

Use Sync Now on the tracker page and wait for the success or failure summary. Then inspect that entry's progress and status on your platform page, confirming that another same-title work was not updated. One binding on the first attempt makes the result clear. When processing several, a success count does not replace individual verification.

After verification, enable synchronization and automatic synchronization as needed. Automatic updates may run when a reading session ends; follow the current description. Exit normally after a chapter, allow synchronization time to finish, then inspect the platform result. On network failure, retain the local record rather than arbitrarily marking all works completed to force a remote change.

## Stop expanding the impact when a binding is wrong

If you discover the wrong remote entry was selected, disable automatic synchronization or pause further operations before checking Manage Bindings. Removing an incorrect binding differs from deleting a platform account or reversing progress already written remotely. To correct an erroneous platform record, confirm the right work and use the platform's own process. Do not assume unbinding automatically rolls back every historical change.

When switching accounts, recheck existing bindings and authorization. Two people's identically named lists cannot be used interchangeably. Seeing binding names after restoring a device backup does not mean the new device has valid authorization. Check login status on the platform card and verify one sample before synchronizing everything.

## Frequently asked questions

**Why does the platform remain unchanged after binding succeeds?** Binding only establishes the relationship. Synchronization still needs the relevant switch, valid authorization, network access, and actual update conditions. Inspect the synchronization result and the correct platform entry rather than relying on a name appearing in the management list.

**Can comic images or exact page numbers synchronize?** Trackers handle work progress and collection status. Comic files stay in their original source or local storage. Platforms differ in progress models and chapter units, so a local page number cannot be equated directly with a remote chapter count.

**Should farther-ahead remote progress always be imported?** Not necessarily. You may be rereading or using a version with different chapter divisions. Compare what the chapters mean and your current purpose before adopting remote progress. A larger number is not automatically more appropriate.
