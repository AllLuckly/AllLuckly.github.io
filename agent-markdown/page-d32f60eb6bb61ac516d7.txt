A broad wall or corridor beside a character may draw attention, but visible open space remains onscreen. Play an authorised scene in iCouldPlay and manually record the figure, surrounding open regions and visual cues pointing beyond the image, separating description from expectation. Negative space here is compositional, not a black screen, missing picture or automatic subject detection.

## Choose an off-centre figure with visible surroundings

Choose an authorised scene of about 20–40 seconds with an off-centre figure and wall, corridor or sky. Record name, duration and source, then open through present iCouldPlay controls and play full-image at original speed. Confirm the open region belongs to the film, not a device border or loading blank. Optionally make a left-side toy/right-side open-table clip externally before import, labelled a plan until made. No scare or entrance is required; never invent a later object to satisfy an interpretation.

## Describe visible regions before atmosphere

On a first pass choose a stable moment and note figure side, open region and remaining objects. Open space may contain wall texture, doors, lights or distance while being less occupied relative to the subject. Do not begin with “danger on the right”. Rough fractions such as “right half relatively open” are visual estimates, not computed area. For continuous movement, choose a short anchored interval and describe change rather than assigning one ratio to the whole scene.

## Keep visible emptiness in the onscreen column

On a second pass split notes into visible onscreen and unseen offscreen. A visible corridor, empty chair or dark wall stays onscreen. A look beyond the right image boundary may cue right-side offscreen space. A dark doorway reveals neither its entire depth nor everything behind it; distinguish visible darkness and uncertain depth. Note occlusion behind a jamb separately from the right frame edge. This classifies current visibility, not an ability to reveal what existed beyond the camera frame.

## Log each visual cue and its direction

Log visible head/eye direction, pointing and entry/exit paths separately. If eyes are unclear, record rightward head orientation with eyeline unknown. For an exit, note the last visible action and approximate time without inventing a route beyond. Open space can attract attention but proves no hidden person. If a pan reveals previously offscreen space, update both time points; the revealed region is now onscreen. Sound is unnecessary for this visual task.

## Check whether later images support the expectation

Play from the open-space anchor to the segment end and log entrance, reframing, continued emptiness or clip ending. No entrance is valid evidence. Preserve actual entry direction even if attention was on the other side. Place personal expectation beside later result without generalising to every viewer. Limit conclusions to the viewed segment, not the entire film. If further context is needed, watch it and explicitly expand the recorded scope.

## Challenge the reading with a different composition

Find a more centred neighbouring shot or film a separate centred reference externally. Record changed subject size, direction and camera movement alongside open space; do not attribute a definite emotion solely to it. Without a suitable comparison, retain observations and an open question. Cropping the right half of the playback image is not another original composition. For a crop experiment, create and label a separate external copy while retaining the original. No iCouldPlay video editing or automatic negative-space removal is assumed.

## Troubleshoot borders and accidental scaling

If open space sits outside playback, distinguish device background, encoded bars and a dark scene wall. Replay full-image and inspect continuous door or floor lines; retain uncertainty rather than deriving plot from blackness. Hide controls when possible and replay. After a source change, check edition, cropped copy and display settings. Separate source boundaries, display range and overinterpretation instead of reinstalling the app or changing unrelated account settings over a reading disagreement.

## Reopen and deliver facts, expectation and result

Reopen the same file, verify identity and duration, then recover the open region by approximate time and action under full-image display. If resume differs, replay manually from earlier and log the actual result; sources need not share progress. Deliver three statements: visible open area and figure placement, specific offscreen cue or its absence, and subsequent support. Include viewed scope and uncertainty; without execution, deliver a checklist only. Success is retrievable separation of negative space, offscreen cues and expectation, not inevitable danger or automated film understanding.
