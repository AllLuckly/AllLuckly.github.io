A shift in clarity from a near object to a far subject may be an intentional focus transition. Observe regions in iCouldPlay without camera-lens control or a claim to refocus recorded media. Use self-made still-life or permitted focus-transition footage to describe visible evidence, not guarantee the camera settings can be recovered.

## Choose stable objects and viewpoint

Choose distinct near and far edges, such as a lined card in front and large lettering behind. Record a transition only with familiar camera controls, or use permitted existing footage. Avoid rapid subject and camera movement that entangles composition and clarity. Record identity and duration and preserve the recording original; do not first sharpen or recompress it for this check.

## Name two fixed observation regions

Name a particular foreground line crossing and a specific background letter, avoiding vague front/back labels. Prefer details trackable throughout without hand or caption occlusion. An object leaving the frame is unseen, not blurred. These are your references, not an app autofocus box, pixel sampler or sharpness score.

## Observe transition direction by playback

Play normally from before the near detail is clear until the far detail settles. On the first pass, log the order of near-clear/far-soft, both-soft or near-soft/far-clear states rather than rushing to pause at the transition. A seek landing is not the whole process. Treat a shot cut separately rather than calling differences across shots one continuous focus pull. Keep author explanations distinct from observations.

## Compare both details at three periods

At approximate before, during and after positions, play from earlier then pause. Record separable foreground lines and recognisable background letter edges. These are observations under current viewing conditions, not inferred focal length or aperture. Detail complexity differs, so legibility alone cannot rank the planes. Record both-soft states without inventing an automatic soft-focus app feature.

## Keep viewing mode fixed while checking scale

Establish a full-image baseline with orientation and approximate viewing distance. Log enlargement or rotation separately and retain both references. Do not compare the foreground before enlargement with the background after it. Scaling can reveal or enlarge blur, not recover absent source detail. Restore the baseline and replay to check whether the described change depends only on display size.

## Keep motion and focus as separate possibilities

Record directional smearing or whole-image motion rather than labelling every soft edge a focus pull. Compression, resolution and playback can affect detail too; one edited clip cannot exclude all causes. Revisit a relatively stationary period for the same object. With insufficient evidence, report apparent softness and an unresolved cause instead of a lens diagnosis or engine change replacing observation.

## Separate loading from a repeatable source effect

If an initial seek looks soft, play onward and revisit, noting whether softness recurs at the same content position or only during loading. Repeatability motivates source inspection but does not establish a camera cause. If a confirmed same-edition original differs, verify the actual playback version. Without originals, avoid declaring source damage or treating one clear moment as proof that a network issue is fixed.

## Exclude caption occlusion without modifying media

Temporarily disable selectable captions if they cover the background letter, then restore the language. Mark burned-in annotations as occlusion rather than blur. If visible subtitle-position options exist, try a clear position while recording the original, without promising movement for every format. Do not modify media or add enhancement for prettier results: that changes the object under observation.

## Save a repeatable clarity log

Keep two references, three periods, viewing conditions and replay results so others can revisit them. Limit conclusions to visible clarity changes and unresolved causes, without claiming measured depth of field, focal length or autofocus accuracy. Restore viewing state and preserve originals. Log any edited edition separately and do not reuse the old file coordinates for it. This is content reading in a player, not sharpening, restoration or device image-quality evaluation.
