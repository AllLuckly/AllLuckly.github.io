## 2. Split the original practice into nine characters

These are the explicitly listed single-code-point characters, not a definition of length for all Japanese text. Two あ occur at distinct positions and both count. ## 5. Distinguish Japanese full stops from English periods

Positions three and nine are 。, U+3002, while English . is U+002E. Similar appearance or placement does not make them the same character.
