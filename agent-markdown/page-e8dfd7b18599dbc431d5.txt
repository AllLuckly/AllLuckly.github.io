Sixteen bits specify a code count, not exact storage of every floating amplitude. ## Fix integer and normalization conventions

Restrict source data to signed 16-bit two’s-complement PCM, s=−32768…32767, normalized as x=s/32768. Zero is integer 0, unlike the midpoint code common in unsigned 8-bit PCM. Floating PCM or another normalization needs a separate case; a WAV extension alone is insufficient. Record encoding, valid bits, byte order, channels and sample rate externally while retaining source audio. No binary sample inspector is assumed in iCouldPlay, and artwork or volume bars cannot fill unknown fields.

## Count codes separately from adjacent intervals

There are65536 integer codes but65535 adjacent intervals. The normalized step q=1/32768≈0.000030517578125. The negative endpoint is−1; the positive endpoint is1−q, not an extra nonexistent 32768 code. Check max−min+1 for code count and max−min for intervals separately. This is a representation range, not sound pressure, device power or a hearing/dynamic-range measurement, and it requires no full-scale endpoint playback.

## Audit half-scale values, endpoints and little-endian bytes

Under the stipulated mapping,0.5 gives 16384=hex4000, little-endian 00 40.−0.5 gives−16384, two’s-complement C000, little-endian 00 C0. Maximum 32767 is FF 7F; minimum−32768 is00 80. The following ledger is not a complete WAV with headers and cannot be imported as audio text. Separate bytes and signed interpretation.00 80 is not zero because its first byte is zero, and C000 is not unconditionally positive 49152.

```text
defined signed16-bit two's-complement model: x=s/32768
s range: -32768..32767; codes=65536; intervals=65535
q=1/32768=0.000030517578125
     x                  s          little-endian hex bytes
  0.5                16384          00 40

 -1                 -32768          00 80
no-dither nearest model: Q(0.49*q)=0; Q(0.51*q)=q
clamped model: Q(+1)=32767/32768; Q(-1)=-1
```

## Test two small inputs under a stated rounding rule

Separately stipulate quantization as rounding 32768x to the nearest integer, positive half ties upward, then clamping to endpoints. Avoid ties with inputs 0.49q and0.51q: they become 0 andq. Nearby inputs can occupy different bins, and common output does not prove equal inputs. Record signed output-minus-input errors. Real tools may round or dither differently; without processing records this undithered model is not their observed algorithm, and tiny differences are not guaranteed audible.

## Distinguish clipping from ordinary quantization

Input+1 yields 32768 before clamping to32767, hence 1−q. Input−1 is already legal and remains−1. An out-of-range 1.25 cannot recover its peak shape by changing a filename’s bit count: multiple inputs collapse to one endpoint. Compare these on paper without raising device volume to maximum. Keep quantization, clipping, compression and later gain separate. Flat waveform tops require source/processing evidence; a player volume slider does not establish source clipping.
