## Identify the inner operation rather than starting leftmost

In (f∘g)(x), the small circle denotes composition here, g then f, not multiplication. Handwritten arrows are note-taking, not an assumed app function-flow control. ## Treat reversed order as a separate task

Create a separate g(f(2)) record: f(2)=2+3=5, then g(5)=2×5=10. Compare ten with seven while keeping input and rules fixed. Ten is not another notation for seven, and using both letters does not make operations identical. Symbolically, f(g(x))=2x+3 while g(f(x))=2(x+3)=2x+6. Doubling applies to the added three too. ## Compare pointwise multiplication on a separate line
