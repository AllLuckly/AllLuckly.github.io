A file's first audio track may be commentary or another language rather than primary dialogue, and labels can be incomplete. Build a content map for an authorised multitrack file, identifying sound before pairing captions. Track numbers are not universal language rules across files.

## Verify source audio resources

Inspect an original sample with multiple tracks in another tool and listen to each, recording language, labels and channels. Identify dialogue and commentary by actual sound because labels may be absent or incorrect.

Retain the original, edition and duration. Do not overwrite unique footage to add tracks or infer their count from container names. An MP4 may legitimately contain one track while an MKV has several; extensions do not dictate the menu count.

## Choose a recognisable spoken passage

Record a scene where a visible person speaks a clear sentence. Commentary can explain production over dialogue and translations may follow similar rhythm, but listen beyond these clues. Opening music or silence cannot reliably establish spoken language.

Choose middle and ending checks because special tracks may be intermittent. Describe actual contents rather than only a number. Content identities remain useful when track order changes or a server returns a processed version.

## Inspect the currently available track list

Use the audio controls actually present for this playback path. Some paths may not report a complete list; record visible options and source instead of promising a setting restores missing tracks. Compare source-tool results to distinguish incomplete reporting from a genuinely single-track file.

Without switching controls, establish basic playback and compare the same file locally. Do not assume universal manual-engine buttons or rename extensions to repair a list. Record original versus visible counts, device and path precisely.

## Identify each selection at the same scene

After each switch, replay the fixed passage and listen for language, commentary and normal output. Allow actual response before another choice so selection remains clear. Language labels still need listening verification.

For silence, check output, quiet intervals and volume against another track. Codec and path conditions can differ; silence does not prove an absent track. Separate unavailable sound from playable content with an inaccurate label for actionable diagnosis.

## Pair captions independently

After choosing dialogue, verify matching captions at beginning, middle and end. Commentary and ordinary captions can legitimately discuss different things; delay cannot reconcile unrelated text. Disable irrelevant captions or use an authorised counterpart for commentary viewing.

For language study, identify the spoken language before assigning original and translated lines. Verify actual sentences beyond filenames and labels. Baked-in words do not disappear with audio switching and should be investigated separately from selectable captions.

## Recheck after reopening or changing source

Reopen the same entry and listen to the reference sentence, observing retained selection rather than assuming per-file or per-source memory. Record any wider setting effects and restore them where necessary so an experiment does not cause later unexpected silence.

For NAS versus server playback, verify original versus processed versions. Counts and numbering can change, so local track two need not equal remote track two. Establish the new list-to-content mapping before attributing a server's omitted audio to player choice.

## Keep a brief sound inventory

Record file, edition, source, visible tracks, observed contents and paired captions. Do not infer complete surround performance from a label. Channel or spatial capability needs separate device testing; this task establishes language and content identity.

Completion means explaining the chosen content, its caption counterpart and a repeatable reopening check. Retain originals and observations for unavailable paths and use a verified option meanwhile. Clear content mapping survives version changes better than remembered track numbers.
