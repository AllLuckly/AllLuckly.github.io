Keep one owned video and audio track fixed while switching between device speaker and paired Bluetooth headphones, checking system destination and actual sound separately.

## Prepare one clip and paired headphones

Choose an authorized short video with recognizable dialogue or a clap; note name, duration and a cue where image and sound coincide. Use your own already paired Bluetooth headphones at a low suitable volume. Apple documents choosing system playback destinations from Control Center or the Lock Screen. iCouldPlay publicly describes video and audio tracks, but does not promise an in-app Bluetooth chooser. Without the accessory, complete only the device-speaker baseline.

## Fix the video and current audio track

Open the clip from an existing iCouldPlay source, verify the image and file identity, then record the active audio track using the player’s actual controls. If the file has one track or no list is visible, record that. Keep track, subtitles, engine and source fixed. Play briefly through the device speaker at a suitable volume, recording image, playhead and recognizable dialogue at the cue. Video playback and an audible selected track are separate observations.

## Confirm the accessory connection in the system

Follow the actual accessory and system instructions to confirm Bluetooth connection, checking the complete device name rather than selecting a similarly named nearby accessory. Connection does not itself establish the current audio destination; record where the system says audio is routed. If connection fails, check power, range and connected device, and leave the round pending if unresolved. Do not unpair unrelated devices or describe a system control as an iCouldPlay-specific button.

## Select the headphones and verify video audio

Keep the same clip and track. Use the system playback-destination control actually available in Control Center or on the Lock Screen to select your headphones. Check the system destination, then observe picture, playhead and headphone sound near the same cue. If the cue has passed, seek a few seconds before it and record the manual jump. A selected destination, playing indication and expected audible dialogue are separate results.

## Compare the two outputs without changing tracks

At the same dialogue or clap, record whether the expected sound is heard through the device speaker and headphones. Loudness alone does not identify the selected track. If the video has different tracks, confirm selection before testing one; do not change track and destination simultaneously. Without measurement equipment, describe only audible sound, noticeable lag or interruption, not millisecond latency, codec or quality improvement. Record independent volume changes. For a clip with multiple dialogue languages, use a clearly identifiable passage and record its identity. Missing or identical track labels cannot be resolved merely by list order. Use the same track in both observations; if the selection is unclear, return to the original sample and verify it before attributing a difference to Bluetooth.

## Return to the device and recheck picture and sound

Choose the iPhone or iPad itself from the system destinations actually available, verify the displayed target and listen at a suitable volume. Return to iCouldPlay to inspect clip, track and position. Record whether playback paused or continued rather than assuming automatic recovery. Keep the accessory in range; deliberate disconnection or unpairing is unnecessary. If the device option is absent or routing is unclear, preserve feedback and stop the round. Do not move or rename the media.

## Troubleshoot file, track and system destination separately

If neither output has sound, inspect the file’s baseline, active track and player mute state. If only headphones are silent, separately inspect Bluetooth connection, system destination and volume. For an image/sound mismatch, compare the same source on the device first; one observation does not establish lag for all Bluetooth devices or files. Change one condition per trial and retain the previous state. Mark unknown track or destination pending instead of reinstalling or deleting the library.

## Deliver three observations and restore

Keep rows for device baseline, headphones and return to device: video and track identity, system destination, image time, audible result and pending work. Restore the original destination, volume and viewing position, retaining the file and pairing. Apple documents Bluetooth audio routing and the app listing describes media and tracks; together they do not establish compatibility with all hardware, codecs or multichannel outputs. No physical listening or server test is claimed.
