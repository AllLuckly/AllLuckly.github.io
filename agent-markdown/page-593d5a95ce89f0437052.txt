A binder clip is not three loosely stacked symbols: a black body and two silver handles. ## Establish body side and jaw direction

Use an approximately 1700×1400 px landscape canvas with pale cream-grey background, jaws lower right. An oblique view exposes a near-triangular side and body width. Establish the folded-sheet envelope in grey-blue values rather than pure black. Separate sketch, body, far/near handles, paper and shadow. The side apex is a fold; lower edges approach the jaws, not a solid pyramid. Expose opening and thin edges if stone-like, or width and side if icon-flat. Retain one viewpoint rather than twisting a side to expose a handle.

## Retain a paper entry between jaw lips

Separate two thin jaw lips and insert a very thin warm-white paper band from the right, then suggest two or three sheets with pale edges. Avoid heavy plier-like jaws, paper through the triangular side or an unexplained gap at entry. Repair lip occlusion if paper is pasted outside; thin sheets and lines if plank-like. This is an already-positioned still life without hands squeezing handles. Sheet count does not establish real model capacity, which is outside this study.

## Give each handle its own attachment pair

Place a two-ended U handle near the edges of each opposing broad face. Label near/far and do not connect one near endpoint to the far handle. Trace each continuous U between its own ends, with background inside. Keep concealed attachments in the sketch only. Two aerials suggest missing endpoints; four rings need regrouping into two handles. Suggest ends turning into the body’s rolled edge without invented welds or screws.

## Keep posed handles attached to their own faces

Let the near handle rise and the far one angle back for depth, retaining their original face attachments. Do not join both tops to one body-fold point for composition. Hide body to inspect complete U shapes, then restore occlusion. Separate a merged central shackle or reduce implausible butterfly-like twisting. These are pose choices, not standard product angles, and do not establish removability, range of motion or correct real use.

## Retain readable turns in the black body

Use deep grey-blue on the upper-left lit body, darker shade and sparse narrow fold highlights rather than pure black everywhere. Give wire mid-grey with broken grey-white reflections, distinct but not uniformly white. Hide reflections to test side and jaws, then restore without crossing paper. Separate body-face values if hole-like; emphasise thin folds rather than rounded plastic highlights. Omit scratches instead of using them to explain wrong joins. Rendering does not establish steel grade, coating process or real thickness.

## Keep paper and ground shadow distinct from jaws

Keep broad warm-white paper with only two or three grey layer edges. Separate short jaw-to-paper contact from soft lower-right ground shadow rather than joining them into a black strip. Expose lips and lighten a deep-hole mouth, or reconnect paper to the table if floating. Simplify handle shadows so two extra crisp U shapes do not suggest four handles. Count real handles with shadow hidden, then restore. Keep other stationery from concealing difficult roots; repair unclear connections structurally.

## Audit two handles and four endpoints

Keep near/far labels and four attachments, tracing each handle end to end and checking body faces and lips. Correct endpoint count does not excuse the wrong face. Adjust one end or occlusion with paper and light fixed. Repose crowded handles or separate contrast rather than deleting one while claiming two. A folded-down version needs new surface occlusion, not a compressed raised image retaining old values. Diagnostic labels help repair but cannot replace readable final connections.

## Inspect thin body edges and open handles in export

Save the layered original and inspect jaws, near attachment and far-handle opening in a PNG at use size. Reduce glare and unnecessary crossings if wire becomes a white lump rather than outlining everything. Separate side values locally if the black body vanishes. Record a specific end error such as the far-right attachment joining the near lip. Judge folded body, two U handles and coherent paper occlusion, not clamping force or operating state. Manual layer/preview checks are not spring or load tests; a new view needs revised endpoints and planes.
