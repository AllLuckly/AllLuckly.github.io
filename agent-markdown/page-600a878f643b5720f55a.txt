## Define the node and one common quantity unit

Create a 1200 by 800 pixel canvas with input 12 from left into a central node, output 5 upper right and 7 lower right. Record 12=5+7 in one fictional unit, not mixed counts/percentages. The node is a split location, not a third output or extra consumption. Plan with thin centre references and separate output-label space. ## Establish the unit width on straight sections

Prototype three horizontal rectangles and verify 120=50+70. Their lengths may differ for layout and do not encode quantity; state that in the legend.
