Without purchasing again, check the current Apple account, existing entitlement, actual restore entry and feedback.

## Preserve current local content

On your own device open a local comic and record its chapter and a nonprivate bookmark or reading record. Confirm a separate original-file backup exists. Restoring a purchase concerns an existing transaction entitlement; it is not the same as restoring comics, reading data or server files. Preserve local content and sync information separately, and do not uninstall the app as a guess.

## Distinguish subscriptions and one-time purchases

ManYue’s public listing distinguishes recurring Pro and Ultra subscriptions from a one-time Ultra lifetime purchase; actual offerings and prices vary by region and purchase page. Check your own purchase type, whether it remains active and the account used. Canceled renewal, an expired subscription and a lifetime purchase are distinct. One historical transaction does not establish every entitlement today. This exercise requires no new purchase or purchase confirmation.

## Check the current media and purchases account

Use Apple’s account and purchase-management interface to check whether the current media-and-purchases account matches the original purchaser. Record only a redacted label such as A or B; do not provide full email, passwords, codes or transaction screenshots in feedback. If the account is unclear, consult your own purchase history or Apple support. Do not sign into another person’s account or change a password for this exercise.

## Inspect the app’s current display

Return to the installed ManYue version and inspect the actual membership or entitlement page. Record device, app version, displayed tier and network state. Expired, unrecognized, loading and unavailable entries are distinct; a page that has not refreshed is not proof of a lost transaction. Confirm your authorized local comic remains accessible. Do not test membership-dependent sync or server access by deleting data.

## Use only an actual restore entry

Apple support describes ways to restore in-app purchases. If the current interface offers a clear Restore Purchases or equivalent entry, read the prompt and use it once, recording actual feedback and time. If absent or it leads to a new purchase confirmation, stop and consult official guidance; do not treat paying again as restoration. Do not provide credentials to third parties or assume one tap must succeed.

## Verify entitlement and purchase status separately

Afterward recheck the displayed app tier, system purchase history and the specific feature needed as separate observations. A sync entry appearing does not prove remote data synced, and a server list does not prove the comic is offline. If the restore reports success but the tier remains unexpected, record app version, redacted account label, purchase type and message. Seek Apple or app support as appropriate without publishing transaction evidence.

## Use a state card rather than treating history as an active entitlement

Privately make columns for account label, purchase type, system-displayed state and app-displayed state. For example, account A may show a subscription with renewal canceled but time remaining in the paid period while the app displays the basic tier. That discrepancy needs investigation; it is not proof of expiry or successful restoration. Give lifetime purchases, historical subscriptions and current subscriptions separate rows, and do not combine transactions from different accounts. The account holder should complete any normal sign-in or verification; never preserve codes in a report. Mark absent expiry or purchase-type information unknown, and redact order numbers and account details from personal screenshots.

## Check one matching entitlement after restoration

Choose one feature included in the purchased tier according to the public description. Record its pre-restore state and then observe the interface afterward without changing remote data. If a Pro sync entry appears, record only that it is visible; proving sync requires a safe sample and separate outcome, not a page label. A restore entry cannot itself extend an expired period; use the current system state and store rules. For support prepare redacted device system, app version, purchase type, time and original error summary without posting a full receipt publicly. Keep current data and the state card if unresolved.

## Record whether an entry was available

If no restore entry was found, record the actual pages checked and the app version. Apple lists common locations but does not guarantee every app uses the same one. Keep local comic samples and independent backups while awaiting support, avoid duplicate purchases and do not borrow another person’s account to test.

## Distinguish failure causes and retain originals

For a network error, record the time and message before checking connectivity. If the system indicates another account or an expired subscription, examine the relevant information for your own case. Change one confirmed condition at a time; do not repeatedly switch accounts, reinstall or erase data. Finally confirm original comics, backups and reading records remain. State whether restoration actually occurred and which entitlements remain untested. No payment, purchase restore or physical-device test is claimed.
