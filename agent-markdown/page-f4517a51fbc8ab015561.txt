The same local recording in 调调 may be heard through different outputs provided by the device, but whether connecting wired headphones switches the route or disconnecting them pauses playback must be observed on your own phone and accessories. Apple documents USB-C wired audio on compatible iPhones. That does not guarantee every third-party adapter or headphone, or establish audio quality or output parameters.

## Confirm equipment and a safe setting

Prepare a compatible USB-C iPhone and compatible USB-C wired headphones, or an appropriate USB-C to 3.5 mm adapter and headphones. Apple lists its USB-C EarPods and USB-C to 3.5 mm adapter as examples; check other products’ own documentation, since matching plug shapes do not prove electrical compatibility. If you lack equipment, mark the exercise unavailable; there is no need to buy it.

Choose a short recording you own or may play, and work somewhere quiet and safe, not while driving or needing environmental warnings. Lower the volume before testing and listen at a comfortable level. Avoid exposing private audio in public. Check the cable and connector for damage or dirt, do not use damaged accessories and do not insert metal tools into ports. Record device, system and 调调 versions and accessory models, not serial numbers.

## Establish a phone-speaker baseline

With headphones disconnected, select the same local recording in 调调 and note title, progress and playback control. Verify that sound is actually audible from the phone speaker. If routed elsewhere, select the phone output using your normal controls or pause the comparison and record the condition. Do not delete Bluetooth pairings or turn off another person’s audio device.

Prepare a table for action, app display, actual sound destination, progress and notes. Pause and play once to establish that the file itself works. If the baseline is silent, check file, volume, output and relevant mute settings before attributing later behavior to the headphones. Keep the track and queue; the library need not be cleared.

## Observe the connection separately

Connect the headphones as the accessory instructions direct. With an adapter, connect the headphone plug to it first, then gently connect the compatible adapter to the phone. Do not force the plug. Observe whether 调调 retains the track and playback state; at a comfortable volume check whether the headphones produce sound and separately whether the speaker still does. Do not leave headphones playing loudly on a table to judge loudness, or assume that a connected plug guarantees audio routing.

If playback continues automatically, record position and sound. If paused, use the visible play control to resume manually and note that action. Position comparison is approximate; manual handling cannot measure precise latency. Plug and unplug behavior may vary, so no automatic switch, pause or resume is assumed and repeated reconnection is unnecessary.

## Disconnect safely and check return

Lower volume and pause first. Ensure that possible speaker playback is appropriate for the surroundings, then hold the connector rather than pulling the cable and disconnect according to its instructions. Observe title, control and output. If appropriate, manually play at an acceptable level and verify the actual source. Record “no sound heard”, “paused” and “output unclear” as distinct outcomes.

If speaker playback is unwanted, stay paused and mark that sound test unfinished. Keep the same recording and starting volume condition; phones may remember different volumes by output, so record what is visible without claiming equal loudness. Afterwards restore the user’s preferred headphones, queue and volume.

## Troubleshoot silence one condition at a time

If headphones are silent, confirm compatible ports and normal connection, then check that 调调 is playing and volume is comfortable. If you have a second authorized short recording, change only the file for a comparison. If other known-good system audio is silent too, follow Apple and accessory documentation for connection checks. Retain observations; do not assume the cause is 调调.

Adapters and headphones can differ in power, interface and supported devices. If compatibility is uncertain, consult their documentation or stop rather than raising volume, modifying connectors or forcing repeated insertions. Do not reset the system, delete the library or declare a model unsupported from one failed attempt. Distinguish a visible playing state from audible sound and mark unfinished steps.

## Save the checklist

Save rows for speaker baseline, headphones connected and disconnected, each with actions, app state and audible result. Reopen the note, ensure the original recording remains, and do not share private audio or device identifiers. This exercise is designed from public documentation and does not claim a physical-device or headphone test. One result applies only to that device and accessory combination; it cannot establish bit-perfect output, sample rate or compatibility with all headphones.

When changing a cable or headphones later, create a separate row identifying the changed accessory and repeat from the speaker baseline. An old record cannot replace a new connection and sound check. Disconnect and stop if an interface overheats or an accessory is damaged.
