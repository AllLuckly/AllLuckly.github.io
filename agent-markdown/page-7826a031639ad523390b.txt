To put a recording you made into 调调, export it from iPhone Voice Memos to Files, then select that copy for import. ## Prepare a short nonprivate recording

In a suitable quiet place, open Voice Memos and record a few seconds of your own practice cue, such as “first section, end.” Do not record another person’s conversation, a private meeting or protected material. Stop and listen in Voice Memos, noting name, duration and a recognizable beginning or ending. Preserve the original system recording. If the system cannot record due to settings or device conditions, stop and record the incomplete step. Do not change microphone permissions you do not understand or substitute someone else’s audio.

Keep the sample short and identifiable; high volume and special equipment are unnecessary. Use a comfortable playback volume and avoid disturbing others with unexpected audio. Later use sound, duration and source together to identify the result; a matching title alone is insufficient.

## Inspect the available system export path

Apple’s Voice Memos guide says a recording can be saved to Files via Share and is exported as M4A by default; some systems also offer an editable sharing option. Open the actual recording’s More or Share interface and confirm it is the prepared sample. Labels can differ by system. If Save to Files is not offered, record the limitation rather than inventing a fixed button.

Choose the ordinary audio export actually offered; the editable share option is not required. Apple says default M4A may flatten layered recordings and convert Spatial Audio to stereo. If the source used those system recording features, the exported copy’s structure may differ. This practice uses an ordinary single recording and checks audible content, not preservation of editable Voice Memos layers or effects in 调调.

## Save an identifiable working copy

In the share sheet select the available Save to Files action and a folder you may use, preferably an accessible local practice location. Give the copy a recognizable name and check its extension without overwriting a same-name file. Use an empty folder or different name on conflict; do not delete the Voice Memos original. Wait for actual completion, then inspect location, name and displayed size in Files.

调调 publicly lists M4A among supported formats and a 512 MB single-file limit, but readability depends on the audio track and system. If the actual export format differs, record its extension and information; simply renaming a suffix is not conversion. For insufficient space, unwritable folders or export failure, preserve the error and avoid repeated overwriting or clearing unrelated files.

## Listen to the copy on the system side

If Files offers a preview or an existing trusted player supports the format, open the exported working copy, listen for the cue and compare its duration with the original Voice Memos record. If preview is unavailable, record “not listened on system side,” not that the file is necessarily corrupt. Use another compatible tool only if appropriate. Hearing one segment does not establish byte-for-byte verification.

Record the different locations of original and copy. They must be identified separately after sharing or copying. A filename does not establish preservation of editable layers, and an existing Voice Memos item does not prove that the Files copy saved completely. Preserve the original and backups throughout.

## Import the copy into 调调

Open 调调 and use its actual file-import entry to find the saved working copy. Confirm location and name, then wait for visible feedback. If the entry is absent or format rejected, mark the step incomplete rather than inventing a converter. If the content is reported as duplicate, inspect the existing local-library item first. Do not delete other items or change audio merely to force a second copy.

Find the item in the local library and compare visible title, duration and audible cue. Audio metadata and filename may differ, so use multiple clues. A short listen covers only the portion heard; do not report the entire recording verified unless it was. A system preview is not proof that 调调 can play it.

## Troubleshoot mismatches by layer

If Voice Memos works but no Files copy exists, check share completion, destination and space. If a copy exists but content differs, check selection of same-name recordings and export options. If the Files copy works but 调调 import fails, separately record format, size, system readability and app feedback. Version, encoding and system conditions may matter. Change one understood condition per retry instead of changing file and permission together and claiming a cause.

There is no need to reinstall 调调, clear recordings or disable system protections. For support provide format, size, versions and visible errors without private audio. Do not send a recording involving others without consent. The user may complete this practice entirely locally.

The user may retain or remove the practice copy, but only after confirming the original and needed backups are safe; keep it when uncertain. Restore prior volume and playback arrangement.
