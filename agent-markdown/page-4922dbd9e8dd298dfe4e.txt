Draw a slide trombone from slightly in front of the side, joining two long tubes through a clear U bend and distinguishing them from the rear bell section. Focus on parallelism, telescoping and gaps instead of merging metal into one gold mass. Use a simplified tenor without an attachment valve, without calculating pitch or actual slide positions.

## Establish axes and the slide envelope

Use an approximately 2200×1400 px landscape canvas, reserving the right for the long slide and left for mouthpiece and bell. Sketch two parallel tube axes joined by a broad U guide at the front, then arrange the remaining silhouette behind. Do not let an oversized bell reduce the slide to a short handle. Separate sketch, tubing, braces and tones, then save native work. Dimensions are compositional, not physical or acoustic. Exclude an F attachment, rotary valve and trigger throughout this chosen exterior instead of adding valve blocks to fill space.

## Join two tubes with a U bend

Build outer slide tubes of similar diameter along both axes, turning continuously through the front U bend. Retain the long narrow background gap between them. Give the bend inner and outer edges and consistent thickness, not a fine line joining two blunt ends. Add no third long parallel tube that blocks the gap like a fork. Trace the return path in monochrome before reflections; curved highlights must not turn the U into a square corner. Where edges overlap, preserve the interior gap rather than expose every edge artificially.

## Show where inner and outer slides overlap

Expose short thinner inner-slide portions near the player, with outer tubes overlapping and extending forward. Mark junctions with narrow rings or slight diameter changes. Both extend in one direction, not oppositely. Clarify exposed portions without implying a cutaway or transparent wall; conceal interior lines behind metal. Repair continuous silhouettes before rings if joints look broken, and restore readable thickness where a tube vanishes. Keep one stationary extension rather than overlay an unexplained second slide position.

## Connect braces without filling the slide gap

Place a short brace between the tubes near the rear according to the official reference, rather than an indefinitely extended line. Differentiate slide grip and rear connections through tone without welding every crossing into one mass. A brace spans the gap while the front U remains continuous; do not fill nearby space with invented screws. Keep annotation leaders visibly distinct from physical components. No hand is drawn here, so tubing and supports must remain understandable without a grip concealing their connections.

## Give the bell its own gradually flaring tube

Extend the returning rear tube gradually into the bell instead of attaching a disc to the slide’s front. Reveal a little interior, with firm near rim, narrower far rim and tonal depth. Put a small cup-like mouthpiece at its proper entry, not a miniature bell on an arbitrary end. Temporarily colour slide and bell sections differently for checking, then restore metal tones; colours do not imply two independent air streams. Do not add invented airflow arrows to prove an internal path that the exterior drawing does not fully show.

## Keep parallel tubes readable through reflections

Use warm grey-gold, cooler brown shadows and a few pale-yellow/near-white accents. Give each slide tube its own tonal bands while preserving background between them instead of bridging the gap with gold. Bell reflections may be broader, but deepest tones stay near seams and cavity rather than outlining every tube black. Taper highlights around bends and stop brace highlights at its edges. If the fitted image becomes a gold rectangle, restore the two-tube negative space and rounded front before adding tiny screws.

## Inspect paths and gaps without inferring slide positions

Hide highlights and trace one outer tube through the U to the other without breaks. Check both overlaps extend alike and every brace has real endpoints. Remove an extra middle line if the bend resembles a third tube. Revisit the temporary section colours if the bell has attached to the slide front. Preserve a failing copy and fix one segment without dragging whole layers out of parallel. Screen extension specifies a pose, not a note or slide-position number; sound also depends on playing conditions and is not simulated here.

## Reopen and check bend, rim and overlaps

Save native work, hide check colours and axes, then reopen a PNG export. Read slide gap and bell at fit-to-window size; inspect U thickness, brace endpoints and overlap rings enlarged. Subdue far detail if crowded without deleting the main return path. Reopen editable slide and rear-reflection layers separately. Document no attachment valve, one static pose and omitted internals. Leave unperformed export/reopening pending; review concerns hand-drawn connections, not mechanical measurements, device acoustics or automatic trombone generation.
