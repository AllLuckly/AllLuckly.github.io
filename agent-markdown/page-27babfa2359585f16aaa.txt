## List explicitly narrated actions first

The invented exercise has a fox promise to leave the last lamp for a companion, then take it away after dark; the companion stops by the door in darkness. It ends without commentary and belongs to neither Aesop nor another real author. List promise, removal and stopping as separate events. Invent no later fall or punishment. Separate stated willingness from action: saying the lamp will remain does not mean it remained. Species identity is distinct from event evidence, and interpretations must refer to these three items.

## Keep humanlike animals distinct from zoology

The promise and waiting are arrangements for these fictional characters, not evidence that all real foxes are untrustworthy. Zoological sources on nocturnal activity, sight or feeding address another subject; keep them separate rather than supplying unstated motives.
