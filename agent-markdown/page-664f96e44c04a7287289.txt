## Check devices and reversible conditions

Use two devices you are authorized to use that meet Apple’s Universal Clipboard requirements, such as an iPhone and Mac. Apple says they must be near one another, signed in to the same Apple Account, with Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and Handoff enabled; devices and system versions must meet compatibility requirements. If the account cannot be confirmed or changing network settings is inappropriate, stop and record the missing condition. Do not switch accounts or disable security features. Workplace management policies may restrict Continuity or clipboard use; follow your organization’s rules.

Copying will replace the current clipboard, and its previous contents may not be recoverable. If it contains work or something you still need, preserve it using your normal safe process or postpone the exercise. Do not use passwords, verification codes, private notes or someone else’s information. ## Select the entire range and copy

Use the device’s available selection controls to select `Tea 09:30`. Check that the selection includes the first letter, final digit and intervening space without adjacent text. Use the copy command actually shown by the system or host. Leave the source intact: do not cut it or copy something else immediately afterward, which may replace the clipboard. Apple says Universal Clipboard contents remain briefly or until another copy on either device replaces them, so proceed promptly.

If the system requests permission, follow the prompt and your privacy choice. If denied, record that permission was not granted; do not bypass the restriction. Seeing a copy menu or invoking copy does not prove the other device received the same text, so the paste check remains necessary.

## Paste into the destination draft and compare

Wait for actual feedback and check that the result is `Tea 09:30`, including case, one space, the colon and final two digits. If the system prompts, choose whether to allow it. Paste once and inspect before repeating. If nothing, stale text or multiple copies appear, record the actual result without overwriting the source. ## Troubleshoot connection and host restrictions

First confirm source text and selection, whether another copy occurred, and that the destination focus is editable. Then check proximity, the same Apple Account, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and Handoff on both devices. Apple also notes that a VPN configuration may affect local networking. Do not sign out, alter enterprise management or disable a VPN without understanding the consequences. If a condition cannot be safely adjusted, leave it pending and stop.

To isolate a host issue, recopy the same source and paste into another ordinary local draft on the destination, recording this as a separate round. If a host blocks paste, permission is denied or system conditions are unmet, record the visible feedback. Deliver the source and destination drafts with a position-by-position match status.
