Downloading an entire collection before a trip can exhaust storage and make verification cumbersome. Estimate space from the books you actually plan to read, measure a few representative downloads, and work in batches. The estimate applies to your files and device rather than a universal compression ratio.

## List the books you realistically expect to read

List anticipated reading situations and choose essential, backup and optional books. Record chapter coverage and edition, and confirm authorised download or import availability. A short chapter and a large volume are different reading commitments. Priorities make it possible to reduce the plan deliberately if space is limited rather than guessing among cover images later.

## Record current available storage

Check available storage in system settings and note other large ongoing tasks such as photo sync or updates. Their effects should not all be attributed to ManYue. Reserve space for ordinary device use according to your needs rather than applying one universal number. Never remove unique originals just to enlarge the comic allowance; understand deletion scope first.

## Choose representative books for measurement

Choose representative monochrome and colour or large-image material, or a short and long book from your single content type. A six-page reference is useful for importing tests but not representative of an art collection’s storage. Record source size, page or chapter count and acquisition method. Server download packaging can differ from the source archive.

## Finish one sample before measuring again

Complete one download or import and inspect its local beginning, middle and end before measuring again. Record the approximate change using available system or app storage information. Statistics can lag and temporary files can affect the reading, so avoid cache wipes or reinstalls in pursuit of artificial precision. Repeat with the second sample under comparable conditions.

## Estimate similar material and leave a buffer

Use sample scale and page count to estimate similar books, totalling essentials and backups separately. Image dimensions, colour and encoding can vary widely, so avoid a single average for a mixed collection. Leave room for temporary work, cache and plan changes. Reduce optional material before backups when the estimate exceeds the available allowance, and revise it after each batch.

## Download in verifiable batches

Finish a small essential batch, verify each local copy and check remaining space before adding more. A large overall progress bar can hide individual failures. Inspect final pages and chapter boundaries. Record a failed book’s space and network conditions before retrying, while keeping successful copies intact. A small pending/completed/offline-checked list prevents duplicate work.

## Recheck storage and offline access before leaving

Disconnect and sample beginning, middle and end for each planned local book. Confirm that all essentials are included and the reserved space remains. Avoid last-minute cleanup that invalidates your offline checks. After the trip, reclaim copies according to backup availability and current deletion prompts, preserving important records. Storage planning should complement the backup plan.

## Questions and completion criteria

Why can device usage exceed archive size? Packaging, extraction, cache and measurement timing can contribute. Does sufficient space prove offline readiness? Files must still be complete and readable disconnected. Finish with prioritised reading, representative measurements, verified batches and a reserved buffer. The estimate needs to guide sensible decisions rather than predict every byte.
