Create a scoped personal reading list for an anthology, verify chapter navigation and references, and keep selected-piece progress separate from whole-book completion.

English translation of the [Chinese original](<https://allluckly.cn/guides/nasbook-anthology-selective-reading/>), translated on 2026-10-27. Original publication dates, attribution, code and version notes are retained.

An anthology can support a complete selective-reading task without cover-to-cover reading. A late position does not establish whole-book completion. Use contents, bookmarks and a short external list to record chosen pieces and reasons without assuming automatic per-piece completion or curriculum generation.

## Define the selection and its boundaries

Use a readable anthology with a sound contents list. Define a topic and choose a few genuinely independent pieces, recording edition and titles. Treat unread title matches as candidates rather than proof of relevance. State excluded scope so the result is not presented as a whole-book judgement.

## Verify each contents entry

Open and verify several entries against body titles and opening paragraphs. Record suitable chapter/phrase or file/printed-page references. For image-only contents, map pages manually. Establish starts and endings before reading so notes cannot silently belong to neighbouring pieces.

## Choose an order serving the question

Arrange a personal order by purpose and note a reason for each piece. Keep the original contents and files unchanged. Add necessary background or replace an unsuitable candidate. Your sequence serves this task rather than redefining the author’s anthology structure.

## Keep a verifiable record for each piece

Write a short topic response, evidence location and uncertainty for each piece. Test a few anchors, distinguishing quotation, narrator and interpretation. Use external page notes for nonselectable content. Keep piece titles alongside the book title so later evidence remains attributable.

## Separate the selection from whole-book status

Mark completed pieces in your list and keep the whole-book state truthful. A late position is only a location. Use actual controls, with an external piece list if no per-piece status exists. A completed selection can coexist with an unfinished book without manipulating statistics.

## Check the thematic conclusion against a counterexample

Compare similarities and differences and revisit a counterexample. Narrow conclusions to the evidence and retain unread scope. Read surrounding passages rather than assembling detached highlights. Label the output as a dated selective-reading observation with piece titles and edition, ready for revision after further reading.

## Reuse anchors with a new scope

For a new question, date a new scope that may reuse old pieces and add others. Verify anchors after edition changes. Preserve notes, selection lists and original files separately, reopening outputs for attribution. Multiple dated selections remain understandable without reconstructing them from percentages.

## Acceptance and common misunderstandings

Finish with working starts, clear reasons, truthful read scope and supported conclusions. Do not infer automatic completion of skipped chapters. A partial review is valid when scoped honestly. Repair incorrect navigation before using its notes as evidence; deliberate selection need not follow every page in order.
