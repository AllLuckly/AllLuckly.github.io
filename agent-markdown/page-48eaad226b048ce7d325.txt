The editorial office replied may use an institutional name for those handling the response; it does not imply that a room grew a mouth. Read a short passage in Book Isle, identifying the referent and evidence of association before discussing effect. Manual notes are not automatic rhetoric detection, and a rhetorical label does not establish that every member agreed.

## Choose a passage containing the name and action

Record the book, edition, passage and speaker. Use an original example: three days after the manuscript was sent, the editorial office replied that captions could be supplied; the message was signed by Lin, the editor on duty. This gives one response and one signature, not a vote, meeting or everyone’s opinion. Mark the institution, action and named person without rewriting it as all editors decided. If a real passage contains only an isolated noun, read forward and back for its predicate and object before classifying it mechanically.

## Test whether the literal object can perform the action

If editorial office means only the room and furniture, those objects cannot compose a reply; an institution and its staff fit the signature. This supports a candidate institutional reference to the relevant people or action without specifying how many participated. In a literal control, the editorial office’s window was open, the predicate concerns the room’s fittings. Do not classify that sentence the same way merely because the name repeats. Identical wording can refer at different levels; classification rests on its role in this sentence, not a permanent property of the word.

## State the association instead of only naming the figure

Here the institution is associated with its acting staff, and the institutional name carries the reply. Both institutional context and signature support that reading. Do not rewrite it as Lin resembles an editorial office, which introduces similarity absent from the source. In another original example, she drank a cup and wanted a refill, drinking and refilling support the contents rather than swallowing the container. The cup shattered can refer literally to the container. Record name, candidate referent, association and evidence for each example, rather than writing only metonymy.

## Do not expand institutional reference into unanimity

The phrase can function as an institutional reply without proving that every editor read the manuscript, agreed, or gave Lin final acceptance authority. Permission to supply captions is not acceptance of the whole work or a promise that any future material may be added. Separate the message’s content, signatory and stated authority, leaving omissions unknown. This checks inference boundaries rather than casting suspicion on a real institution. The fictional example is not submission advice; in an actual book, rely on its wording and later evidence.

## Distinguish similarity and part–whole readings

As a contrast, the editor is like a bridge connecting author and reader uses comparable function to explain a role. Its emphasis is similarity, not a literal bridge taking part in correspondence, so keep it separate from institution-for-actor reference. For hand standing for a person, different traditions may classify part–whole substitution separately as synecdoche. Note the definition being used rather than denying an association to enforce one label. Describe the relation first, allow alternative labels for borderline cases, and do not promise one classification for every associated expression.

## Use later pronouns and actions to refine candidates

A later sentence in which Lin requests clear image sources supports his involvement in the exchange without proving he alone formed the earlier institutional view. They will discuss it next week supplies a plural clue, but context must still identify who they are. Keep each new clue’s location and distinguish earlier knowledge from later information instead of overwriting uncertainty. If the same name later refers to the room, reassess that sentence. One figurative use does not automatically propagate to every repeated occurrence.

## Check shorthand, personification and metonymy separately

For a room was complaining, ask whether the people inside are meant or whether human feeling is assigned to a space. Without context, the verb alone does not settle it. An abbreviation is a shorter name and need not substitute a referent. If the signature is unclear, return to the complete passage and layout rather than importing a commenter’s explanation as textual evidence. Mark paraphrases as your own; do not place invented unanimity in quotation marks. In discussion, retain the necessary short wording, location and association path rather than exchanging uncheckable labels.

## Leave and return to restate who did what

Leave reading and return to the passage. Locate the actual references to the editorial office and Lin, then restate the referents in the institutional reply, office-window and cup-content examples. Explain why the bridge control uses resemblance and why agreement by every member remains unproven. Keep source location, voice, literal name, candidate referent, evidence of association and inference limits. Success means another reader can check your reference judgment from the text, not automatic noun highlighting or proof of authorial motives and institutional processes.
