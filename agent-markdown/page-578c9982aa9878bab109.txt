### 2. A Swift2.0 recreation of Aixianfeng

[Download:](<https://github.com/ZhongTaoTian/LoveFreshBeen>)

> Introduction: this open-source project recreates Aixianfeng, an e-commerce APP. It uses Swift2.0 and Xcode7.0.1. The interface is built entirely in code, without XIB or StoryBoard. Development took about 2 months of spare time. All data is local. Supporting tools were PhotoShop CS6 for image editing and Charles for capturing network traffic. It was written in a hurry and is imperfect in many places. Leave suggestions or optimization ideas in the article's comments; I will read and reply to them individually.
