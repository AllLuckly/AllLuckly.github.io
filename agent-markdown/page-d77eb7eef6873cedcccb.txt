ABOUT

I’m an iOS developer, and I also enjoy sketching and interior design.

This blog used to focus mainly on iOS technical articles. Alongside development notes, I now write about the apps I make: how to use them, what you can do with them and how to work through problems.

## The apps I’m making

![Aquarell icon](<https://allluckly.cn/assets/images/apps/pastel.png>)

### Aquarell

A drawing app with brushes, layers and palettes. Make a watercolour-style illustration or begin with a leaf or a cup. Here I explain the canvas setup, colouring and export steps.

[About the app ↗](<https://allluckly.cn/en/apps/aquarell/>)[Read the tutorials →](<https://allluckly.cn/en/guides/aquarell/>)

![ManYue icon](<https://allluckly.cn/assets/images/apps/man_yue.png>)

### ManYue

A comic reader for your own files on a phone or tablet, with connections to a NAS or comic server. The tutorials begin with local import, then cover shelves, offline reading and server connections.

[About the app ↗](<https://allluckly.cn/en/apps/manyue/>)[Read the tutorials →](<https://allluckly.cn/en/guides/manyue/>)

![VectorPaint icon](<https://allluckly.cn/assets/images/apps/draw_illustration.png>)

### VectorPaint

For vector drawing, icons and SVG edits. It works mainly with shapes, paths and nodes. If you want a simple logo or a graphic that can scale, start with a geometric icon.

[About the app ↗](<https://allluckly.cn/en/apps/vectorpaint/>)[Read the tutorials →](<https://allluckly.cn/en/guides/vectorpaint/>)

![Book Isle icon](<https://allluckly.cn/assets/images/apps/nas_book.png>)

### Book Isle

For organising personal ebooks and connecting NAS or Calibre libraries. I begin with importing one book and adjusting its reading layout, then cover tags, notes and reading-data sync. The tutorials distinguish readable formats from manageable files, and remote entries from real downloads.

[About the app ↗](<https://allluckly.cn/en/apps/nasbook/>)[Read the tutorials →](<https://allluckly.cn/en/guides/nasbook/>)

![iCouldPlay icon](<https://allluckly.cn/assets/images/apps/icouldplay.png>)

### iCouldPlay

One place to play local videos, NAS files and content from your personal media servers. Tutorials begin with a single video, then cover the library, subtitles, audio tracks and playback issues, with a clear result to compare after each change.

[About the app ↗](<https://allluckly.cn/en/apps/icouldplay/>)[Read the tutorials →](<https://allluckly.cn/en/guides/icouldplay/>)

![Yearn icon](<https://allluckly.cn/assets/images/apps/yearn.png>)

### Yearn

A retro game emulator for a collection of game files you provide. I cover importing, controllers, display settings and save checks. Begin by successfully saving and loading one game, then organise the rest of your collection.

[About the app ↗](<https://allluckly.cn/en/apps/yearn/>)[Read the tutorials →](<https://allluckly.cn/en/guides/yearn/>)

![调调 icon](<https://allluckly.cn/assets/images/apps/diaodiao.jpg>)

### 调调

Keep your own audio close. Import through Files or a computer on the same Wi-Fi network, listen to imported songs offline, organise a queue and set a sleep timer. You can also connect a compatible personal music server.

[Meet the personal music player ↗](<https://allluckly.cn/en/apps/diaodiao/>)

![Jianlai icon](<https://allluckly.cn/assets/images/apps/jianlai.jpg>)

### Jianlai

A keyboard with a look you can customise. Combine themes, colours, fonts and backgrounds, style the space bar separately, and use supported input languages and suggestions in available fields.

[Meet the custom keyboard ↗](<https://allluckly.cn/en/apps/jianlai/>)
