```text
Night caretaker

```

## Start with fictional voice and an addressed listener

Poetry Foundation describes an imagined speaker addressing a listener whose speech is not presented, usually not the reader. Use that working definition without classifying every first-person poem alike. D1’s inspector and second-person address ground an internal listener. Keep author, speaker, reader separate. Dramatic does not require stage solitude, and a short poem is not automatically lyrical autobiography. Establish formal evidence before interpreting self-revelation.

## Trace address and deictic words to the listener

Inspector names the addressee; your gloves refers to that person’s gloves; before you came provides a reported arrival reference; You continues the discouragement. Connect these phrases to one listener candidate without inventing age, agency or belief. Gloves mentioned in speech are not independently seen objects. ## Unquoted reply does not mean muteness

All four lines use one presented voice and quote no inspector reply. This does not establish inability to speak, absence of action, no questions or permanent silence. The key? may be self-questioning, topic repetition or response to an unquoted question; the text does not decide uniquely. Separate the visible lack of direct reply from hypotheses. Do not insert listener words into the source. Wider context may supply reactions, so a four-line window cannot establish only one person exists in the whole work.

## Separate self-report from reader inference

The speaker claims sweeping ash, placing a key in the river and discourages looking behind a door. Possible interpretations include directing attention, explaining actions or evading inspection. None proves lying, crime or hidden contents. These are self-reports, not independently certified by an omniscient narrator. Attach wording to each inference and consider alternatives, such as ordinary storage behind the door. Suspicion may remain, with limits; suspense is not a verdict.

Need not means no necessity, not must not prohibition or cannot inability. Discouraging inspection can guide attention, but these lines do not state whether the listener eventually looks. Preserve modal scope instead of strengthening it into you must never open the door for suspense.
