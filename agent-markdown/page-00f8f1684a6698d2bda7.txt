A question mark does not establish hypophora or a rhetorical question. Read three original passages in Book Isle, tracing question, answer and speaker continuity before interpreting their function. English and Chinese rhetorical terminology is not always one-to-one; this exercise uses hypophora for raising and answering one’s own question. The app locates text, while the reader classifies it from context.

## Preserve three complete original passages

Preserve A: “为什么还要留一盏灯？因为晚归的人仍需要找到门。” (“Why leave a lamp on? Because late arrivals still need to find the door.”), with one voice answering immediately. B: “小林问：钥匙在哪里？小周答：在抽屉里。” (“Lin asks: Where is the key? Zhou answers: In the drawer.”), an actual exchange. C, after everyone sees the lock intact: “门锁明明完好，难道是风把钥匙藏进了抽屉？” (“The lock is intact; did the wind really hide the key in the drawer?”), without a following self-answer. Record background, file, location and quotation boundaries, not three isolated question marks.

## Trace the questioner and answerer in A

Mark A’s question and the answer beginning with “因为”, then confirm no speaker change or quotation intervenes. It raises the reason for the lamp and immediately supplies late arrivals’ need, meeting this exercise’s self-answering definition. Explain the focusing-then-reasoning effect, not a mechanical rule based on “why”. If fuller text identifies another questioner, revise the attribution. A single voice is this original card’s condition, not proof that every adjacent question and answer shares one speaker.

## Separate information seeking in B

Lin lacks the key’s location and Zhou supplies the drawer as new information. Record questioner, answerer and informational change rather than classify any nearby interview or dialogue pair as hypophora. Typographic proximity does not merge speakers. A narrator reporting the exchange introduces another layer; one font does not imply one voice. Establish attribution before asking whether Zhou is truthful, which requires later story evidence and cannot be inferred from question marks and colons.

## Use context to interpret C’s rhetorical force

In the given setting C offers the implausible candidate that wind hid the key, tending to reject it and prompt consideration of human action rather than seek wind’s technique. Note the intact lock and wording “明明” and “难道”, while retaining the condition that personified or magical wind would require reassessment. Such wording does not impose one fixed intensity. Rejecting a wind explanation does not identify a particular culprit: rhetorical exclusion and actual responsibility are different conclusions.

## Match the answer to the question asked

A asks why the lamp remains on; finding the door supplies a relevant reason. A blue lampshade description would not answer that why-question. Separate question type, response and whether it settles the issue, including cases where an answer follows a digression. This example answers immediately; it does not prohibit broader rhetorical treatments of delayed answers. State the definition when using another terminology system. Traceable sentence relations matter more than forcing every difference into one Chinese or English label.

## Change context as a controlled comparison

Save C with a control background: a fairy tale has explicitly established a wind spirit capable of carrying keys, while the question stays unchanged. It may now be a genuine inquiry; real-world assumptions cannot automatically establish rhetorical force. Name both backgrounds and keep the control out of the original story. Context dependence does not make all readings equally supported: each needs supplied conditions. With missing later context, retain candidates until evidence narrows them instead of using uncertainty as proof of rhetoric.

## Check punctuation-only and speaker errors

If A is mistaken for an interview, check for an actual speaker change. If B is called self-answering, revisit Lin’s and Zhou’s speech labels. If C allegedly identifies the culprit, remove that unsupported conclusion. For nested quotations or unclear scans, find a clear edition instead of replacing punctuation to manufacture an effect. Keep original wording separate from interpretation, never pasting analysis into a quotation. Manual attribution needs no automatic voice detector; competing readings require evidence rather than an invented system verdict.

## Reopen and verify the question-answer chain

Reopen the file and verify all three passages and backgrounds. Reconstruct who asks, who answers, answer scope and contextual function. Retain A’s self-answering chain, B’s information exchange and C’s rhetorical candidate with its fairy-tale alternative, each tied to location. Existing bookmarks may help; cross-device sync need not have been tested. Another reader should reconstruct the reasoning from the text. Reassess if an edition removes an answer or adds another voice instead of treating a label as context-independent forever.
