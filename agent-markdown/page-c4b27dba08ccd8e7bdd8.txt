DEVELOPMENT NOTES · 9 articles

Beyond native apps are web interactions, mini programs and this blog itself. This collection also keeps source-code recommendations and retrospectives, preserving both concrete solutions and the path of learning.

## How to read this collection

1. Read WebView basics before JavaScript-to-native calls. Focus on responsibilities and data flow; historical UIWebView examples are not current implementation advice.
2. Read the mini-program series in order to understand pages, data and events, then compare it with current tools and platform documentation.
3. Read recommendations, blog indexing notes and annual reviews in publication order. They reflect the projects and tools of their time; external links and indexing outcomes need a fresh check.

These notes date from 2015–2017. Their examples have not been rerun for this archive update. Check the dates and version notes before applying the ideas to a current project.
