## Establish a low body continuous with the long tail

Use a horizontal canvas of about 2200 × 1500 pixels. Taper the small head leftwards, round the back and extend the tail gradually from the rump towards the lower right rather than attaching a thin string. Before scales, check continuity of body, short neck and head. Separate body colour, scales, underside and feet from the sketch on pale sandy grey. Follow the Sunda pangolin shown by NParks, without mixing ears or gaits from every pangolin species. Pixels are layout suggestions. Save and check the body-to-tail silhouette before zoning.

## Define the unscaled underside with a soft boundary

Reserve a soft, unscaled or sparsely haired underside and inner-limb region. This side view exposes only a suitable strip, not an animal flipped onto its back for explanation. Separate dorsal scales from the underside with a gentle boundary rather than a rigid plastic lid. Refine nose and eye regions from the reference without extending large scales into the eyeball. For all-over armour, restore the lower zone; for a white apron, reduce contrast and turn the boundary with the body. This distinguishes surfaces, not a section or armour-strength demonstration.

## Give each scale a hidden base and exposed edge

Select six near-side scales for checking, not as a whole-animal count. Hide each broad base under the preceding scale and expose its free edge towards the rear. At overlap retain only the foreground edge rather than outlining isolated complete diamonds. Vary back and flank shapes, wider near and narrower far. For a fishnet, remove meaningless crossing lines and restore explicit overlaps. For a hedgehog, shorten scale edges mistaken for long spines. Simplify reference shapes without imposing a strictly regular hexagonal array on real scales.

## Turn overlapping scales over shoulders and hips

Lightly draw three volume guides: turn the shoulder pattern rearwards, narrow flank scales towards the underside and taper the rump into the tail root. These guides are not three fixed anatomical scale rows. Keep nearer edges clearer and show only parts of far edges without turning head and neck into another viewpoint to align the pattern. Inspect neighbouring overlaps without colour, avoiding impossible cycles where every scale covers the next. For flat roof tiles, restore the back’s curve and shading rather than merely skewing the whole layer.

## Reduce tail scales gradually without severing the tail root

Continue rump volume into the tail root, narrowing visible scales towards the tip rather than abruptly switching to a smooth rat tail. Inner and outer curves differ in length; scale edges must turn with them instead of carrying a straight strip of pattern around the bend. Simplify a few tip edges rather than making a serrated brush. For a separate-pinecone tail, restore its broad root and taper; for a break, check an overly dark root shadow. This reference-based placement does not generalise climbing or support behaviour across species.

## Keep the small head and short limbs distinct from scales

Retain nose and small eye on the pointed head, with only the visible reference ear, not long rabbit ears. Extend near fore- and hindlimbs beneath shoulder and hip, suggesting claws with a few curved edges and leaving hidden far limbs incomplete. Stop scale contours at overlaps; feet must not grow from scale tips. Use sparse short strokes on the soft underside, firmer scale edges and shared upper-left light. If every texture is soft, strengthen selected overlaps. For metal armour, remove uniform white framing: keratin does not imply polished steel.

## Check the six sample scales together with the underside

Hide tiny highlights and the background. Revisit six numbered sample scales, identifying what covers each base and where its free edge points, then confirm the unscaled underside. Correct overlap before removing numbers if directions conflict. If the belly is scaled, restore zoning; another colour alone does not make it unscaled. This is no complete scale census, so drawn counts cannot determine weight, age or species. Keep the faulty version and repair one shoulder, flank and tail-root area, rechecking small without reversing the whole pattern.
