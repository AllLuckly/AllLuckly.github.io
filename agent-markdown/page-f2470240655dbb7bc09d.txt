## Block the long bill and substantial body

Use an approximately 2000×1500 px landscape canvas, leaving room for a left-facing bill, body lower-right and short legs on one ground plane. Block an oval body, curved neck and small head; extend the bill with the head rather than attach a rod to a sphere. Make the body substantially larger than the head without a universal ratio. Separate sketch, plumage, bill/pouch and ground, then save native work. This invented shore pose needs no added feeding event. Read bill, neck and body silhouette before feathers.

## Give both mandibles a shared base and slight opening

Locate the bill base in front of the eye, then draw long upper and lower mandibles joined near the mouth corner with a modest opening, not exaggerated scissors. The firmer upper contour differs from the pouch-bearing lower bill; do not merge them into a banana. Continue the mouth seam forward, softening where occluded rather than inventing a third bill. Keep the eye on the head behind the base, not in the pouch. Inspect the head transition enlarged and uncropped tip at fit-to-window size. This opening is a pose, not evidence of swallowing.

## Suspend the pouch from the lower bill

Join a soft pouch below the lower bill, broader near the mouth corner and narrowing toward the tip, with a shallow hanging contour. It must not emerge behind the eye as a ball or sit atop the upper mandible. Show a relaxed, unfilled state through a few soft folds, not transparent fish-filled anatomy. Separate pouch from neck and avoid a black cavity cutting through the head. If it reads as a beard, restore a continuous thin surface. If it resembles another mouth, remove extra hard edges and re-establish the lower bill as its upper boundary.

## Connect neck and body beneath a folded wing

Continue the neck behind the head into the chest rather than substitute the pouch for the entire neck. Place a folded wing on the upper body side, tapering rearward, with black flight feathers only where visible. Do not replace white plumage with a black wing mass. The far wing may be concealed; note incomplete display, not a one-winged bird. Group feather directions into three areas before defining the wing. Feathers do not root in belly or pouch. Distinguish rear wingtip from tail so one black point cannot stand for both.

## Attach short legs and webbed feet beneath the body

Place two short visible legs below the torso, the far one slightly behind and potentially concealed, with feet on one shore plane. Show webbing through toe directions plus connecting thin surfaces, not separated chicken claws. Occlusion does not establish a reduced biological toe count. Use narrow contact shadows rather than a base merging both feet. No waterline is present, so do not cut off legs in midair and add blue strokes as a substitute. Hide feather detail to check roots, feet and ground, then restore a substantial body supported from below, not legs hanging from the tail.

## Model white plumage separately from pouch texture

Use slightly warm white plumage with cool pale shadows and grouped dark-grey flight feathers instead of black lines in every feather seam. Give bill and pouch muted yellow-orange and warm grey, with softer pouch folds than hard bill edges. Do not make both identical plastic shells. Relate underside shadow to ground without swallowing ankles. Separate merging pouch and neck through value, not a thick black incision. Palette is illustrative; a reference without a prominent plate does not deny seasonal changes or identify sex or health from yellow intensity.

## Troubleshoot lower bill, pouch rim and folded wing

Hide feather texture and trace head–upper bill, lower bill–pouch and neck–body separately. The pouch remains below. Find its attachment boundary if floating; restore the shared mouth corner if mandibles become separate blades. Reposition a belly-rooted wing through the body sketch rather than recolour it. Assign two representative legs and the near folded wing, documenting concealed far parts without adding floating wings to meet a count. Preserve a failing version and repair locally. Static pouch shape does not establish observed fishing, water capacity or swallowing.
