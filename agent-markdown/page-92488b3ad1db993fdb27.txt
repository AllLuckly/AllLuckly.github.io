## Prepare an authorized comic

Prepare a small owned or authorized CBZ comic on a computer and external storage device. Record filename, size, edition and recognizable first and last pages. Keep an independent computer original; distinguish same-named files on the drive by folder or edition before importing. Confirm available space on the phone or tablet and do not move the entire library yet. This exercise uses the system file picker to access external storage; it requires no NAS and does not assume insertion automatically adds comics to the shelf.

## Check connector, adapter and power

Choose a compatible cable or adapter for the iPhone or iPad and storage device; some external drives require additional power. Apple describes different power options for different connectors, so follow your actual device and accessory instructions. First confirm the drive can be read by its original computer, then connect it to the mobile device while retaining its data. If recognition fails, check power, cable and connection individually rather than repeatedly unplugging it or declaring the comic corrupt. A trusted working accessory can serve as a comparison.

## Confirm the drive in Files first

Open the system Files app, tap Browse, find the external device under Locations and navigate to the comic folder. If only Recents appears, return to Browse rather than relying on that list. Check the device folder, filename and size. If the drive is absent, check connection, power and system compatibility first. Apple documents partition and filesystem requirements; inspect without erasing or reformatting an existing disk to import a comic. If the format is incompatible, preserve the data and let its owner decide the next step. If the drive is unreadable on the computer too, stop and investigate there rather than blaming ManYue. If it works on the computer but is absent on the mobile device, record both observations and check adapter and power. If you own another known-good compatible drive with test data, check read-only whether it appears to narrow the device or connection issue; do not exchange unverified data.

## Confirm the intended archive and edition

Record device name, folder path, filename, size and recognizable edition. For same-named volumes, compare their contents on the computer first. Similar names or covers do not prove identical content, and a volume suffix may be truncated. Choose a complete CBZ copy; changing a suffix is not format conversion, and do not extract an archive over its source directory. If access fails or the copy is incomplete, investigate separately on the original computer and the drive.

## Open the file picker from ManYue

Open the local comic import entry actually visible in ManYue. In the system picker browse to the external device and verified folder, select the single target and wait according to the displayed prompts. A shelf entry not appearing immediately is not necessarily failure: selection, reading, processing and import are distinct stages. If the device is absent in the picker, first check it remains available in Files and inspect the current browse location. Avoid repeated import taps and do not assume identical prompts in every version.

## Keep the drive connected while verifying

After import completes, locate the work on the shelf and check recognizable first, middle and last pages against the recorded edition and cover. If progress, blank pages or errors remain, record the page and message and keep the device connected. A cover without readable content is only a visible cover; if no entry appears, check import status and shelf scope. Sampled pages establish only what you inspected, not an exhaustive page-by-page audit.

## Disconnect and verify the local copy

After import and access have finished and no copy is running, disconnect external storage according to the device guidance. Return to ManYue, locate the work and check first and last pages again; if practical temporarily disconnect the network too, then restore it. Readability of checked pages after disconnection is the observation. If they fail, record it and reconnect to inspect the source without reformatting or deleting the drive. Mark untested if safe disconnection or offline checking was not possible; do not assume a complete app copy. Record the test time and device and recheck after changing devices.

## Record the failure stage

Record device model, system and app versions, whether external storage appeared in Files, target path, import feedback and reading outcome; redact private paths and comic content when sharing. If Files has no drive, investigate connector and power first; if the drive is visible but the item is missing, inspect the folder; if selectable but unreadable, investigate import and archive. Preserve computer original and external copy and confirm both remain in place. Do not make reformatting or clearing app data a routine repair, and do not infer every drive is compatible from one success.
