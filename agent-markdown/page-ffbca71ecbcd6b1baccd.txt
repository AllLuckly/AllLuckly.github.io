A tape dispenser contains both a rolled cylinder and a flat extension of the same material. A ring beside a pointed platform can leave their connection unreadable. Draw a stationary desktop dispenser without demonstrating cutting. Trace the roll-to-platform path, then transparency, plastic and shadow manually in Aquarell. Proportions are illustration choices, not cutting-tool manufacturing or safety-test evidence.

## Align base and roll directions

Use an approximately 1800×1400 px landscape canvas with pale grey-green background, rear upper left and front platform lower right. The roll axis spans the base width. Establish base envelope and roll-end ellipse with one viewpoint rather than a frontal roll beside a side-on platform. Separate sketch, base, roll, loose strip, platform and shadow; choose grey-blue plastic. Omit cutting teeth initially. Repair roll/axle-seat position if wheel-on-shoe-like rather than adding reflection. Reduce excessive base thickness and retain rear space for the roll.

## Give core and outer roll aligned ellipses

Align the outer roll ellipse and smaller core opening, using warm-grey interior rather than solid black. Show only exposed far-side depth, not a full distant circle through the near face. Suggest one or two quiet winding layers rather than dark grooves everywhere. Reduce heavy edges and side thickness if tyre-like or expose axial depth if sticker-flat. Hide the base to inspect the roll, then restore axle-seat occlusion. The opening must reveal a coherent background, not two incompatible views at once.

## Connect a broad strip path to the platform

Choose a plausible departure from the roll exterior and lead a flat strip to the front platform, establishing its centre path and two edges. Width changes follow perspective: the nearer part is wider and the farther part slightly narrower; the strip cannot emerge from the core hole. Remove diagnostic direction arrows from the finish. Restore width if thread-like or soften departure and add slight curvature if rigid-board-like. Do not switch sides mid-air or pass through solid base. Establish continuous material first; transparency cannot excuse a missing connection.

## Seat the strip on the platform

Let the strip briefly rest on the front platform and end at a readable position with modest rear occlusion. Suggest the cutting edge with a few small notches, not an enormous saw or nearby fingers. Inspect strip/platform alone for gaps at contact or unexplained passage underneath. Repair placement and tight shadow if flag-like; retain a distinct thin edge if white-paint-like. Tooth count is not part of the task and may be omitted where unclear. Recognition should not depend on exaggerated teeth.

## Use edges and subtle value for clear tape

Use pale warm grey and restrained contrast, preserving background structure through the tape with slight value shifts and narrow reflections instead of opaque milky white. Layered roll material may appear darker than one strip without implying measured transmittance. Recover disappearing tape locally through background/edge separation, not a black frame. Reduce broad white and thick edges if frosted-board-like. Reflections follow turning rather than identical white lines everywhere. Transparency and continuity require separate checks; seeing background does not establish a correct strip path.

## Separate roll occlusion from ground shadow

Let the base cover lower roll portions without splitting its outline into disconnected halves. Cast ground shadow lower right, tight beneath the base, with separate explanations for core and platform darks. Hide shadow to inspect completeness and restore contact. Reconnect a floating base or narrow a melting black band. Keep strip shadow softer and lighter so it does not become a second tape. Broader plastic highlights are sufficient; brands, lettering and hidden mechanisms need not fill space.

## Audit one roll feeding one strip

Keep a simplified outer roll, departure, strip edges and platform endpoint and trace the material once. Investigate shadows or highlights if a branch appears; reconnect a gap rather than covering it with translucent colour. Change one turn or landing while holding ellipses and light fixed. A different departure needs a new version, not a moved endpoint retaining the old root. Remove diagnostic labels so the final form, not arrows, explains one material path.

## Check for a broken strip in the output

Save the layered original and inspect departure, strip width and platform contact in a PNG at use size. Strengthen local value or quiet background if the strip becomes broken thread rather than outlining it black. Lighten a black-hole core while retaining its lip. Record faults such as reflection erasing the strip’s right edge near the platform for layer repair. This is a static exterior/path study, not cutting performance, safety or a claim of downloading a template. Recheck ellipses, axle seat and strip plane for a new view rather than mirroring old highlights.
