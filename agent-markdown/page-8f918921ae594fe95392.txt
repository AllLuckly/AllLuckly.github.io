调调 plays your audio; AirPlay discovery and output selection involve Apple’s system and the receiver. Selecting a destination, seeing a playing state and hearing sound from that device are separate observations.

## Prepare authorized audio and an available receiver

Choose an owned or authorized recording with a recognizable spoken or musical opening. Record its title, duration and audible cue and retain the original file. Use an audio AirPlay receiver you own or may use, in a place where playing it will not disturb others. If no receiver is available, complete only the baseline on the original output device and mark receiver observations pending. Use a suitable low volume and do not send content to unknown devices.

## Record current output and player state

Play a few seconds in 调调 through the iPhone’s current output. Confirm track identity, playhead and audible cue. Record output, volume, queue and any existing sleep timer; do not delete the recording or clear the queue. If audio already fails, inspect file and volume before comparing output. Sound from the original output device establishes the baseline, not a newly connected AirPlay receiver.

## Confirm network and receiver identity

Follow actual Apple and receiver instructions for network prerequisites and identify the intended receiver in the system’s visible list. If devices share a name, ask the owner or use a recognizable name; do not test another person’s equipment. Record network type and a device alias without publishing home addresses, identifiers or accounts. If absent, mark the receiver pending. Do not disable a firewall, network isolation or expose the receiver publicly.

## Switch through a visible system control

Keeping the same track and playback position, use the actual iPhone system AirPlay audio output control to select the identified receiver. Record system destination before and after and the action time. Do not assume 调调 supplies a particular AirPlay icon, and do not mistake a receiver icon for audible sound. Follow your own device’s connection prompts; stop if the destination cannot be established rather than transmitting to an unknown device.

## Observe player and sound separately

After selection inspect current track, play/pause state and playhead in 调调, then listen briefly near the receiver for the recognizable cue. Mark “system shows selected device,” “player shows playback” and “receiver produces target sound” separately as observed, failed or pending. If phone and receiver sound differ in timing or one is silent, record actual conditions. Without measurement equipment do not invent latency figures or automatically attribute the outcome to the app.

## Restore the original output as a control

Use the same system output control to restore the initial destination. Confirm the track and listen through the phone or original receiver. Record any manual pause, resume or volume change; manual recovery is not automatic recovery. If the original output is unavailable, mark it pending rather than resetting the entire network. Playback naturally advances, so compare a genuinely recognizable cue instead of demanding identical instants.

## Troubleshoot at the correct layer

If multiple receivers can be selected, begin with one identified device so simultaneous sound does not obscure attribution. Only when the system actually supports it and the user wishes, make a separate multi-device observation. Record single or multiple destinations, receiver volumes and the played passage. Room acoustics affect listening impressions; do not estimate precise synchronization differences or audio quality from distant loudness. If a receiver name changes, recheck the system list instead of assuming it is the earlier device.

Write the actual action order, such as select receiver, observe player, listen near receiver, then restore phone output. Record network or volume changes made mid-trial, and repeat if necessary instead of merging results across conditions. If the actual sounding device cannot be established, leave that item pending even when the system reports a connection.

If the system list lacks the target, inspect receiver power, identity, network and Apple’s actual feedback. If selectable but silent, check receiver volume, mute and output. If 调调 is paused or the track ended, check player state first. If destination is correct but audio is unrecognizable, use another owned short recording as a separate control. Change one condition per retry and retain the starting state; network policy belongs to the device owner or administrator.

## Restore and deliver

Stop the test and restore original output, volume, queue and timer state. Deliver nonprivate device alias, network condition, track identity, system destination, player state, audible result and unfinished checks. A listed device is not successful playback, and one short successful segment does not guarantee all receivers, networks or formats. Apple’s AirPlay guidance and public 调调 playback information inform this exercise; no private-network or physical-device test is claimed.
