## Construct the outward equilateral apex

Choose an upward screen apex C(250,400−50√3), approximately y=313.397. BC, CD and original BD all equal 100, forming an equilateral triangle. ## Calculate route and endpoint distance separately

Four segments of 100 give route length 400, while unchanged A/E remain 300 apart: ratio 4:3. Bounding-box perimeter is not curve length, and a slope’s horizontal projection 50 is not its length.
