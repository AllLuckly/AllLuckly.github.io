A leaf diagram may contain water, carbon-dioxide and sunlight arrows without all three being matter inputs. Read original panels and a chemical ledger in ManYue to check atoms, coefficients and energy. This represents a simplified overall relation for oxygenic photosynthesis, not a single real reaction step or all plant physiology.

## Give four substances and light different legends

Externally draw a leaf. Use solid arrows for carbon dioxide and water entering, carbohydrate formation and oxygen release, plus a wavy arrow for light energy. Label the page as a matter-and-energy diagram: arrow thickness does not measure reaction rate, and root water uptake is not the leaf’s instantaneous net water use. Put the diagram on P1, the atom ledger on P2, and coefficient/mass comparisons on P3, retaining the source file. Glucose represents carbohydrate bookkeeping in the overall equation, not a claim that a chloroplast makes one glucose directly in a single step. Pair colour labels with words so that grayscale reading retains arrow identity.

## Copy the net equation and distinguish coefficients from subscripts

Use the simplified net equation below. Subscripts specify one molecule; coefficient 6 multiplies its entire composition. Six CO2 molecules each contain one C,two O,not formula C6O2. One C6H12O6 has six C,twelve H,six O; an omitted coefficient means one,not zero. Explain coefficients and subscripts separately. Correct a mistaken character and recount rather than alter molecular formulas to force balance. Place light energy beside the arrow as a condition,not an invented C/H/O substance or seventh reactant.

```text
6 CO2 + 6 H2O -> C6H12O6 + 6 O2  [light energy input]
left atoms:  C=6, H=12, O=18
right atoms: C=6, H=12, O=18
```

## Count each element rather than total molecules

Carbon is six on each side. Six waters supply twelve H,matching twelve in sugar. Oxygen is twelve from CO2 plus six from water,eighteen; products have six in sugar plus twelve in six O2,also eighteen. Total molecules are twelve versus seven and need not match: each element’s atoms are conserved. Misreading six O2 as six oxygen atoms yields only twelve product O and exposes a missed subscript. Keep three element rows with sources rather than one total 36 that could hide offsetting element errors.

## Check a formal mass ledger using moles

State approximate atomic masses C 12, H 1, O 16. Molar masses are CO2 44g/mol,water 18,glucose 180,oxygen 32. Per mole of glucose in the net equation,reactants are 264g CO2 plus 108g water,total 372g; products are 180g glucose plus 192g O2,also 372g. Six is a molar ratio,not six grams; molecular number ratios are not mass ratios. This is stoichiometric bookkeeping,not a claim that a plant converts a whole cup of water to a specified sugar mass or a measured yield/growth rate.

Reverse-check the mass ledger:192g O2 at 32g/mol is 6mol;180g glucose at 180g/mol is 1mol,restoring 6:1 coefficients. Treating 192 as an oxygen-atom count mixes grams,moles and particles. Repair the units instead of only replacing a number.

## A net equation cannot trace every oxygen atom’s origin

Add an expanded overall ledger: six CO2 plus twelve H2O yield one glucose,six O2 and six H2O. Each side has C6, H24, O24. Cancel six waters from both sides to obtain the net equation. Balance alone cannot track each product oxygen atom’s original molecule or prove released O2 came directly from CO2. Biological light reactions use water and produce oxygen; source tracing requires mechanism or labeling evidence,not connecting the nearest drawn arrow. Net input and expanded bookkeeping are different,compatible descriptions.

Before cancellation,box reactant and product waters separately and label algebraic net bookkeeping. Cancellation does not mean every incoming water molecule emerges unchanged,nor do arrow lengths represent all real intermediate steps. Mechanism has its own evidential scope.

```text
6 CO2 + 12 H2O -> C6H12O6 + 6 O2 + 6 H2O
left atoms:  C=6, H=24, O=24
right atoms: C=6, H=24, O=24
cancel 6 H2O from both sides -> net equation
```

## Separate light input from whole-plant balances

Light drives the process and contributes to chemical-energy storage; it creates no C,H,O atoms. A balanced mass ledger does not remove the light requirement. Plants also respire,so measured net gas exchange reflects multiple processes rather than guaranteed oxygen release at every moment. The equation’s carbohydrate carbon comes from CO2; water/mineral uptake does not mean all organic matter comes from soil. This is not a complete metabolic network. Keep matter, energy and whole-plant measurements separate and define scope for added processes.
