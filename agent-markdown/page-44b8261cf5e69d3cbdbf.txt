## Organise three levels around one axis

On an approximately 1600×1600 px canvas, align reamer tip, strainer centre and cup base vertically, with a slightly elevated view. ## Seat the strainer on the cup mouth

It must not float like a disc or collapse to a zero-thickness line inside the cup. Draw thin sidewall, top and narrow seating seam.
