## Preserve purposes and complete wording

Use original A, “他为占有而保存，她为分享而保存。” (“He preserves to possess; she preserves to share.”). Both organise old letters they are authorised to manage; no actual disclosure is stated. Retain source, subjects and full predicates, not only possess/share. Chinese is the analysed original; translation need not match character counts. External notes suffice, and wording must not be edited merely for symmetry.

## Align grammatical slots

Align subject, purpose and preserving action. Shared action supports comparison while possess/share occupy differing purpose slots. Different pronouns are not inherently opposing ideas. Parallelism highlights correspondence, but BYU describes contrast as often, not invariably, parallel. Separate this example’s form from the term’s general scope instead of requiring equal counts for all antithesis.

## Identify purpose rather than completed acts

Possess/share contrast keeping for oneself with making available to others in this context. Stated purposes do not prove universal refusal or actual publication of every letter. Separate purpose, action and result rather than fill unstated events through a rhetorical label. Moral evaluation, permissions and privacy require further context; one sentence does not establish good/evil identities or one universally correct disclosure method.

## Use aligned but nonopposed clauses

B becomes “他为查阅而保存，她为整理而保存。” (“He preserves to consult; she preserves to organise.”). Slots remain parallel, while consultation and organisation may cooperate rather than oppose. Do not inherit A’s contrast from form alone or dismiss B’s value. Only purpose words change. Later context could create conflict, requiring updated evidence rather than a universal claim that these ideas can never contrast.

## Locate opposition beyond isolated antonyms

For “门开了，灯暗了。” (“The door opened; the lamp dimmed.”), open/dim modify different objects and do not automatically supply one antonymic dimension. Light/dark or open/closed controls need appropriate context. This does not require all antithesis to involve one object; it requires an explainable comparison. Record words, targets and dimensions rather than force a dictionary antonym list onto any adjacent clauses.

## Separate opposition from ABBA order

A repeats the same directional structure rather than requiring ABBA reversal. Chiasmus concerns conceptual or lexical reversal; antithesis concerns contrasting ideas. They can overlap or occur separately. Reversing A’s whole clauses preserves possess/share opposition while changing reading order, not character purposes. Keep the control separate and distinguish attention sequence from story chronology or causation.

## Check translated labels and overstrong evaluations

Natural English word order does not determine Chinese parallelism. Claims of wholly selfish/selfless personalities may overextend purpose words; retain candidates when context is missing. Shelf ratings do not provide moral scores for clauses. Check missing words and punctuation against the source rather than repair it into an ideal sentence and attribute that form to the author. Original cards and slot diagrams suffice without private letters.

## Reopen and verify form and idea separately

Reopen A/B without labels, align slots and distinguish A’s purpose contrast from B’s nonnecessary opposition. Retain wording, dimension, unstated acts, order-only control and ABBA distinction. Reanalyse changed contexts or purpose words instead of treating a bookmark as a conclusion.
