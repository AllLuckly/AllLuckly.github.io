### [Cape Lighthouse: build a geometric seascape with 17 closed paths in VectorPaint](<https://allluckly.cn/en/works/vectorpaint-cape-lighthouse-duo/>)

Use closed pen paths, fills and strokes in VectorPaint on an iPhone Duo simulator to build a sun, mountains, sea, cape and lighthouse. Follow the stacking of 17 objects, a window-closure mistake and three separate wave highlights.

Aquarell  ·   2026.10.23

### [Moonlit Pines: build a geometric night landscape with Aquarell polygon selections](<https://allluckly.cn/en/works/aquarell-moonlit-pines-duo/>)

Use polygon selections, fills and four layers in Aquarell on an iPhone Duo simulator to make snowy mountains, three pines, a moon, twelve stars and lake reflections. Includes a chaptered video, a five-colour palette and the native 1400×1000 PNG.

### [Aquarell creative process: paint a night scene with soft pools of light](<https://allluckly.cn/en/works/aquarell-night-glow/>)

Layer short mint, lilac and warm-white brush marks over deep blue to practise brush size, opacity and empty space. Includes the recorded workflow, colour settings and native exported artwork.
