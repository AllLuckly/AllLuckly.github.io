[Start with the first tutorial](<https://allluckly.cn/en/guides/manyue-getting-started/>)[About ManYue](<https://allluckly.cn/en/apps/manyue/>)

Start with one local file you own or are authorised to read. After importing and reading it, choose a NAS or server guide that matches your storage. A successful connection still needs a page-reading and offline check.

**Choose a tutorial for your task**

- [Prepare offline reading](<https://allluckly.cn/en/guides/manyue-offline-preflight/>): Check that the chapters you need open without a connection.
- [Connect a WebDAV library](<https://allluckly.cn/en/guides/manyue-webdav-library/>): Check the address, folder permissions and downloaded copies.
- [Connect to Komga](<https://allluckly.cn/en/guides/manyue-komga-library/>): Check the server account, series and chapter access.
- [Sync reading records](<https://allluckly.cn/en/guides/manyue-webdav-sync/>): Configure data sync separately and choose the first upload or download.

ManYue does not provide comics. Some remote-library and sync features require a membership; use the current in-app screen as the reference. Syncing reading records and transferring comic files are different tasks.
