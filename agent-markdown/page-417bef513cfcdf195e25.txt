## Start with a sole resting on the table

Use an approximately 1800×1400 px landscape canvas with pale warm-grey background. Point the nose upper right and rear lower left, viewed slightly above to expose shell and near sole edge. Sketch a rounded triangular plane, wider rear and tapered nose without a blade point. Separate sketch, sole, shell, handle and shadow. Keep the sole compatible with one tabletop, not a raised nose contradicting a flat rear. Flatten a boat-like belly or smooth an angular clipped-box transition. Pixel proportions are compositional, not model dimensions.

## Keep sole thickness on one plane

Add a narrow silver-grey near edge with gentle perspective variation, not erratic melted thickness. Most of the top is covered by shell; expose only useful rim rather than drawing a whole metal triangle through it. Hide shell to check a complete sole, then restore enclosure. Add restrained metallic value if rubber-like or thin the edge if brick-based. Omit underside steam holes invisible from this angle rather than rotating the bottom onto the side to identify the appliance. Retain one exterior viewpoint.

## Seat the upper shell over the sole

Use muted blue-grey shell rising toward the rear and lowering toward the nose, with a plausible seam above the sole. Its lower boundary follows the sole rather than floating as a separate oval. Establish volume with two broad values, omitting reflections, lettering and dials initially. Reduce excessive shell height or broaden the sole if cabin-like; expose a near turn if sticker-flat. The seam is a component boundary, not a continuous black rupture. Explain occlusion with contours rather than invented internal mechanisms.

## Inspect the grip opening as a background shape

Build a bridge-like handle on the shell’s rear half, connecting both roots and retaining a grip opening. Mark that opening with background colour first, then add handle thickness without filling it. Darken the inner wall while leaving background visible through the opening, not a solid black component. A button-like shape needs a genuine opening; a floating bridge needs connected roots. Foreshortening may narrow it but cannot combine frontal and side viewpoints. The illustrated gap does not establish real hand fit or safe grip.

## Differentiate shell and metal under one light

Light upper left, giving shell broad soft light, handle turns slightly clearer values and sole narrow grey-white reflections. Do not copy a sharp white outline onto both plastic and metal. Hide reflections to confirm flat sole, shell and opening; restore them and remove bright bands falsely closing the gap. Reduce white shell dots if mirror-toy-like or add local temperature and metal contrast if plaster-like. Retain a little reflected light inside the handle rather than a black hole. Material rendering needs no invented power indicator or implied operating state.

## Use ground shadow to confirm a flat support

Use tight narrow sole contact and softer lower-right cast shadow, not a nose piercing the table. Hide shadow and check the sole itself, then restore contact at the rear as well. Reconnect edge and tight shadow if floating or narrow a broad black band if sinking. Omit a cord here; an optional cord must remain explicitly unplugged with an accounted-for endpoint rather than an unknown off-frame connection. Keep the background simple without sockets, steam or a glowing sole. Still-life space is sufficient.

## Audit solid silhouette and negative space

Save a monochrome structural version, inspecting sole outline and grip opening separately as solid boundary and background passage. If a thumbnail becomes a solid pointed lump, repair opening size and shell layering rather than adding an explanatory word. Retain handle-root guides and verify both connections. Change one item at a time, not sole length, handle height and light simultaneously. A heel-standing variant needs new contact and underside visibility, not a rotated lying image retaining old shadow. Keep viewpoint coherent too.

## Check that darks do not swallow the opening

Save the layered original and inspect grip opening, rounded nose and near sole edge in a PNG at use size. Adjust inner-wall value and retain background if the gap becomes black rather than whitening everything. Repair a boat-like sole contour instead of deepening shadow.
