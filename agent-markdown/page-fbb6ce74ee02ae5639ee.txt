## Establish the domed carapace volume

Use an approximately 1700×1400 px canvas, head toward near left and body central. Establish shell footprint, dome and ground contact, with near rim lower than far rim in the oblique view. Reserve openings and soften mechanical symmetry if bowl-like; clarify front/back and rim if rock-like. A high dome references the species without measuring size or mass. Omit water/swimming; grounding belongs to an invented resting composition.

## Place the plastron beneath, not as another dome

## Expose head and neck through the front opening

## Keep tail and rear opening subordinate

Keep rear shade from becoming a giant empty hole, and never lift the carapace to expose parts.
