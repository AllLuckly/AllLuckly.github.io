Track entry, placement and occlusion inside door or window frames without confusing them with player cropping.

English translation of the [Chinese original](<https://allluckly.cn/guides/icouldplay-frame-within-frame-doorway-image-boundary/>), translated on 2026-11-14. Original publication dates, attribution, code and version notes are retained.

A doorway, window or railing can form a second visual boundary around a character without changing the file’s aspect ratio. Watch a short framed scene in iCouldPlay and manually distinguish the outer image, internal physical frame and character placement. Record how framing limits visible content in external notes; no new automatic composition analysis is needed.

## Choose a scene with traceable internal edges

Prepare an authorised scene of about 15–30 seconds with a clear doorway/window and slight character movement. Record filename, duration, source and entry; play to confirm the object is inside the image, not a decorative outer border. Alternatively make a person/toy doorway clip externally before importing; a plan is not playback evidence. Use an available whole-image display before zooming. One main internal frame is enough; many windows impede continuous edge tracking.

## Record the outer image before internal edges

Pause where edges are clear. In external notes record the visible video boundary, then which internal top, sides and bottom appear. An internal frame need not show all four edges; record even three visible edges as they are, and do not invent an unseen sill. Separate controls, device borders and scene walls rather than merging black areas. Restore whole-image display if zoom obscures boundaries. Describe any encoded black bars without inferring the original camera format. Identify edge location and object before interpretation.

## Describe placement relative to the internal frame

On a second pass, anchor entry, central/side placement and departure where present, recording time and action. Describe head below lintel or body near right jamb, not screen pixels as real dimensions. State “no centred phase” if absent. Leaving the doorway interior differs from leaving the video image. If camera movement shifts the doorway, track that same physical frame without automatically assigning motion to the character. No player tracking boxes or coordinate export are required.

## Separate physical occlusion from visual enclosure

At a near-jamb moment, note whether it hides hand, shoulder or leg and what is visible before and after. Visual enclosure need not physically occlude the person. If only the upper body appears, distinguish outer-image cropping, scene obstruction and original framing; keep uncertainty when unresolved. Do not invent a full pose in darkness. Replay across the interval rather than treating one paused image as proof of movement through the whole doorway.

## Compare a neighbouring shot without enclosure

Find a neighbouring unframed shot and separately note subject size, surrounding space and occlusion. Record simultaneous changes in distance, angle and action rather than attributing everything to enclosure. A single-shot study is valid if no suitable comparison exists. A separate externally filmed no-doorframe version may hold position and direction roughly constant, with residual differences logged. External capture/editing prepares media; iCouldPlay does not automatically remove a doorway or create an alternative view.

## Separate visible restriction from emotional reading

Separate facts such as a jamb hiding a shoulder or a small figure inside a window from an interpretation such as possible distance. Framing alone proves neither imprisonment nor a universal emotional response. Add narrative evidence explicitly where relevant. Preserve a free exit as counterevidence rather than deleting it. Another reader could interpret the fact card differently, within existing media permissions; sending messages is unnecessary, and rereading alone can reveal unsupported inference.

## Troubleshoot false internal frames caused by cropping

If a doorway suddenly coincides with the video boundary, inspect zoom/display changes and restore full range using present controls. Hide controls where possible instead of reading a toolbar as a sill. Verify file and edition when a new copy lacks side walls. Replay from an earlier action anchor after imprecise seeking, retaining approximate seconds without frame claims. Separate display issues, encoded image limits and physical scene occlusion; only the first calls for player-setting investigation.

## Recover three boundary layers after reopening

Reopen the same clip, verify name and duration, and recover anchors by time and action rather than resume alone. Recheck outer image, internal physical edges and occluded character area, noting full-image display. Log changed conditions before revising old notes, without inventing unseen edges for consistency. Deliver a visible fact, qualified interpretation and unresolved point. Without actual playback, retain a plan. Success is retrievable boundary separation, not automatic composition detection or one proven narrative meaning.
